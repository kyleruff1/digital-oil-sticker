## Outcome

Milestone M00 ("Decisions and proof") converts the Digital Oil Sticker product idea into tested constraints and written decisions, then proves the binding runtime architecture: a Mob `0.7.20` application generated with `mix mob.new digital_oil_sticker --liveview`, with BEAM/Phoenix running entirely on the device, a native WebView loading LiveView over IPv4 loopback, two on-device Ecto/SQLite repositories (a replaceable `CatalogRepo` and a durable `UserRepo`), and `mob_notify` local notifications. Netlify is a separate static publishing surface and is never the application runtime.

When this epic closes, the product has a ratified constitution, a proven runtime architecture, a proven persistence strategy, a proven local-notification capability matrix, and a ratified vehicle/maintenance data boundary — all evidenced on physical hardware, with every consciously accepted limitation owned and reviewable.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

Mob is a pre-1.0 BEAM-on-device framework, and the loopback LiveView runtime is unusual enough that it must be proven on physical devices before any feature investment. Freezing decisions first prevents the recurring architecture mistakes this roadmap explicitly guards against: treating Netlify as an application host, treating vPIC as a maintenance-data source, mixing catalog and user data in one database, or assuming push infrastructure. Every later milestone (M01 engineering foundation, M02 UX and content contract, M03 data acquisition, and the M04–M08 chain behind them) is blocked on this gate. No feature milestone may start before this gate.

## Included

Child issues tracked by this epic (all must be accepted for the exit gate):

- DOS-M00-001 — Ratify product invariants, terminology, and measurable non-functional targets: the short product constitution that becomes the precedence document for all later tickets.
- DOS-M00-002 — Ratify and prove the on-device Mob/LiveView architecture and Netlify boundary: the binding-architecture ADR plus a reproducible physical-device proof, including both documented Mob bridges and the DeviceCommandBroker/root-screen path.
- DOS-M00-003 — Prove Mob 0.7.20 on physical iOS and Android devices: a version-pinned compatibility report converting Mob's pre-1.0 status into explicit upgrade and containment rules.
- DOS-M00-004 — Prove two on-device Ecto/SQLite repositories and their migration strategy: a persistence ADR and physical-device benchmark fixing paths, startup order, migrations, corruption recovery, and catalog replacement behavior.
- DOS-M00-005 — Prove `mob_notify` local reminders and define truthful delivery fallbacks: a capability matrix and physical-device proof fixing product copy and fallback behavior for every unsupported lifecycle case.
- DOS-M00-006 — Prove vehicle and maintenance-data boundaries with a representative source slice: a data-coverage decision and small end-to-end proof separating vPIC identity data from licensed maintenance/oil/filter sources.

Epic-level tracking also includes: maintaining the milestone limitation register (owner, user-facing fallback, review date for each consciously accepted limitation) and confirming the gate verdict that releases M01, M02, and M03.

## Functional requirements

Entry and exit gate conditions for the milestone:

- FR-1 (entry): M00 may open immediately once the roadmap bootstrap lands; it has no upstream milestone blockers.
- FR-2 (exit): DOS-M00-001 through DOS-M00-006 are accepted, each with its acceptance criteria met and evidence attached. Prose such as "investigated" or "works locally" is not sufficient for any child.
- FR-3 (exit): a physical iPhone and a physical Android device have been used for the Mob, persistence, and notification proofs; simulator-only evidence does not satisfy the gate.
- FR-4 (exit): the selected architecture has no unresolved "to be determined" dependency that would invalidate the storage, hosting, or offline model.
- FR-5 (exit): every consciously accepted limitation has an owner, a user-facing fallback, and a review date recorded in the milestone limitation register.
- FR-6 (stop gate): if any binding invariant fails in a child proof, M01 remains blocked pending an explicitly approved replacement-architecture ADR; no silent substitution is permitted.
- FR-7 (release): on gate pass, M01, M02, and permitted free-source investigation in M03 may open once their exact M00 blockers close; downstream issues are not marked Ready merely because work could be prototyped.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- Risk: Mob `0.7.20` is pre-1.0 and may change APIs incompatibly or fail a binding invariant on physical hardware. Mitigation: the go/no-go spike structure, the stop-gate rule, and the isolation/upgrade policy from DOS-M00-003.
- Risk: physical-device access (hardware, signing prerequisites) delays proofs. Mitigation: capture prerequisites early in DOS-M00-003; no dates are promised by this plan.
- Risk: data-rights findings in DOS-M00-006 narrow the honest MVP coverage claim. Mitigation: the coverage contract separates identity support from recommendation support statuses.
- Decision owned here: the final gate verdict, including whether any consciously accepted limitation is compatible with proceeding.
- Open question: none may remain at close — that is the point of the gate (FR-4).
