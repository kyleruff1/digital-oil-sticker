> **RESCOPED FOR BROWSER MVP — 2026-08-01.** [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) established the browser-first MVP. This card is retained with the same identity; its Outcome and functional requirements below are the rescoped spec. The pre-pivot spec is preserved at the bottom under "Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)". Disposition per [PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md](../../docs/governance/PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md#issue-by-issue-classification): _Freeze a responsive **web** interaction model (URLs, back button, refresh, tab focus) instead of a native mobile one; the language/content contract carries over intact._

## Outcome

Produce implementation-ready flows, component and state specifications, content rules, accessibility expectations, and a testable clickable prototype for a **hosted Phoenix LiveView browser application** at `digitaloilsticker.com`, covering local onboarding, one active MVP vehicle, oil-change records, forecasts, and in-app due/overdue reminder state. Personal data lives in anonymous browser storage (IndexedDB, per ADR-0004 §"The client-storage + LiveView hydration protocol"); the catalog is queried from the server. Preserve a clearly separated post-MVP design direction for multi-vehicle navigation without adding it to MVP implementation acceptance.

Designs must show the full state surface a browser LiveView creates that a packaged pre-pivot client did not: **pre-hydration, disconnected/reconnecting, hydration timeout, storage-denied, storage-blocked/private-browsing, quota-exhausted, evicted (data-missing), catalog-request-failure, missing-data, and destructive** — not only the happy path. The pre-pivot platform-runtime failure state is retired (finding 1); its browser replacements are enumerated above and made mandatory by FR-6, FR-7, and FR-8.

The **web interaction model** and language contract are frozen before feature UI is built: every route (real, shareable URL), browser-history/back-button rule, refresh-behavior, tab-focus behavior, multi-tab-consistency rule, screen, state, and sentence that M05/M06 implement is specified here so no implementation ticket invents product behavior, terminology, navigation, or hydration semantics.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

Feature implementation cannot begin honest UI work while the browser interaction model, hydration protocol, storage-loss states, LiveView disconnect/reconnect behavior, WCAG-for-browsers contract, and content rules are undecided. Under ADR-0004 the seam between LiveView's server-held socket assigns and the user's IndexedDB record is the single highest-risk new surface in the product; without a frozen UX for it, tickets would either flash a false empty garage on every reconnect (an INV-5-adjacent failure even though nothing was lost) or invent conflicting recovery flows for storage eviction.

This epic unblocks the M02 → M05 edge of the cross-milestone DAG: DOS-M05-000 ("Single-vehicle offline MVP" — rescoped under the pivot to "hosted, anonymous browser storage, connectivity required for catalog") is blocked on both M02 and M04. It gives DOS-M06-000 its forecast/in-app-due-state UX contract (the OS-notification half of that milestone is deferred per ADR-0004 and the pivot doc), and it gives QA the content/state matrix used in DOS-M07-000 hardening.

It also depends on the **proposed M09** milestone (Hosted browser platform and anonymous client storage) named in [PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md §"Proposed new milestone"](../../docs/governance/PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md#proposed-new-milestone) — specifically M09-003 (hydration protocol), M09-004 (pre-hydration/storage-denied/quota/eviction UX and copy), M09-005 (server-side catalog query layer), and M09-009 (browser support matrix). Because M09 is proposed rather than ratified, this epic's Preconditions treat the *ratification* of M09 as the gate (not the closure of individual M09 children), and children in this milestone may progress against ADR-0004 in the interim.

## Included

Child issues tracked by this epic (each retains its DOS-M02-00N identity and inherits its rescope disposition from the pivot doc):

- DOS-M02-001 — Information architecture, routes, and end-to-end task flows: **real shareable URLs**, browser-history and back-button semantics, refresh-behavior, deep-link entry, tab-focus behavior, and per-flow **pre-hydration entry state**.
- DOS-M02-002 — Design system and accessibility contract: tokens, reusable LiveView components, interaction states, WCAG 2.2 AA verified against browser assistive technology (VoiceOver-on-Safari, TalkBack-on-Chrome, NVDA/JAWS-on-Windows, keyboard-only desktop). - DOS-M02-003 — First-run, local-profile-in-browser, privacy, and data-readiness UX: hydration-from-empty on first visit (never a false empty garage), catalog-query readiness/failure, plain-language privacy that says "this browser on this device" and actively contradicts the hosted-URL implication of server storage, plus explicit handling of private-browsing and storage-blocked cases.
- DOS-M02-004 — Vehicle search, filtering, selection, and custom-vehicle fallback served by a **server-side catalog query layer**: progressive filtering, match quality, custom vehicles, identity edits, plus network-latency, request-failure, and no-connection states that a local-database design never needed.
- DOS-M02-005 — One-vehicle MVP and post-MVP garage direction as an **accessible web navigation pattern**: blocking one-active-vehicle experience plus a non-blocking multi-vehicle appendix respecified with real URLs per vehicle (post-MVP).
- DOS-M02-006 — Oil-change logging, history, and recommendation/product selection: event fields, validation, history, and source-separated recommendation display. Every create/edit commits through the `pushEvent` → hook → IndexedDB round trip and is not shown as saved until the hook acknowledges (INV-24.2).
- DOS-M02-007 — Driving-usage sampling and explainable due-date/mileage forecasts. Because there is no background scheduler in a browser, "recurring odometer prompt" is respecified as an **on-visit prompt driven by elapsed time since the last sample**, with confidence states and explanations unchanged.
- DOS-M02-008 — In-app reminder UX only: delete the OS permission-prompt and OS notification-delivery flows; MVP delivers due/overdue state in the dashboard and on the vehicle card, and copy actively states that nothing arrives while the app is closed (INV-17).

Epic-level coordination: keeping route terminology, component names, content rules, hydration-state vocabulary, storage-loss vocabulary, and disconnect/reconnect vocabulary consistent across all eight specifications; assembling the milestone-level browser-based clickable prototype and content/state matrix from child deliverables.

## Excluded

- Any application code, LiveView implementation, or deployment work — this milestone produces specifications and prototypes only. The **hosted Phoenix / Fly.io / IndexedDB / hydration-protocol architecture is not excluded** — it is the target this milestone is specifying UI for (finding 2). What was previously excluded as "remote Phoenix host / PWA/browser production surface" is retired.
- Implementing multi-vehicle tabs/garage in MVP; the post-MVP direction is documented but never added to MVP implementation acceptance.
- Any UX that assumes **online accounts**, **runtime third-party API lookup**, **guaranteed notification delivery** (OS notifications, Web Push, email, SMS — all deferred or out of scope), or **unverified compatibility data** (INV-15/INV-16). These exclusions carry over from the pre-pivot spec and remain binding under ADR-0004.
- Any UX that implies the record is backed up on a server, synced across devices, or recoverable by the operator (INV-3, INV-4, INV-5, INV-25). Prohibited words per ADR-0004 §"Export and import": "backup", "synced", "safe", "we'll remember", "your account".
- A **PWA / service-worker offline mode**. ADR-0004 explicitly defers it as a separate decision; the MVP does not work offline and no copy may soften this. (The narrower prohibition — "PWA/service-worker offline surface" — replaces the pre-pivot blanket ban on any "PWA/browser production surface", per finding 2.)
- **OS local notifications and Web Push.** `mob_notify` does not exist in a browser; Web Push requires a service worker, VAPID keys, and a third-party push service holding per-device subscriptions, which conflicts with INV-3/INV-4 and is deferred to the proposed M09-010 spike.
- Netlify marketing/support-site design and store screenshots; and all child-issue Excluded items (each child's exclusions apply unchanged in their rescoped form).
- Data acquisition, licensing, or catalog-content work (M03) and persistence implementation (M04).

## Functional requirements

- FR-1 (entry gate): Work on this milestone begins only after the rescoped DOS-M00-000 gate closes on ADR-0004's architecture.
- FR-2 (entry gate): Screen-level data requirements in child specs cite approved M01 contracts in their rescoped form (DOS-M01-002 browser-storage port, DOS-M01-003 IndexedDB object-store layout for the user store, DOS-M01-003 server-side `CatalogRepo` schema) rather than invented fields.
- FR-3 (standing constraint — rescoped, addresses finding 2): No UX produced in this milestone assumes an online account, runtime third-party API lookup, guaranteed notification delivery, or unverified compatibility data. The pre-pivot clauses forbidding "remote Phoenix host" and "PWA/browser production surface" are **retired**: the MVP *is* a hosted Phoenix LiveView on Fly.io (ADR-0004). The narrower prohibition on a **PWA / service-worker offline mode** and on **Web Push** remains, and each requires its own future decision.
- FR-4 (web interaction model — new, addresses the pivot line): Each child spec defines the browser-first interaction rules for its surfaces: real shareable URLs (route + parameters), browser back-button/forward semantics, refresh/reload behavior (must re-hydrate rather than lose state), tab focus/blur behavior, deep-link entry (into a route with no prior in-app history), and multi-tab consistency (per INV-24.7 and ADR-0004 §"Multi-tab consistency"). The pre-pivot platform-package prototypes framing is retired.
- FR-5 (pre-hydration and hydration UX — new, addresses finding 1): Every screen defines its **pre-hydration** entry state (a determinate skeleton, never a false empty garage) per ADR-0004 §"First-paint and empty-state rules" and INV-24.3. The three states `:hydrating`, `:empty`, and `:storage_unavailable` render differently and are labeled consistently across all eight children. On hydration returning zero records after `dos_boot_state == "has_data"`, the spec defines the explicit `:data_missing` warning state (never a silent empty state).
- FR-6 (storage-honesty states — new, replaces the pre-pivot "denied-permission" line and addresses finding 1): Every screen that reads or writes personal data specifies its behavior for **storage-denied**, **quota-exhausted**, **evicted / data-missing**, **private-browsing**, and **user-cleared-storage** states, with copy that satisfies INV-25 (honest storage-loss messaging) and the ADR-0004 prohibited-words list. Silent degradation to in-memory state that looks durable is a spec defect.
- FR-7 (connectivity and disconnect states — new, replaces the pre-pivot platform-runtime failure requirement per finding 1): Every screen defines its behavior for **LiveView disconnected / reconnecting** (using `phx-disconnected` styling that communicates reconnection honestly, per ADR-0004 §"WebSocket and LiveView considerations"), **hydration timeout** (per ADR-0004 §"Mount and hydration sequence" step 6, default 5,000 ms), and **catalog-request-failure** for the server-side catalog query surface (M09-005). Airplane-mode / no-connection is a first-class state: the app does not work offline at all (ADR-0004 §"Lost — honestly"), and every spec says so plainly.
- FR-8 (mutation round-trip states — new): Every write specifies its **saving / saved / not-saved-to-this-browser** rendering per ADR-0004 §"Mutation round trip". A mutation is never presented as committed until the hook acknowledges the client write (INV-24.2). The 2,000 ms unacknowledged-write state and its persistent, non-dismissible copy are specified per screen.
- FR-9 (accessibility for browser AT — restated): DOS-M02-002 owns WCAG 2.2 AA verified against a named browser and assistive-technology matrix — VoiceOver-on-Safari (macOS and iOS), TalkBack-on-Chrome (Android), NVDA and JAWS on Windows, and keyboard-only desktop use. Touch-target minimums and text-scaling (200% zoom without loss) requirements survive; the OS-accessibility-API surface is retired.
- FR-10 (browser and engine matrix — new): Every child covers, at minimum, the CONSTITUTION §9 browser support matrix — current and prior stable release of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari (desktop) plus iOS Safari 16+ and Android Chrome — at compact and large text sizes. IndexedDB availability is treated as runtime-probed (INV-24.6), not assumed.
- FR-11 (exit gate): DOS-M02-001 through DOS-M02-008 are accepted in their rescoped form.
- FR-12 (exit gate): A **browser-based clickable prototype** (opened at a URL, exercised in a real browser at both text sizes) and a content/state matrix cover every core path.
- FR-13 (exit gate): Engineering and QA can implement and test each state without inventing product behavior — every state has specified data requirements, hydration/mutation semantics, copy rules, and expected transitions.
- FR-14 (exit gate): Designs demonstrably show the state surface enumerated in FR-5, FR-6, FR-7, and FR-8, plus missing-data and destructive states — not only the happy path.
- FR-15 (exit gate): The post-MVP multi-vehicle direction (garage/tabs/search) is preserved as a clearly separated appendix and does not appear in any MVP implementation acceptance criterion; it is respecified using accessible web navigation with real URLs per vehicle (per DOS-M02-005 rescope).
- FR-16 (exit gate): No accepted design uses a prohibited word from ADR-0004 §"Export and import" ("backup", "synced", "safe", "we'll remember", "your account") in a way that implies durability, syncing, or server-side storage.

### Data and persistence

N/A at the epic level. Child specifications cite:

- The server-side, read-only `CatalogRepo` (Ecto/SQLite baked into the Fly image per ADR-0004 §"Catalog database on Fly") for all catalog reads.

- The versioned envelope shape per ADR-0004 §"Payload envelope" for hydrate, write-back, and export.

The epic verifies no child invents storage fields or persists any personal data server-side (INV-3, INV-23).

### Interfaces and events

N/A at the epic level. Children reference:

- The domain events used by the forecast/reminder flows (in-app due-state transitions computed on hydration and after each mutation; `mob_notify` is retired for the MVP).
- The `BroadcastChannel("dos_local_store")` per ADR-0004 §"Multi-tab consistency" for multi-tab notification.

### Failure and recovery behavior

N/A at the epic level, except the standing requirement that every child specifies the states enumerated in FR-5, FR-6, FR-7, and FR-8: pre-hydration, disconnected/reconnecting, hydration timeout, storage-denied, storage-blocked/private-browsing, quota-exhausted, evicted/data-missing, catalog-request-failure, missing-data, and destructive.

### Security and privacy

N/A at the epic level. Child specs must respect the hosted security boundary (INV-22, ADR-0004 §"Security and privacy boundary for a hosted application"): HTTPS/WSS only, CSRF/origin checks on the socket, the strict CSP, and the prohibition on any third-party origin. Copy and states must reflect that the session cookie contains no personal data and no stable user identifier (per ADR-0004 §"CSRF and session"), and that operator console access is for application diagnosis not for user observation. DOS-M02-003 owns the privacy UX contract in the rescoped form.

### Accessibility and performance

N/A at the epic level. DOS-M02-002 owns the WCAG 2.2 AA baseline (browser AT — VoiceOver/TalkBack/NVDA/JAWS/keyboard-only), touch-target minimums, and text-scaling requirements. The performance envelope children must design against comes from CONSTITUTION §9 as re-ratified in revision 2.0.0: **provisional** page-load TTI p75 ≤ 3.5 s, first-visit payload ≤ 250 KiB compressed HTML+CSS+JS with ≤ 500 KiB total transfer and zero third-party origins, and hydration sub-second on a cold connection (R2 in ADR-0004). Local-loopback p95 ≤ 100 ms interaction latency is retired; per-geography p95 measured targets replace it as they are re-ratified.

## UX and content states

The epic's exit-level state inventory aggregates the child matrices and covers, at minimum, at compact and large text sizes on the FR-10 browser matrix:

- **Hydration states:** pre-hydration skeleton (`:hydrating`), hydrated-empty (`:empty`, first-ever visit), hydrated-loaded (`:loaded`), storage-unavailable (`:storage_unavailable`), and data-missing (`:data_missing`) after `dos_boot_state == "has_data"` returns zero records.
- **Connectivity states:** LiveView connected, LiveView disconnected / reconnecting (`phx-disconnected` styling), hydration timeout at 5,000 ms.
- **Mutation states:** saving (optimistic with "saving" affordance), saved, and not-saved-to-this-browser at the 2,000 ms unacknowledged-write threshold.
- **Storage-loss states:** private-browsing / blocked storage, quota-exceeded on write, quota-approaching (>80% per ADR-0004 §"Quota and eviction"), user-cleared-storage, browser-evicted, and multi-tab-conflict (last-write-wins with the loss made visible per ADR-0004 §"Multi-tab consistency").
- **Catalog-query states:** loading, no-connection, request-failure, unsupported (`data_version` moved, per ADR-0004 §"Validation"), and known-unknown per ADR-0003 confidence tiers.
- **Forecast states:** insufficient-data, confident, low-confidence, OLM/manual warnings.
- **Reminder states:** in-app due, in-app overdue, dismissed / snoozed within the current session (in-app only; no scheduled delivery).
- **Destructive states:** vehicle deletion, service-event deletion, storage erasure, import-with-replace, with confirmations that satisfy INV-5/INV-25.
- **Onboarding:** first-run without confusing a returning-user reconnect (FR-5).
- **Cross-cutting:** copy compliance with the ADR-0004 prohibited-words list.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- **Risk (elevated by the pivot):** the pre-pivot spec's "offline first launch works with bundled catalog" mental model leaks into new work. Mitigation: FR-3, FR-7, and AC-4 make the honest ADR-0004 §"Lost — honestly" losses explicit, and the historical section at the bottom is preserved so the change is auditable rather than silently rewritten.
- **Risk (highest new risk):** browser eviction of infrequently-used site storage (ADR-0004 R1). A user who checks their oil once a quarter is exactly the profile most at risk. Mitigation at this milestone: the `:data_missing` state (FR-5), the honest storage-loss copy (FR-6, INV-25), and export-nudge specs owned by DOS-M02-003 and DOS-M02-006. Actual per-browser eviction behavior must be **measured** by the M09 conformance suite (M09-009), not assumed from documentation.
- **Risk:** M01 or M09 contract churn invalidates already-specified screen data requirements. Mitigation: children cite contract versions; changed contracts reopen the affected spec through change control.
- **Risk:** post-MVP multi-vehicle design ambition leaks into MVP acceptance. Mitigation: FR-15 / AC-5 require labeled appendices and forbid MVP acceptance references.
- **Risk:** specs drift toward happy-path fidelity because failure states are harder to draw and the browser adds more of them (pre-hydration, disconnect, hydration timeout, five storage-loss variants). Mitigation: AC-4 enumerates the required failure states explicitly.
- **Risk:** copy that reads as "your data is on their server" because the app is now at a URL. Mitigation: FR-16 / AC-9 forbid the ADR-0004 prohibited words and require actively contradicting the hosted-URL implication in first-run and privacy copy.
- **Open question:** exact browser-based prototype tooling (which prototyping platform yields real URLs, back-button behavior, and text-scaling for evaluation) is a child-level decision; the epic only requires that the result be testable in a real browser at both text sizes on the FR-10 matrix.
- **Open question:** multi-tab conflict resolution is last-write-wins at record granularity per ADR-0004 §"Multi-tab consistency", with losses made visible. Whether any real workflow (two tabs, one logging a service while the other edits it) makes this materially wrong is recorded here rather than presented as settled.
- **Decision recorded:** no UX may promise behavior ADR-0004 explicitly deferred — service-worker offline, Web Push, OS notifications, multi-device sync. Disagreements route to a new ADR (potentially ADR-0005 for a service-worker/PWA decision), not design workarounds.

## Definition of done

All eight children are accepted and closed in their rescoped form with attached evidence; the milestone exit gate (browser-based clickable prototype plus content/state matrix covering every core path on the FR-10 browser matrix at compact and large text sizes; every hydration, storage-loss, disconnect/reconnect, and mutation-round-trip state specified; engineering and QA able to implement/test without inventing behavior) is verified in a recorded sign-off; the post-MVP direction is separated and respecified as an accessible web navigation pattern; no accepted design violates the standing constraint in FR-3 or the copy rule in FR-16; and this epic links every deliverable so DOS-M05-000/DOS-M06-000 planning can proceed without re-litigating browser interaction, hydration, or storage-loss decisions.
