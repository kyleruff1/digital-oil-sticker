## Outcome
Produce a reproducible, legally distributable, versioned, read-only offline catalog artifact for a rolling 30-model-year scope. The artifact supports deterministic vehicle lookup and, only where licensed evidence exists, oil-service schedules, vehicle lubricant requirements, API-licensed oil products, and oil-filter fitment.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks
The product's non-negotiable rules require that the app be offline from first launch, that no automotive fact be fabricated, and that every displayed manufacturer interval, viscosity/specification, capacity, oil-product claim, and filter fitment have provenance and a license permitting offline redistribution. This epic is the only path by which such data legally enters the application. It unblocks M04 (local domain and persistence, which installs and reads the bundled catalog), M05 (single-vehicle MVP flows built on catalog lookup), and M07 (hardening and release, which requires data-quality sign-off). Without this epic there is no vehicle selector, no sourced schedule, and no defensible recommendation claim.

## Context and source of truth
- Master prompt section 4.4 "vPIC identifies vehicles; it does not recommend maintenance": the free-first, field-by-field acquisition strategy, the three-lane approval model (green lane government data under 17 U.S.C. §105, documented-facts lane, permission-needed lane), the free candidate matrix that M03 must investigate before opening a commercial procurement ticket, and the preferred procurement of a one-time bulk snapshot with perpetual rights.
- Master prompt section 5 "Frozen scope boundaries": U.S. market, rolling 30-model-year window (1997–2026 inclusive for the 2026 baseline), passenger cars, multipurpose passenger vehicles, and light pickups/vans that use engine oil; support statuses `identity_only`, `schedule_supported`, `full_product_supported`, `not_applicable`, `unsupported`.
- Master prompt section 8 "Database and data-contract baseline": the `CatalogRepo` table baseline (`catalog_metadata`, `data_sources`, `makes`, `models`, `vehicle_configurations`, `maintenance_schedules`, `oil_requirements`, `oil_brands`/`oil_products`/`oil_product_claims`, `filter_brands`/`filter_products`/`filter_fitments`, `aliases`, optional FTS tables) and the builder outputs (rejected-row reports, coverage counts, collisions, source diffs, manifest, deterministic SQLite artifact).
- Section 11.2 "Research-backed source policy (binding on both milestones)": findings verified against official publisher pages on 2026-07-31, including vPIC scope and limits, FuelEconomy.gov and EPA certification data roles, API EOLCS access constraints, MOTOR and Auto Care commercial terms, and the rule that no catalog fact may be described as "manufacturer recommended" without OEM-resolving field-level provenance.
- Section 11.2 "Mandatory fallback if commercial licenses are not secured" and "Acquisition strategy: free first, one-time licensed snapshot for proven gaps."
- Section 12 "Cross-milestone execution order" and the "Required dependency order for GitHub Projects" list for M03/M04 sequencing.

## Included
Child issues of this epic (create each as a sub-issue; IDs are immutable):
- DOS-M03-001 — Approve the data-source and redistribution rights matrix: the signed product/legal gate for every non-vPIC production ingest, including the free-source proof of concept and gap-driven procurement.
- DOS-M03-002 — Freeze the catalog vocabulary, identity rules, and field-level provenance contract: the versioned data dictionary, stable-ID rules, and provenance/crosswalk contract targeted by all adapters.
- DOS-M03-003 — Build an immutable raw-data landing zone and reproducible ingest ledger: content-addressed raw storage, ingest receipts, archive-integrity defenses, and retention/tombstone behavior.
- DOS-M03-004 — Implement a courteous, contract-tested vPIC acquisition client: a single rate-disciplined, cached, retried, contract-validated boundary for approved vPIC endpoints.
- DOS-M03-005 — Acquire the rolling 30-year vPIC vehicle identity corpus: the resumable major-make, light-duty year/make/model/type enumeration with per-cell coverage statuses, plus the free-first FuelEconomy.gov enrichment evaluation.
- DOS-M03-006 — Normalize vehicle configurations and create auditable cross-source identities: NFKC normalization, the configuration hierarchy, `vehicle_external_ids`, and review-gated crosswalks with no unsafe fuzzy joins.
- DOS-M03-007 — Gate and ingest rights-approved OEM-derived oil-service schedules and lubricant requirements: normal/severe, initial/repeating, time/mileage/condition schedules and lubricant facts with OEM provenance, or fixture-only when no license is approved.
- DOS-M03-008 — Gate and ingest the API EOLCS licensed-product directory: time-bounded licensee/brand/product/claim rows that never imply vehicle fitment, gated on written API rights.
- DOS-M03-009 — Gate and ingest rights-approved filter products, applications, and reference data: free-first supplier fitment inventory, then ACES/PIES/VCdb only for documented gaps, with every qualifier retained.
- DOS-M03-010 — Compile, quality-gate, and publish a deterministic offline catalog release: the content-addressed SQLite artifact, `catalog-manifest.json`, QA gates with blocker/waiver severity, and bundle staging for M04.

## Excluded
- Live vPIC calls from end-user devices.
- VIN history.
- Recall/safety data.
- Repair instructions.
- Automatic inference of oil/filter compatibility.
- A server-side user database.
Also excluded at the epic level: signed online catalog delivery (deferred to DOS-M08), any runtime network dependency for catalog availability, and beginning gated implementation by substituting scraped or guessed data.

## Test plan
### Automated
Aggregate of child CI gates: source-policy lint failures on unauthorized/expired sources, ingest-ledger integrity fixtures, vPIC client contract tests, coverage-cell verification, crosswalk determinism tests, adapter fixture suites, and the DOS-M03-010 reproducibility and QA-gate jobs. The epic closes only when these gates are green on the release commit.
### Manual/device matrix
The DOS-M03-010 packaged-artifact smoke run on one clean physical iOS device and one clean physical Android device (install, copy/verify/query bundled catalog, offline app kill/reboot survival, simulated app-upgrade rollback) executed through M04 conformance tests.

## Rollout and rollback
The epic's deliverable is a versioned, immutable, content-addressed catalog release staged into the application bundle. Promotion is CI-gated by DOS-M03-010 blockers; rollback is retention of the previously installed compatible catalog during an app upgrade (subject to license terms). No released catalog is mutated in place. Online update channels are out of scope until M08.

## Dependency rationale
In the M00→M08 DAG, M00 → M03 (architecture and coverage decisions gate data acquisition), M03 → M04 (the local persistence milestone installs and reads the catalog artifact), and M03 → M07 (release hardening requires the data-quality and rights evidence produced here). M01, M02, and permitted free-source investigation in M03 may run in parallel after their exact M00 blockers close. Within the epic, DOS-M03-001 and DOS-M03-002 are the P0 gates that everything else consumes; the assembler encodes the child-level dependencies listed in each child issue.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions
- Risk: commercial licenses (MOTOR, Auto Care, suppliers, API EOLCS) may not be secured on acceptable one-time-snapshot terms; mitigation is the mandatory free-approved baseline plus user-entered fallback, which keeps the app shippable.
- Risk: free-source coverage may be thinner than expected for schedules/fluids/fitment; the quantified gap analysis in DOS-M03-001 bounds procurement to real gaps.
- Risk: NHTSA applies unpublished automated traffic control; DOS-M03-004's conservative courtesy limits mitigate.
- Decision recorded: the vPIC standalone downloadable database is VIN-decode-only and is not a candidate for make/model enumeration.
- Open question: whether measured device budgets force catalog sharding (resolved inside DOS-M03-010 by measurement, not assumption).
