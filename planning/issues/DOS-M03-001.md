> **RESCOPED FOR BROWSER MVP — 2026-08-01.** [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) established the browser-first MVP. This card is retained with the same identity; its Outcome and functional requirements below are the rescoped spec. The pre-pivot spec is preserved at the bottom under "Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)". Disposition per [PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md](../../docs/governance/PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md#issue-by-issue-classification): _The rights analysis must now evaluate serving data over a public web service, which is a materially different redistribution mode from shipping rows inside a signed binary and may change the answer for individual sources._

## Outcome
A signed product/legal decision record establishes, per source and per field, which candidate sources may be acquired, transformed, stored, **served from a public Phoenix LiveView endpoint on Fly.io behind `digitaloilsticker.com`**, refreshed, attributed, rate-limited, and retained after contract termination. Every previously "approved for offline redistribution" answer is re-examined under the hosted redistribution mode, and the matrix's disposition column now records `web_serving` rights explicitly rather than inferring them from a prior offline approval.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks
Under [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) the catalog is not shipped inside a signed device binary — it is served by a hosted Phoenix LiveView application, baked into the Fly deploy image as a read-only SQLite database and queried on every user interaction. That is a materially different redistribution mode: the audience is anyone on the public internet, the surface is scrapable, packaging is not a boundary, and every catalog row travels over the wire in response to a URL. An offline-redistribution approval does **not** transfer to this mode. This card is the hard gate that decides, per source and per field, whether hosted serving is permitted at all and under what constraints (attribution placement, rate limits, response caps, non-bulk-API rules, retention after termination, and continued service of already-published catalog releases).

It unblocks the raw landing zone (DOS-M03-003) and the gated adapters for schedules/fluids (DOS-M03-007), EOLCS products (DOS-M03-008), and filter fitment (DOS-M03-009); it gates production completion of the catalog release (DOS-M03-010); it feeds the read-only server-side catalog query layer (DOS-M09-005), the internet-facing hardening work (DOS-M09-008), and the Fly deploy provenance (DOS-M09-006); and it feeds the browser attribution surfaces that the re-scoped M02 UX cards now own. It enforces INV-15 restated ("no fabricated automotive facts; provenance required, including redistribution rights for **serving over the network**, not only offline redistribution") and it is the source of truth for INV-22 (hosted transport security) as it applies to catalog content.

## Included
- Rewrite `docs/data/source-rights-matrix.md` around the hosted redistribution mode. Every existing row is re-evaluated; every new row is evaluated first under this mode. Sources are triaged into three redistribution-mode groups: (a) U.S. Government works under [17 U.S.C. §105](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html) (vPIC, FuelEconomy.gov, EPA certification data), whose prior approval carries over unchanged; (b) documented-facts and permission-needed lanes (OEM, MOTOR, API EOLCS, Auto Care, filter suppliers), which are **gated** — no production serving from the hosted app — until web-serving rights are affirmatively re-evidenced; (c) sources with no acceptable web-serving terms, which are prohibited from the hosted catalog and drop into the documented user-entered fallback.
- Keep one row per candidate — not only chosen vendors — including the NHTSA vPIC API (with its VIN-decode-only standalone database recorded as unsuitable for make/model enumeration), FuelEconomy.gov web services/downloads, other relevant government/open datasets, each OEM-published candidate, MOTOR Maintenance Schedules, MOTOR Fluids, API EOLCS, Auto Care VCdb/Qdb/PCdb/Brand Table/PAdb, and each filter supplier/aggregator.
- Inventory candidates separately for the nine fact domains (vehicle identity/configuration; normal/severe oil interval; time/mileage/condition-based schedule; OEM lubricant specification; viscosity; capacity/context; EOLCS license/product claim; filter brand/product; vehicle-filter fitment). A source may be approved for one field and prohibited for another, and approval is now per-field **and** per-redistribution-mode.
- Record per row (extended from the pre-pivot set): owner/contact, official URL, exact terms/license name and version or `not_found`, terms evidence capture date, access mechanism, cost model (`free`, `one_time_snapshot`, `subscription`, `usage_based`, `unknown`), quoted/contracted cost, credentials owner, geographic scope, covered model years/configurations, update cadence, measured completeness, attribution text and placement rules, automated-access right, transformation/derived-work right, raw-data retention, build/backup retention, historical-snapshot right, **web-serving redistribution right (this is the new load-bearing field)**, **whether serving is limited to per-request lookups (no bulk export API)**, **required per-response caps or rate limits (with numeric ceilings quoted from the terms)**, **whether inclusion of the source in a scraper-visible response requires additional notice or licensing**, **continued serving of already-published catalog releases after the agreement ends**, sublicensing/end-user scope, screenshot/mark usage, deletion-on-termination duty, update options, and approval evidence link.
- Extend the status enum for hosted delivery: `web_approved`, `web_approved_with_conditions`, `web_evaluation_only`, `offline_only_prior_approval` (explicitly not authorized for hosted serving), `pending`, `rejected`. Only `web_approved` and `web_approved_with_conditions` may reach a production Fly deploy image. `offline_only_prior_approval` is a distinct state so a prior offline "yes" cannot be silently reused as a hosted "yes."
- Ask vendors specifically whether: (a) their content may be served from a public web application accessed at a stable custom domain, (b) whether the content may be baked into a versioned deploy artifact and served identically across all hosted machines, (c) whether serving requires an authenticated request path, per-response rate limits, or a "no bulk retrieval" restriction, (d) whether scrapability of the responses (there is no packaging boundary) changes the answer, (e) what happens to already-published catalog releases still being served after termination, and (f) whether user-visible attribution on the browser UI (not an app bundle credits screen) satisfies their attribution requirement.
- Retain the free-source proof of concept before issuing commercial requests; add a **hosted-serving PoC** that renders each approved source through the actual server-side catalog query layer (DOS-M09-005) and confirms the response shape does not exceed any per-response cap recorded in the matrix.
- For unresolved gaps, keep the RFI/RFP pack; add the web-serving questions above to the standard vendor questionnaire so ambiguity in a written reply is a documented "no," not a documented "maybe."
- Preserve contract redline questions and the final written answer for every required right — including the new web-serving fields. Marketing statements and sales emails are not sufficient approval.
- Write a Product fallback decision for every `pending`, `offline_only_prior_approval`, or `rejected` row. For schedules, fluids, EOLCS, and fitment the default fallback is user-entered data (labeled `User entered`, never `Manufacturer recommended` or `Compatible`).

## Excluded
- Negotiating or purchasing contracts in code.
- Treating public web visibility as redistribution permission — this excludes an even harder case now: treating an earlier offline-redistribution approval as tacit web-serving approval.
- Legal conclusions by engineers.
- Reasoning about a hypothetical bulk-download API off the hosted catalog: the runtime query layer (DOS-M09-005) is per-request only, and this card ratifies that constraint rather than opens it.
- Reasoning about PWA-side or client-cached catalog rows: the PWA / service-worker offline mode is an explicitly deferred separate decision (DOS-M09-010). If it revives, the matrix will require another redistribution-mode column at that time.

## Functional requirements
- FR-1: `docs/data/source-rights-matrix.md` exists with one row per candidate source (chosen or not), covering at minimum the NHTSA vPIC API (standalone database recorded as VIN-decode-only and unsuitable for make/model enumeration), FuelEconomy.gov web services/downloads, other relevant government/open datasets, each OEM-published candidate, MOTOR Maintenance Schedules, MOTOR Fluids, API EOLCS, Auto Care VCdb/Qdb/PCdb/Brand Table/PAdb, and each filter supplier/aggregator. Carried from pre-pivot; the row set is unchanged, the rights columns are not.
- FR-2: The matrix inventories each of the nine fact domains separately (vehicle identity/configuration; normal/severe oil interval; time/mileage/condition-based schedule; OEM lubricant specification; viscosity; capacity/context; EOLCS license/product claim; filter brand/product; vehicle-filter fitment). Per-field approval/prohibition remains expressible and is now crossed with per-redistribution-mode approval/prohibition.
- FR-3: Every row carries the extended attribute set listed under Included: the pre-pivot attributes **plus** `web_serving`, `per_request_only_no_bulk_api`, `response_caps_or_rate_limits`, `scrape_visibility_notice_or_licensing`, `continued_serving_after_termination`, and `attribution_placement_ui`. `not_found` is recorded explicitly rather than left blank. A boolean is backed by an evidence reference, never inferred from an earlier offline decision.
- FR-4: The status enum is exactly `web_approved`, `web_approved_with_conditions`, `web_evaluation_only`, `offline_only_prior_approval`, `pending`, `rejected`. Only the first two may reach a production Fly deploy image. Ambiguous terms map to `pending`. **This retires the pre-pivot FR-4 enum** — an `approved` value that predates 2026-08-01 must be migrated to one of the new states, not carried across, and the migration is a reviewed operation.
- FR-5: The free-source proof-of-concept catalog is built only from clearly reusable sources under the U.S. Government works lane, and the hosted-serving PoC renders it end-to-end through the actual server-side query layer (DOS-M09-005). The output is a quantified field/coverage/quality gap report **and** a hosted-serving conformance note (response shape, per-response counts, per-source rate-limit satisfiability) before any commercial request is issued.
- FR-6: RFI/RFP packs, sample evaluations, scoring, and contract redlines exist for documented gaps only, and record answers to the vendor questions on stable provider IDs, normalization/cross-referencing, hosted serving from a public custom domain, per-response caps or rate limits, the "no bulk API" restriction, scrapability of hosted responses, continued serving of already-published catalog releases after termination, and whether browser-UI attribution satisfies the attribution requirement.
- FR-7: A machine-readable `config/data_sources.yml` mirrors the matrix; each source has `source_key`, `status` (from the FR-4 enum), `allowed_environments`, `delivery_method`, `license_version`, `attribution_text`, `expires_on`, and `approval_evidence`, extended with field-level `allowed_uses`, `cost_model`, `derived_works`, `historical_snapshots`, `retain_build_artifacts`, `terms_captured_at`, and `terms_sha256`. It **adds** the hosted-redistribution keys: `web_serving`, `per_request_only_no_bulk_api`, `response_caps_or_rate_limits` (structured: max rows/response, max requests/minute/IP, max requests/day/IP; numeric values or `not_specified` — never blank), `scrape_visibility_notice_or_licensing`, `continued_serving_after_termination`, `attribution_placement_ui`. **The pre-pivot key `offline_redistribution` is retired**: enforcement now reads `web_serving`, and any source that still carries only `offline_redistribution: true` without an explicit `web_serving` value is treated as `pending`. Every boolean is backed by an evidence reference.
- FR-8: CI fails a production Fly deploy image build if an enabled source is missing, expired, not `web_approved` / `web_approved_with_conditions`, or has `web_serving: false` (including the specific case where `offline_redistribution: true` but `web_serving` is unset — this is precisely the class the rescope targets). The failure diagnostic names the source, the failing predicate, and the exact matrix row.
- FR-9: Secrets/API keys are referenced by environment-variable name only and never stored in source control, fixtures, manifests, deploy image layers, or logs. The build-time acquisition path may hold vendor credentials; the runtime request path holds none (ADR-0004 §"Secrets": "There are no third-party API keys at runtime").
- FR-10: A Product fallback decision (feature flag plus UI/data fallback, defaulting to user-entered data for schedules, fluids, EOLCS, and fitment) exists for every `pending`, `offline_only_prior_approval`, or `rejected` row. The flag is respected by the server-side catalog query layer (DOS-M09-005), which returns an explicit unsupported status rather than an empty answer when a source is disabled.
- FR-11 (new): Attribution and any vendor-required marks are surfaced in the hosted browser application on the pages that render the sourced facts, and on a stable `/attribution` route. Attribution copy is short enough to render in the app's attribution views (which M02/M05/M09 own) and is the source of truth for the Netlify static attribution pages served from `legal.digitaloilsticker.com`. There is no app-bundle "credits screen" surface; browser DOM and static-site pages are the only surfaces.
- FR-12 (new): The runtime query layer that consumes this matrix (DOS-M09-005) MUST NOT expose bulk download or per-source dump endpoints of any kind. It MUST enforce per-IP and per-socket rate limits at or below the tightest `response_caps_or_rate_limits` value recorded for any enabled source. This binding is recorded in this card because it is a redistribution-rights constraint, not a performance choice.
- FR-13 (new): A source's server-side response MUST NOT expose values (raw record IDs, provider tokens, licensing tokens, contract identifiers) whose disclosure would breach the source's terms. The matrix records, per source, which internal fields must be stripped or opaqued before response serialization.
- FR-14 (new): The pre-pivot FR framing that CI gates on `offline_redistribution` is explicitly retired. This FR exists so future readers see the retirement is deliberate, not lost in an edit.
- FR-15 (new): Rollback of a matrix state MUST NOT re-enable a source for hosted serving without explicit reviewer sign-off recorded in the reverting change. Because the deploy image is the redistribution artifact, a silent rollback that re-includes a `pending` source is a redistribution event, not a build regression.

## Technical implementation contract
### Components and ownership boundaries
- `docs/data/source-rights-matrix.md`: human-readable decision record; owned jointly by the product owner and the authorized legal/business reviewer; engineers contribute evidence, never legal conclusions. The rescope adds the hosted-redistribution columns and reviewer sign-off on those columns specifically.
- `config/data_sources.yml`: machine-readable mirror consumed by the ingest pipeline, by the CI policy lint at deploy image build time, and by the read-only server-side catalog query layer (DOS-M09-005) at request time to enforce feature-flag-driven fallbacks. Owned by the data workstream with change review by product/legal.
- Policy-lint CI job: evaluates every enabled source against the YAML contract on the Fly deploy image build (not on unit test runs, though it is welcome to run there too). Owned by the pipeline/DevOps boundary; the Fly deploy pipeline (DOS-M09-006) refuses to build an image that fails this check.
- RFI/RFP pack, sample scorecards, redline archive, and hosted-serving PoC output: procurement and evidence artifacts stored with the planning docs; sanitized before commit.
- Attribution copy repository: owned here (source of truth for the strings), consumed by M02/M05/M09 browser UI and by the Netlify static site (DOS-M09-007). Copy changes flow from this card, not the other way around.

### Data and persistence
No runtime database changes. Persistence is Git-versioned documents plus `config/data_sources.yml`. Terms evidence snapshots are stored with `terms_captured_at` and `terms_sha256` so a later dispute can prove exactly which terms were reviewed. Downstream, approved entries feed the `data_sources` CatalogRepo table defined in master prompt section 8 (stable `id`, provider/type/name, canonical URL or document locator, license reference, provider version, retrieved/effective/verified timestamps, SHA-256, attribution, authority and review status), and are baked into the read-only SQLite catalog inside the Fly deploy image per ADR-0004 §"Catalog database on Fly." The catalog database is opened read-only; there is no runtime write path that could re-introduce a disallowed source.

### Interfaces and events
- The ingest ledger (DOS-M03-003) and every adapter (DOS-M03-004/005/007/008/009) resolve a `source_key` in `config/data_sources.yml` before acquiring or transforming anything. Adapters also read the hosted-redistribution keys so a source that is acquirable but not web-servable is retained at raw/normalized layers with the hosted flag off, ready to be released the moment terms permit.
- The catalog compiler (DOS-M03-010) reads the same file to enforce unauthorized-source, expired-rights, and *not-authorized-for-web-serving* blockers, and to emit the license/attribution report. The report structure adds per-source hosted-redistribution rows.
- The Fly deploy pipeline (DOS-M09-006) refuses to publish an image whose bundled catalog contains rows sourced from anything other than `web_approved` / `web_approved_with_conditions`.
- The read-only server-side catalog query layer (DOS-M09-005) reads the enabled/disabled state per source at boot and respects it in every response, returning explicit unsupported statuses (INV-11) rather than silently omitting data.
- Status transitions are ordinary reviewed pull requests referencing approval evidence; no runtime events, no dynamic feature-flag service.

### Failure and recovery behavior
- CI policy lint fails closed: a missing, expired, `pending`, `web_evaluation_only`, `offline_only_prior_approval`, `rejected`, or `web_serving: false` source that is enabled for production stops the Fly deploy image build with a diagnostic naming the source, the failing predicate, and the exact matrix row.
- Revocation or expiry of a right (`expires_on`) automatically re-gates the affected adapters; the recovery path is either renewed written terms (deploy a new image once the matrix is updated) or activation of the documented fallback flag (also a deploy — there is no runtime toggle). "Continued serving after termination" applies to catalog releases that were already deployed at the moment of termination; the next deploy after termination must not re-include the source.
- Loss of approval evidence is treated as `pending` (fail closed), never as tacit approval. A rollback to a prior image that pre-dates the loss of approval is treated as a redistribution event (FR-15) and requires reviewer sign-off in the reverting change.

### Security and privacy
- Secrets/API keys by environment-variable name only; never in source control, fixtures, manifests, deploy image layers, or logs. A secret-scan job runs over the matrix, YAML, evidence archive, and Docker image layers.
- Approval evidence links are sanitized (no personal contact data beyond what procurement requires; no credentials embedded in URLs).
- Vendor sample data is stored under its evaluation license terms and never mixed into production fixtures.
- The runtime query layer that consumes this matrix takes no personal parameters and never logs values passed as query parameters — this is enforced by DOS-M09-005 and DOS-M09-008 but recorded here so the redistribution-rights record includes it. A vendor-facing consequence: search/resolve endpoints cannot become a de facto activity log about the user, which strengthens the honesty of every attribution claim.

### Accessibility and performance
The card ships no UI. Its accessibility- and performance-adjacent obligations are (a) `attribution_text` must be short enough to render in the app's attribution views and readable by a screen reader without reordering (M02/M05/M09 own the surfaces), and (b) `response_caps_or_rate_limits` must be tight enough that they can be enforced within the DOS-M09-005 latency budget without becoming the dominant p95 factor. Both are recorded per row and reviewed against the surface owners before matrix sign-off.

## UX and content states
This issue ships no UI, but it fixes content-state policy consumed later: every non-`web_approved` source has a documented UI/data fallback and feature flag; fallback values are labeled `User entered`, never `Manufacturer recommended` or `Compatible`; missing/unlicensed domains present as unknown/unsupported (INV-11) rather than as fabricated or silently omitted values; required attribution and trademark treatment recorded here is the source of truth for the browser application, the `/attribution` route in the LiveView app, and the Netlify static attribution pages served from `legal.digitaloilsticker.com`. There is no app-bundle credits surface to keep in sync.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions
- Risk: **the rescope may downgrade sources previously approved for offline redistribution.** This is the whole point of the card and the risk is that it materially shrinks the near-term catalog. Mitigation: the free-source (17 U.S.C. §105) baseline is unaffected and remains shippable; the documented user-entered fallback covers schedules/fluids/EOLCS/fitment when a source cannot serve over the web. This risk is accepted, not mitigated away.
- Risk: hosted catalog responses are publicly scrapable in a way an app bundle was not (per ADR-0004 §"Consequences → Lost — honestly" and the pivot doc). Rate limiting and per-response caps are documented mitigations that reduce practical bulk extraction; they are not a legal solution. If a source's terms make scrapability unacceptable, the source is `rejected` rather than served under aspirational rate limits.
- Risk: vendors offer subscription-only terms; handled by AC-7's separate product/finance/legal ADR or the manual/disabled fallback — never a silent subscription dependency, and now the subscription lifecycle has to also cover the hosted-serving continuation clause (FR-15).
- Risk: terms evidence for public sources is missing or ambiguous; policy is `pending`, not `web_approved`, which can shrink the hosted baseline; mitigated by the documented-facts lane (human-reviewed extraction queue) where terms permit.
- Risk: procurement timelines stall downstream adapters; mitigated because adapters may proceed fixture-only with production flags off, and because acquisition may proceed at raw/normalized layers with the hosted flag off, ready to release the moment terms permit.
- Decision recorded: the vPIC standalone database is unsuitable for make/model enumeration (VIN-decode-only) — carried into the matrix verbatim.
- Decision recorded: the runtime query layer (DOS-M09-005) is per-request only. No bulk download endpoint is offered under any source's terms — this is a product-side floor set below every vendor's tightest cap.
- Open question: which OEM-published candidates and filter suppliers will answer RFI/RFP requests with usable written terms *for hosted serving specifically*; the matrix must show the searched set either way.
- Open question: whether a future PWA/service-worker decision (DOS-M09-010) will reintroduce a client-cached slice of the catalog and thereby require a third redistribution-mode column here. Recorded so it is not forgotten; not resolved by this card.

### Technical implementation contract (pre-pivot)
#### Components and ownership boundaries
- `docs/data/source-rights-matrix.md`: human-readable decision record; owned jointly by the product owner and the authorized legal/business reviewer; engineers contribute evidence, never legal conclusions.
- `config/data_sources.yml`: machine-readable mirror consumed by the ingest pipeline and CI policy lint; owned by the data workstream with change review by product/legal.
- Policy-lint CI job: evaluates every enabled source against the YAML contract on production builds; owned by the pipeline/DevOps boundary.
- RFI/RFP pack, sample scorecards, and redline archive: procurement artifacts stored with the planning docs; sanitized before commit.
#### Data and persistence (pre-pivot)
No runtime database changes. Persistence is Git-versioned documents plus `config/data_sources.yml`. Terms evidence snapshots are stored with `terms_captured_at` and `terms_sha256` so a later dispute can prove exactly which terms were reviewed. Downstream, approved entries feed the `data_sources` CatalogRepo table defined in master prompt section 8 (stable `id`, provider/type/name, canonical URL or document locator, license reference, provider version, retrieved/effective/verified timestamps, SHA-256, attribution, authority and review status).
#### Interfaces and events (pre-pivot)
- The ingest ledger (DOS-M03-003) and every adapter (DOS-M03-004/005/007/008/009) resolve a `source_key` in `config/data_sources.yml` before acquiring or transforming anything.
- The catalog compiler (DOS-M03-010) reads the same file to enforce unauthorized-source and expired-rights blockers and to emit the license/attribution report.
- Status transitions are ordinary reviewed pull requests referencing approval evidence; no runtime events.
#### Failure and recovery behavior (pre-pivot)
- CI policy lint fails closed: a missing, expired, `pending`, `evaluation_only`, `rejected`, or `offline_redistribution: false` source that is enabled for production stops the build with a diagnostic naming the source and failing predicate.
- Revocation or expiry of a right (`expires_on`) automatically re-gates the affected adapters; the recovery path is either renewed written terms or activation of the documented fallback flag.
- Loss of approval evidence is treated as `pending` (fail closed), never as tacit approval.
#### Security and privacy (pre-pivot)
- Secrets/API keys by environment-variable name only; never in source control, fixtures, manifests, mobile bundle assets, or logs. A secret-scan job runs over the matrix, YAML, and evidence archive.
- Approval evidence links are sanitized (no personal contact data beyond what procurement requires; no credentials embedded in URLs).
- Vendor sample data is stored under its evaluation license terms and never mixed into production fixtures.
#### Accessibility and performance (pre-pivot)
N/A — planning/governance deliverable with no UI or runtime path. The only performance-adjacent output is that `attribution_text` must be short enough to render in the app's attribution views, which M02/M05 own.
