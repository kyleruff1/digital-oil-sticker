## Outcome
A signed product/legal decision record establishes which sources may be acquired, transformed, stored, shipped offline, refreshed, attributed, and retained after contract termination.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks
This is the hard gate for every non-vPIC production ingest. It prevents engineers from building against a source that cannot legally be bundled in the offline app and gives product an explicit reduced-data fallback. It directly unblocks the raw landing zone (DOS-M03-003) and the gated adapters for schedules/fluids (DOS-M03-007), EOLCS products (DOS-M03-008), and filter fitment (DOS-M03-009), and it gates production completion of the catalog release (DOS-M03-010). It also enforces non-negotiable rule 4 (no fabricated automotive facts; every displayed fact has provenance and a license permitting offline redistribution).

## Included
- Create `docs/data/source-rights-matrix.md` with one row per candidate—not only chosen vendors—including the NHTSA vPIC API (with its VIN-decode-only standalone database recorded as unsuitable for make/model enumeration), FuelEconomy.gov web services/downloads, other relevant government/open datasets, each OEM-published candidate, MOTOR Maintenance Schedules, MOTOR Fluids, API EOLCS, Auto Care VCdb/Qdb/PCdb/Brand Table/PAdb, and each filter supplier/aggregator.
- Inventory candidates separately for: vehicle identity/configuration; normal/severe oil interval; time/mileage/condition-based schedule; OEM lubricant specification; viscosity; capacity/context; EOLCS license/product claim; filter brand/product; and vehicle-filter fitment. A source may be approved for one field and prohibited for another.
- Record per row: owner/contact, official URL, exact terms/license name and version or `not_found`, terms evidence capture date, access mechanism, cost model (`free`, `one_time_snapshot`, `subscription`, `usage_based`, `unknown`), quoted/contracted cost, credentials owner, geographic scope, covered model years/configurations, update cadence, measured completeness, attribution, automated-access right, transformation/derived-work right, raw-data retention, build/backup retention, historical-snapshot right, offline/device embedding and redistribution, continued distribution after termination, sublicensing/end-user scope, screenshot/mark usage, deletion-on-termination duty, update options, and approval evidence link.
- Record a status enum: `approved`, `approved_with_conditions`, `evaluation_only`, `pending`, `rejected`. Only the first two may reach a production catalog.
- Ask vendors specifically whether stable provider IDs may be stored, whether records may be normalized/cross-referenced, whether a content-addressed device copy is permitted, and what happens to already-installed copies on termination.
- Run a free-source proof of concept before issuing commercial requests. Produce a field/coverage/quality gap report; vendor procurement is limited to documented gaps.
- For unresolved gaps, create an RFI/RFP pack with normalized sample input/output, requested rolling 30-year/major-make coverage, required qualifiers/revisions, expected record counts, delivery/checksum/change semantics, sample/evaluation license, acceptance tests, and the preferred one-time perpetual snapshot rights. Evaluate at least the credible options found; record scoring for rights fit, field/vehicle coverage, sample quality, crosswalk effort, payload size, update options, support, one-time and five-year cost, and vendor lock-in.
- Obtain a representative sample under evaluation terms and run adapter/coverage/quality/spatial-size spikes before selection. Preserve contract redline questions and the final written answer for every required right; marketing statements and sales emails that do not amend the agreement are not sufficient approval.
- Write a Product fallback decision for every pending/rejected row. For schedules, fluids, EOLCS, and fitment the default fallback is user-entered data.

## Technical implementation contract
### Components and ownership boundaries
- `docs/data/source-rights-matrix.md`: human-readable decision record; owned jointly by the product owner and the authorized legal/business reviewer; engineers contribute evidence, never legal conclusions.
- `config/data_sources.yml`: machine-readable mirror consumed by the ingest pipeline and CI policy lint; owned by the data workstream with change review by product/legal.
- Policy-lint CI job: evaluates every enabled source against the YAML contract on production builds; owned by the pipeline/DevOps boundary.
- RFI/RFP pack, sample scorecards, and redline archive: procurement artifacts stored with the planning docs; sanitized before commit.
### Data and persistence
No runtime database changes. Persistence is Git-versioned documents plus `config/data_sources.yml`. Terms evidence snapshots are stored with `terms_captured_at` and `terms_sha256` so a later dispute can prove exactly which terms were reviewed. Downstream, approved entries feed the `data_sources` CatalogRepo table defined in master prompt section 8 (stable `id`, provider/type/name, canonical URL or document locator, license reference, provider version, retrieved/effective/verified timestamps, SHA-256, attribution, authority and review status).
### Interfaces and events
- The ingest ledger (DOS-M03-003) and every adapter (DOS-M03-004/005/007/008/009) resolve a `source_key` in `config/data_sources.yml` before acquiring or transforming anything.
- The catalog compiler (DOS-M03-010) reads the same file to enforce unauthorized-source and expired-rights blockers and to emit the license/attribution report.
- Status transitions are ordinary reviewed pull requests referencing approval evidence; no runtime events.
### Failure and recovery behavior
- CI policy lint fails closed: a missing, expired, `pending`, `evaluation_only`, `rejected`, or `offline_redistribution: false` source that is enabled for production stops the build with a diagnostic naming the source and failing predicate.
- Revocation or expiry of a right (`expires_on`) automatically re-gates the affected adapters; the recovery path is either renewed written terms or activation of the documented fallback flag.
- Loss of approval evidence is treated as `pending` (fail closed), never as tacit approval.
### Security and privacy
- Secrets/API keys by environment-variable name only; never in source control, fixtures, manifests, mobile bundle assets, or logs. A secret-scan job runs over the matrix, YAML, and evidence archive.
- Approval evidence links are sanitized (no personal contact data beyond what procurement requires; no credentials embedded in URLs).
- Vendor sample data is stored under its evaluation license terms and never mixed into production fixtures.
### Accessibility and performance
N/A — planning/governance deliverable with no UI or runtime path. The only performance-adjacent output is that `attribution_text` must be short enough to render in the app's attribution views, which M02/M05 own.

## UX and content states
This issue ships no UI, but it fixes content-state policy consumed later: every non-approved source has a documented UI/data fallback and feature flag; fallback values are labeled `User entered`, never `Manufacturer recommended` or `Compatible`; missing/unlicensed domains present as unknown/unsupported rather than fabricated values; required attribution and trademark treatment recorded here is the source of truth for the app and static-site attribution pages.

## Rollout and rollback
The matrix and YAML are versioned in Git; "rollout" is merging an approved revision, which immediately governs CI. Rollback is reverting to the previous approved revision. Downgrading a source's status (e.g., after termination or terms change) must flip its feature flag to the documented fallback in the same change; already-shipped catalog versions follow the contractually recorded continued-distribution terms rather than ad-hoc deletion.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions
- Risk: vendors offer subscription-only terms; handled by AC-7's separate product/finance/legal ADR or the manual/disabled fallback — never a silent subscription dependency.
- Risk: terms evidence for public sources is missing or ambiguous; policy is `pending`, not `approved`, which can shrink the free baseline; mitigated by the documented-facts lane (human-reviewed extraction queue) where terms permit.
- Risk: procurement timelines stall downstream adapters; mitigated because adapters may proceed fixture-only with production flags off.
- Decision to record: the vPIC standalone database is unsuitable for make/model enumeration (VIN-decode-only) — carried into the matrix verbatim.
- Open question: which OEM-published candidates and filter suppliers will answer RFI/RFP requests with usable written terms; the matrix must show the searched set either way.
