## Outcome
When the source matrix permits—whether the source is free/public, manually extracted discrete OEM facts, or a commercial snapshot—one or more adapters import configuration-qualified engine-oil intervals and lubricant requirements while preserving normal/severe conditions, initial/repeating rules, units, and OEM provenance.

> **2026-08-02 — ADR-0007 authorization.** The owner has selected lane **(b)** (commercial license, no independent verification) and authorized the FR-2 sample/contract spike with MOTOR or an approved equivalent. This card is no longer gated for lane-selection purposes; the immediate work is the spike itself, whose measurements land back in [DOS-M03-001](DOS-M03-001.md). The publication gate for imported rows remains off until ADR-0007's Q3 (coverage floor), Q4 (behavior for uncovered vehicles), Q5 (budget ceiling), and Q6 (rights-revocation behavior) are decided from the spike's evidence. See [ADR-0007](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0007-manufacturer-oil-schedule-sourcing.md).

## Why this matters and what it unblocks
The core recommendation feature must be based on rights-approved, attributable OEM-derived schedule facts, not vPIC or developer guesses. This issue supplies the schedule inputs used later by reminder logic without assuming that paid data is inherently better than acceptable public data. It feeds the `maintenance_schedules` and `oil_requirements` domains, enables the M05 "source-backed schedule before saving the vehicle" journey step, and blocks the schedule sections of DOS-M03-010.

## Context and source of truth
- Master prompt section 4.4: vPIC has no engine-oil interval, viscosity, oil capacity, oil-product, or filter-fitment fields; the documented-facts lane permits normalizing discrete facts from public OEM owner manuals, maintenance booklets, warranty/maintenance guides — original UI wording, no vendored PDFs/prose/table layouts; the free candidate matrix row "OEM time/distance/condition intervals and oil requirements"; procurement prefers a one-time bulk snapshot with perpetual rights.
- Master prompt section 8: `maintenance_schedules` (configuration/rule key, `service_type = engine_oil`, normal/severe/flexible condition, nullable miles/months, oil-life-monitor flag, recommendation text, source ID/locator, effective dates and verification state; at least one threshold or a documented indicator rule) and `oil_requirements` (SAE viscosity, API/ILSAC/ACEA/OEM codes as separately normalized facts, capacity value/unit and with/without-filter qualifier; no restricted standard text).
- Section 11.2 "Research-backed source policy" (verified 2026-07-31): MOTOR markets licensed OEM maintenance-schedule and fluids data with 1985+ domestic and import light-duty coverage; commercial terms must explicitly allow consumer-app use, derived/offline databases, device distribution, refreshes, and termination handling. Research-anchor URLs: [MOTOR Maintenance Schedules](https://www.motor.com/products-services/data-products/maintenance-schedules/), [MOTOR Fluids](https://www.motor.com/products-services/data-products/fluids/).
- Section 11.2 "Mandatory fallback": schedules default to user-entered data labeled `User entered` when no license is approved; non-negotiable rule 7 (vehicle controls prevail — oil-life monitors are labeled guidance, never converted to invented fixed intervals).
- DOS-M03-001 free-source inventory/gap report; DOS-M03-002 vocabulary; DOS-M03-003 landing zone; DOS-M03-006 crosswalks.

## Included
- Begin with the M03-001 free-source inventory and gap report. Evaluate each OEM/government/public candidate whose explicit terms could cover schedule/fluids fields; do not reject a free source merely because a commercial provider exists, and do not reuse publicly displayed OEM material absent adequate terms.
- For remaining gaps, run a provider sample/contract/data dictionary spike for MOTOR Maintenance Schedules + Fluids or an approved equivalent. Prefer a one-time rolling-30-year snapshot with perpetual derived/offline redistribution. Capture delivery format, authentication, rate limits, change semantics, measured coverage, provider vehicle IDs, one-time/update pricing, and every embedding/retention/termination right in M03-001.
- Parse only engine-oil/oil-filter service operations for initial scope, but preserve the provider's operation IDs so future services can be added without remapping.
- Represent mileage-only, time-only, and `whichever comes first` schedules; normal and severe schedules; initial service distinct from repeating interval; engine-oil-life-system/condition-based guidance as a non-numeric policy rather than invented miles/months.
- Ingest lubricant viscosity grades, OEM specification codes, capacity (including whether with-filter/dry-fill/unknown), engine/configuration qualifiers, temperature/operating qualifiers, and source notes where permitted.
- Preserve effective dates and source revisions. Never collapse conflicting or overlapping schedules without a recorded precedence/review decision.
- If no license is approved, implement the normalized adapter interface and synthetic fixtures only; production feature flag remains off and UI uses user-entered intervals/specifications.
- Activation of this card populates the downstream inner-plan key `maintenance_plan.manufacturer_viscosity` on each affected vehicle record (nullable string; the SAE viscosity grade from the licensed row's `oil_requirements.viscosity_grade`, per FR-6). This key is already wired in `DigitalOilStickerWeb.VehicleProfileLive`: when non-nil it renders under the "Manufacturer viscosity" heading with the "Factory recommendation" badge next to the value; when nil (today's shipping state) the whole section is suppressed. The badge is defined in `DigitalOilStickerWeb.Components.Badges.factory_recommendation_badge/1` and its display rules are in [RECOMMENDATION_CLAIMS_POLICY.md §"Factory recommendation label"](../../docs/product/RECOMMENDATION_CLAIMS_POLICY.md#factory-recommendation-label) and [ADR-0007 §"Downstream — Factory recommendation label"](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0007-manufacturer-oil-schedule-sourcing.md#downstream--factory-recommendation-label). No new client deployment is needed for the display to activate — the ingest that lands the field is the only unlocked step. The source-identifying fields (`source_locator`, `source_page`, `source_effective_date`, `source_id`, `verification_state`) stay server-side per ADR-0007 §"What every lane must preserve"; they must not ride out on the client's vehicle record.

## Excluded
- Redistributing source-manual PDFs, protected prose, table layouts, or images.
- Extracting from a source/method rejected by M03-001.
- Deriving a schedule from generic oil marketing.
- Converting condition-based monitors to unsupported fixed intervals.
- Recommending a retail product merely because viscosity matches.
Note preserved from the catalog: human-reviewed extraction of approved discrete factual values into original fields is explicitly in scope and must retain document/page/section provenance and second-review evidence.

## Functional requirements
- FR-1: Adapter work starts from the M03-001 free-source inventory and gap report; each OEM/government/public candidate with potentially adequate terms is evaluated on its own merits; no free source is rejected merely because a commercial provider exists, and no publicly displayed OEM material is reused absent adequate terms.
- FR-2: For residual gaps, a provider spike (MOTOR Maintenance Schedules + Fluids or approved equivalent) captures delivery format, authentication, rate limits, change semantics, measured coverage, provider vehicle IDs, one-time/update pricing, and every embedding/retention/termination right, recorded back into the M03-001 matrix, preferring a one-time rolling-30-year snapshot with perpetual derived/offline redistribution.
- FR-3: Parsing covers only engine-oil/oil-filter service operations initially while preserving provider operation IDs for future remap-free expansion.
- FR-4: Normalized schedule records support mileage-only, time-only, and `whichever comes first` kinds; normal and severe operating conditions; initial service distinct from repeating interval; and oil-life-system/condition-based guidance as a non-numeric policy (never invented miles/months).
- FR-5: Normalized schedule fields are exactly: `external_schedule_key`, `external_vehicle_key`, `service_code`, `operating_condition`, `schedule_kind`, `initial_interval_miles`, `initial_interval_months`, `repeat_interval_miles`, `repeat_interval_months`, `oil_life_monitor_required`, `qualifier_text`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, and full provenance.
- FR-6: Normalized requirement fields are exactly: `external_requirement_key`, `external_vehicle_key`, `engine_key`, `viscosity_grade`, `specification_code`, `capacity_value`, `capacity_unit`, `capacity_context`, `temperature_min_c`, `temperature_max_c`, `qualifier_text`, and provenance.
- FR-7: Intervals are nullable individually but a fixed schedule must provide miles or months; positive integer checks apply; "whichever comes first" is computed downstream from independently preserved dimensions, not collapsed at ingest.
- FR-8: Effective dates and source revisions are preserved; conflicting or overlapping schedules are never collapsed without a recorded precedence/review decision (DOS-M03-002 conflict policy).
- FR-9: Manual documented-facts extraction retains document/page/section provenance and second-review evidence for every extracted value.
- FR-10: No record reaches publication without an exact/reviewed vehicle crosswalk (DOS-M03-006) and allowed provenance.
- FR-11: If no license is approved, the normalized adapter interface ships with synthetic fixtures only; the production feature flag remains off and the UI path uses user-entered intervals/specifications labeled `User entered`.
- FR-12: Canonical unit conversion (integer miles, positive integer months, decimal liters) preserves the auditable source value/unit alongside the converted value.

## Technical implementation contract
### Components and ownership boundaries
- Per-source adapters (public-OEM extraction adapter, provider snapshot adapter) sharing one normalized output contract; owned by the data workstream.
- Human-reviewed extraction queue for documented-facts sources: capture form (document URL/revision/market/applicability/page-section locator/value/reviewer/checksum), first-pass extractor, and mandatory second reviewer.
- Feature gate: production schedule ingestion is enabled per source by `config/data_sources.yml` status only.
- Domain review loop: automotive domain reviewer checks reconciliation samples before publication.
### Data and persistence
- Raw deliveries/documents in the DOS-M03-003 landing zone with receipts; normalized schedule/requirement rows in staging validated against DOS-M03-002 schemas with full `_provenance` (including `field_path` for document extractions).
- Downstream mapping to section 8 `maintenance_schedules` (`service_type = engine_oil`, OLM flag, effective dates, verification state) and `oil_requirements` (separately normalized viscosity/spec codes; capacity with with/without-filter qualifier; no restricted standard text).
- Source value/unit pairs persist beside canonical conversions for auditability (FR-12).
### Interfaces and events
- Input: rights-gated deliveries and approved-document extraction entries; provider vehicle identities flow to DOS-M03-006 for crosswalking.
- Output: normalized schedule/requirement staging plus coverage and conflict reports (records by make/year/configuration, unmatched provider keys, schedule kinds) consumed by DOS-M03-010's schedule sections.
- Gate signals: per-source feature flags and rights status determine whether the adapter output is publishable or fixture-only.
### Failure and recovery behavior
- Records failing schema, unit, positivity, or provenance checks are rejected to a quarantine report with reasons; nothing invalid reaches staging as publishable.
- Unmatched `external_vehicle_key`s are retained and reported; they never publish and never fuzzy-join.
- Conflicting/overlapping schedules surface as QA conflicts requiring recorded precedence/review decisions; ingest never auto-resolves.
- Rights revocation flips the source's flag off; already-released catalogs follow contractual continued-distribution terms; the UI fallback is user-entered data.
### Security and privacy
- Licensed deliveries stay in the access-controlled landing zone; no provider payloads, credentials, or contract documents in Git, fixtures, or logs.
- Synthetic fixtures are clearly branded synthetic and excluded from production builds.
- No personal data is present in schedule/fluids sources; extraction-queue records identify reviewers by role/identity per team policy, not more.
### Accessibility and performance
N/A for accessibility — build-side adapters. Performance: adapter throughput is batch-oriented and secondary to correctness; payload-size measurements from provider samples feed the DOS-M03-001 scoring (spatial-size spike) and DOS-M03-010 size budgets.
### Data change specifics
- Source/version/license: each adapter binds to a `source_key` with status `approved`/`approved_with_conditions` for production (fixture-only otherwise); provider snapshot versions and document revisions are pinned in provenance; license versions recorded per delivery.
- Mapping: provider/document facts → FR-5/FR-6 normalized fields → section 8 `maintenance_schedules`/`oil_requirements`; provider operation IDs preserved; original wording replaced by original UI phrasing downstream (no protected prose).
- Stable keys: `external_schedule_key`/`external_requirement_key` from immutable provider keys or documented natural keys per DOS-M03-002; vehicle linkage exclusively via `external_vehicle_key` through DOS-M03-006 crosswalks.
- Constraints/indexes: positive-integer interval checks; miles-or-months requirement for fixed schedules; uniqueness on external keys + effective dates; staging indexes on `external_vehicle_key` and operating condition.
- Idempotency: re-running an adapter over the same raw SHA and transform version yields identical normalized hashes; snapshot re-imports dedupe by external key + revision.
- Rejected-row policy: schema/unit/provenance failures and unmatched vehicles quarantine with reasons; conflict candidates route to the recorded precedence/review process; nothing silently dropped.
- Checksums: raw delivery SHA-256 via receipts; extraction-queue entries carry document checksums; normalized output hashes recorded per run.
- Coverage report: records by make/year/configuration, unmatched provider keys, and schedule kinds; conflict report enumerates unresolved overlaps (AC-5).
- Fixture strategy: synthetic fixtures cover normal/severe, miles/months, initial/repeat, OLM-only, multiple engines, capacity context, and revisions (AC-2); provider-derived fixtures only where license terms permit.
- Update cadence: one-time snapshot baseline; optional refresh purchases are separately priced decisions recorded in M03-001; document extractions are event-driven per approved document revision.
- Rollback/recovery: staging outputs versioned per run; a bad import is discarded by re-pinning the prior run; published catalog corrections ship as new catalog versions with supersession, never in-place edits.

## UX and content states
No UI is built here, but this issue determines UI truth states: `schedule_supported` vs. user-entered fallback; OLM/condition-based guidance rendered as a monitor instruction rather than a fabricated date (non-negotiable rule 7); "Source unavailable" and `User entered` labels when the flag is off; provenance (provider/document, source version/revision, retrieved/verified date, market/condition) available for every published schedule per the section 6 terminology contract.

## Test plan
### Automated
- Adapter contract suite over the FR-5/FR-6 field sets: schema validation, positive-integer checks, miles-or-months enforcement for fixed schedules, nullable-interval handling, OLM-policy (non-numeric) representation.
- Fixture matrix per AC-2 (normal/severe, miles/months, initial/repeat, OLM-only, multiple engines, capacity context, revisions), all deterministic and license-clean.
- Unit round-trip property tests: source value/unit → canonical → display conversions without loss; audit fields preserved (AC-4).
- Publication-gate tests: records without exact/reviewed crosswalks or with disallowed provenance cannot enter publishable staging (AC-3).
- Conflict tests: overlapping/conflicting schedule fixtures produce QA conflicts, never silent collapse.
- Idempotency test: identical raw SHA + transform version reruns produce identical normalized hashes.
### Manual/device matrix
- Automotive domain reviewer checks reconciliation samples (normalized values vs. source documents/deliveries) and signs off.
- Second-review verification of a sample of documented-facts extractions (document/page/section provenance present, checksums match).
- Procurement/legal confirmation that the production flag state matches the rights matrix (approved delivery vs. fixture-only).
- No device work: N/A — build-side adapters.

## Rollout and rollback
Production enablement is per-source feature-flag driven by rights status; a source ships only in a catalog release passing DOS-M03-010 gates. Rollback: disable the source flag and rebuild (fallback to user-entered UI), or re-pin the prior catalog version; historical user snapshots of schedule values persist in the browser's IndexedDB `dos_local` per [DOS-M09-001](DOS-M09-001.md) regardless of catalog changes (there is no server-side `UserRepo` — INV-23, ADR-0004 §4). Snapshot refreshes are new versioned imports with diff review, never in-place mutation.

## Documentation and evidence
Approved contract flag or disabled-feature evidence; adapter contract suite; reconciliation samples checked by automotive domain reviewer; coverage/conflict reports. Plus the provider spike write-up (delivery, coverage, pricing, rights capture) and the documented-facts extraction-queue records with second-review evidence.

## Dependency rationale
Depends on DOS-M03-001 (the rights gate and free-source gap report that decide free vs. snapshot vs. fixture-only), DOS-M03-002 (schedule/requirement vocabulary, units, conflict policy), and DOS-M03-003 (receipted landing for deliveries and documents). ## Risks, decisions, and open questions
- Risk: free/OEM-documented coverage is sparse or version-mismatched per configuration; mitigated by measured configuration-by-configuration coverage reporting and the gap-bounded procurement path.
- Risk: provider vehicle IDs resolve poorly to the vPIC-derived corpus; the crosswalk review queue quantifies and gates this instead of hiding it.
- Risk: subtle unit/context errors (with-filter vs. dry-fill capacity); mitigated by `capacity_context`, round-trip tests, and domain review.
- Decision: OLM/condition-based systems are represented as non-numeric policy — converting them to fixed intervals is excluded scope.
- Open question: whether MOTOR (or an equivalent) grants the one-time perpetual snapshot terms; both outcomes are planned (production adapter vs. fixture-only + user-entered fallback).

All acceptance criteria checked; adapters and extraction queue implemented against the exact FR-5/FR-6 contracts; automated suites (contract, fixtures, round-trip, gates, conflicts, idempotency) green in CI; domain-review and second-review evidence attached; coverage/conflict reports published; rights-matrix flag state verified; DOS-M03-010 schedule sections unblocked or the fixture-only/disabled disposition documented.
