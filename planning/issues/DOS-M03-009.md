## Outcome
Free supplier files or commercial filter products/applications whose planned use is approved resolve through the required vehicle reference identities, including every qualifier and effective date. ACES/PIES/VCdb are used only when needed and rights-approved; they are not assumed prerequisites for an otherwise complete free supplier feed.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks
ACES supplies a standard and VCdb supplies reference identities; neither alone supplies a manufacturer's filter application rows. This issue prevents the common scope error of calling a year/make/model-only guess "compatible." It supplies the `filter_brands`/`filter_products`/`filter_fitments` domain, enables the M05 filter picker to show only licensed configuration-specific applications, and blocks the filter sections of DOS-M03-010. It enforces non-negotiable rule 5: filter compatibility is part-number/configuration-specific.

## Context and source of truth
- Master prompt section 4.4: candidate sources must deliver configuration-specific filter applications; ACES is the exchange standard for fitment; it and VCdb do not themselves grant a parts catalog; free candidate matrix row "Oil-filter application" — exact part-to-configuration fitment plus qualifiers only; a brand, dimensions alone, or unlicensed aggregator cross-reference is insufficient.
- Master prompt section 8: `filter_brands`, `filter_products`, `filter_fitments` (stable brand/part number, configuration/provider application key, position/service type, qualifiers, source and effective status).
- Section 11.2 "Research-backed source policy" (verified 2026-07-31): Auto Care Association defines ACES as a fitment communication standard and VCdb as the normalized vehicle reference database; VCdb/Qdb/PCdb/Brand Table access is subscription-based; a VCdb subscription supplies reference IDs and does not grant a filter manufacturer's application records or product catalog; product application records must come from a filter supplier, distributor, or licensed aggregator, with redistribution rights; PIES and its reference databases may be needed for product attributes. Research-anchor URLs: [ACES](https://www.autocare.org/aces), [VCdb](https://www.autocare.org/data-and-information/data-standards/databases/vehicle-configuration-database-vcdb), [Auto Care subscriptions](https://www.autocare.org/data-standards/subscriptions), [PIES](https://www.autocare.org/pies).
- Public manufacturer candidates named by this issue: [WIX Filter Finder](https://www.wixfilters.com/en-us/filter-finder.html), [Purolator Part Finder](https://www.purolatornow.com/en/part-finder.html), [FRAM online catalog](https://www.fram-europe.com/en/catalogue/online-catalogue.html).
- Section 11.2 "Mandatory fallback": fitment defaults to user-entered filter part numbers labeled `User entered` when rights are not secured; DOS-M03-001 governs per-source dispositions.

## Included
- First inventory any supplier/government/OEM product and fitment datasets that are free **and explicitly reusable**; measure their field/configuration coverage and use paid ACES/PIES/provider data only for documented gaps.
- Seed that inventory with official public manufacturer candidates such as the WIX Filter Finder, Purolator Part Finder, and FRAM online catalog, plus any first-party downloadable application guides discovered for the U.S. market. These are candidate publishers, not pre-approved bulk feeds: request an export/API or written reuse permission when the public interface does not publish suitable automation/redistribution terms.
- For remaining gaps, obtain required Auto Care rights and supplier/aggregator agreements for ACES application XML/approved JSON and, when needed, PIES product content. Prefer a one-time version-pinned bulk snapshot. Confirm separately for reference and supplier data the rights to normalize/derive, retain build backups, vendor in the app, store on end-user devices, retain historical snapshots, continue distribution of the acquired version after termination, and optionally buy future updates.
- Pin ACES schema version, VCdb/Qdb/PCdb/Brand Table/PAdb versions, supplier delivery version, and subscription code handling. Credentials/subscription codes stay build-side only.
- Validate incoming documents against the pinned schema and reference DB versions before transformation.
- Ingest filter brand, product/part number, line/sub-brand, status, application vehicle/base-vehicle/configuration IDs, position, quantity, notes, engine/aspiration/fuel/body/drive/transmission qualifiers, Qdb qualifiers, and effective dates supplied.
- Preserve supplier application record IDs and `add/change/delete` semantics. A deletion removes the current catalog fitment but does not rewrite historical user snapshots.
- Resolve ACES/provider vehicles through DOS-M03-006; publish only exact or reviewed qualified applications. A base-vehicle-only application may be broad only when the supplier record explicitly asserts it without disqualifying qualifiers.
- Treat each supplier as its own assertion. Conflicting supplier fitment is a QA conflict, not an automatic union described as universal compatibility.

## Excluded
- Assuming a VCdb subscription includes application data.
- Crawling a supplier web catalog when its terms or robots/access rules reject that method.
- Using part-number cross-references as fitment proof unless a rights-approved source explicitly provides that assertion.
- Dropping qualifiers to increase match count.
Note preserved from the catalog: an offered public download/feed or written-permission export remains in scope.

## Preconditions

- DOS-M03-003 operational (deliveries land with receipts and pinned versions).

## Functional requirements
- FR-1: The free-and-explicitly-reusable supplier/government/OEM inventory is completed first, with measured field/configuration coverage, and paid ACES/PIES/provider procurement is limited to documented gaps.
- FR-2: The inventory is seeded with the named public manufacturer candidates (WIX Filter Finder, Purolator Part Finder, FRAM online catalog) plus discovered first-party downloadable application guides for the U.S. market, each handled as a candidate publisher requiring an export/API or written reuse permission when public terms are unsuitable.
- FR-3: For gaps, Auto Care rights and supplier/aggregator agreements cover ACES application XML/approved JSON and, when needed, PIES product content, preferring a one-time version-pinned bulk snapshot, with the rights to normalize/derive, retain build backups, vendor in the app, store on end-user devices, retain historical snapshots, continue distribution after termination, and optionally buy future updates confirmed separately for reference data and supplier data.
- FR-4: ACES schema version, VCdb/Qdb/PCdb/Brand Table/PAdb versions, supplier delivery version, and subscription code handling are pinned; credentials/subscription codes are build-side only.
- FR-5: Incoming documents are validated against the pinned schema and reference DB versions before transformation; mismatches are rejected, not coerced.
- FR-6: Ingest captures filter brand, product/part number, line/sub-brand, status, application vehicle/base-vehicle/configuration IDs, position, quantity, notes, engine/aspiration/fuel/body/drive/transmission qualifiers, Qdb qualifiers, and effective dates supplied.
- FR-7: Supplier application record IDs and `add/change/delete` semantics are preserved; a deletion removes the current catalog fitment but never rewrites historical user snapshots.
- FR-8: The normalized application record is exactly: `supplier_application_key`, `external_vehicle_key`, `filter_product_key`, `position`, `quantity`, `qualifiers[]` (typed code + source text), `application_status`, `effective_from`, `effective_to`, and provenance.
- FR-9: Vehicles resolve through DOS-M03-006; only exact or reviewed qualified applications publish; a base-vehicle-only application is broad only when the supplier record explicitly asserts it without disqualifying qualifiers.
- FR-10: Each supplier is its own assertion; conflicting supplier fitment becomes a QA conflict, never an automatic union described as universal compatibility.
- FR-11: Part-number search normalizes by uppercase/alphanumeric shadow key while preserving the display part number; products are never merged solely because normalized part numbers match across brands.
- FR-12: Records referring to missing reference IDs, invalid schema versions, unresolved delete targets, or unsupported qualifier semantics are rejected and quarantined with reason.

## Technical implementation contract
### Components and ownership boundaries
- Free-supplier inventory and coverage measurement task: seeds and quantifies the free-first path; owned by the data workstream with rights capture via DOS-M03-001.
- ACES/supplier document validator: schema/reference-version enforcement before transformation.
- Fitment adapter(s): per-supplier parsers emitting the FR-8 normalized application contract with `add/change/delete` handling.
- Part-number normalizer: shadow-key generation with brand-scoped identity.
- Conflict detector: cross-supplier disagreement reporting into QA.
### Data and persistence
- Raw supplier/ACES deliveries in the DOS-M03-003 landing zone with receipts and pinned versions; normalized staging validated against DOS-M03-002 schemas with full `_provenance`.
- Downstream mapping to section 8 `filter_brands`/`filter_products`/`filter_fitments` (stable brand/part number, configuration/provider application key, position/service type, qualifiers, source and effective status).
- Reference identities (VCdb et al., when licensed) enter `vehicle_external_ids` via DOS-M03-006; quarantine storage holds rejected records with reasons.
### Interfaces and events
- Input: authorized supplier exports/feeds, ACES application XML/approved JSON, PIES content when needed; all through the landing zone.
- Vehicle resolution: `external_vehicle_key` → DOS-M03-006 crosswalks; unresolved vehicles stay unpublished and reported.
- Output: publishable fitment staging plus coverage report distinguishing reference vehicle coverage, supplier application coverage, exact configuration coverage, and quarantined/unmatched rows; supplier/version manifest — consumed by DOS-M03-010's filter sections.
### Failure and recovery behavior
- Validation failures (reference-version mismatch, invalid XML, unknown qualifier, missing product, unresolved delete target, ambiguous vehicle mapping) quarantine with reasons; the run continues and reports.
- `delete` events with no matching current fitment are quarantined as unresolved delete targets, never silently ignored.
- Supplier conflicts route to QA with both assertions retained (DOS-M03-002 conflict policy).
- Rights termination for any source flips its flag; releases follow contracted continued-distribution terms; UI fallback is user-entered filter part numbers.
### Security and privacy
- Credentials/subscription codes are build-side only, referenced by environment-variable name, never in code, fixtures, manifests, bundle assets, or logs.
- Licensed reference databases and supplier payloads stay in access-controlled storage per their agreements; fixtures are synthetic or license-clean.
- No personal data is involved; no crawling of supplier web catalogs against their terms or robots/access rules.
### Accessibility and performance
N/A for accessibility — build-side adapters. Performance: fitment tables are among the largest catalog domains; measure row counts and payload contribution for DOS-M03-010 size budgets; shadow-key indexing must support tolerant offline part-number lookup without FTS dependence.
### Data change specifics
- Source/version/license: per-supplier and per-reference-DB rights via DOS-M03-001; pinned ACES schema version, VCdb/Qdb/PCdb/Brand Table/PAdb versions, and supplier delivery versions recorded in the supplier/version manifest and every receipt.
- Mapping: supplier/ACES records → FR-8 normalized applications (typed qualifiers with preserved source text) → section 8 filter tables; PIES attributes (when licensed) map to product content fields only.
- Stable keys: `supplier_application_key` from preserved supplier record IDs; product identity is brand-scoped part number per DOS-M03-002 stable-ID rules; shadow keys are search aids, never identity.
- Constraints/indexes: uniqueness on supplier application key + effective window; brand-scoped product uniqueness; indexes on `external_vehicle_key`, `filter_product_key`, and the normalized shadow key; publishable views restricted to exact/reviewed-qualified crosswalks.
- Idempotency: re-running against the same delivery SHA + transform version yields identical staging hashes; `add/change/delete` streams replay deterministically to the same end state.
- Rejected-row policy: FR-12 quarantine with reasons; quarantine counts appear in the coverage report; no qualifier is dropped to force a match.
- Checksums: delivery SHA-256 via receipts; staging output hashes per run; manifest reconciles record counts.
- Coverage report: reference vehicle coverage vs. supplier application coverage vs. exact configuration coverage vs. quarantined/unmatched rows (AC-4).
- Fixture strategy: golden fixtures for reference-version mismatch, invalid XML, unknown qualifier, missing product, delete event, and ambiguous vehicle mapping; synthetic supplier feeds for UI/testing; license-gated real samples only under evaluation terms.
- Update cadence: snapshot-pinned baseline; supplier update deliveries are new versioned imports with `add/change/delete` replay and diff review; cadence per contracted update terms.
- Rollback/recovery: versioned imports allow re-pinning a prior state; deletions never rewrite historical user snapshots; conflicts and quarantines are recoverable review artifacts, not data loss.

## UX and content states
No UI is built here, but the adapter fixes filter content states: only licensed configuration-specific applications may render as fitment ("Meets the recorded requirements" territory is the resolver's, and it requires these records); qualifiers always display with an application rather than being hidden; conflicting supplier assertions surface as `conflict`, never a merged "fits all"; unsupported configurations fall back to user-entered filter part numbers labeled `User entered`; historical service events keep their filter snapshots even after catalog deletions.

## Test plan
### Automated
- Schema-validation suite against pinned ACES/reference versions: the six AC-2 fixture classes (reference-version mismatch, invalid XML, unknown qualifier, missing product, delete event, ambiguous vehicle mapping) each behave as specified.
- Adapter golden fixtures: qualifier retention end-to-end (typed code + source text), position/quantity/effective-date preservation, `add/change/delete` replay determinism.
- Publication-gate tests: unresolved or ambiguous vehicle crosswalks cannot publish; base-vehicle-only breadth requires the explicit supplier assertion without disqualifying qualifiers.
- Conflict tests: contradictory supplier fixtures produce QA conflicts, not unions.
- Part-number tests: case/punctuation-tolerant lookup via shadow keys; property tests proving no cross-brand merge on shadow-key collision (AC-5).
- Quarantine accounting: rejected rows appear with reasons and reconcile with coverage-report counts.
### Manual/device matrix
- Sample crosswalk review: data owner audits a sample of published applications against supplier source records and qualifier sets.
- Rights verification: per-source flag states match the DOS-M03-001 matrix, including the separate Auto Care reference vs. supplier application approvals.
- Free-inventory review: product owner reviews the measured free-source coverage and the gap-bounded procurement scope.
- No device work: N/A — build-side adapters.

## Rollout and rollback
Per-supplier flags gate production; fitment ships only in catalog releases passing DOS-M03-010 gates with the supplier/version manifest. Updates are versioned imports replaying `add/change/delete` with diff review. Rollback is re-pinning the prior import or disabling a supplier (fallback to user-entered part numbers); historical user snapshots are never rewritten by catalog deletions.

## Dependency rationale
Depends on DOS-M03-001 (separate rights approvals for Auto Care reference data and each supplier's application/product data; free-first inventory mandate), DOS-M03-002 (fitment vocabulary, qualifier typing, conflict policy, stable IDs), and DOS-M03-003 (receipted, version-pinned landing). ## Risks, decisions, and open questions
- Risk: no supplier grants a suitable export/feed; planned outcome — production filter catalog disabled, user-entered part numbers, app still shippable.
- Risk: assuming VCdb subscription grants applications (a documented common error); excluded explicitly and enforced by separate rights rows.
- Risk: qualifier loss inflating apparent coverage; hard-blocked by FR-12, AC-3, and the no-qualifier-dropping exclusion.
- Risk: cross-brand part-number collisions merging distinct products; blocked by brand-scoped identity and AC-5 property tests.
- Decision: each supplier is an independent assertion; conflicts are QA states, never unions.
- Open question: which of WIX/Purolator/FRAM (or other first-party publishers) will provide exports or written permission; the inventory records the searched set and outcomes either way.
