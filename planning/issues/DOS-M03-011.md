## Outcome
A deep-research spike delivers a measured disposition on whether deterministic, reproducible automated extraction of the discrete factual values already authorized by `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` — viscosity grade, oil capacity, initial/repeat interval miles/months, severe-service alternative, oil-life-monitor presence, applicability qualifiers — from publicly-published OEM owner's manuals could later supplement the ADR-0007 lane (b) licensed corpus as the manual-verification arm of a hybrid lane (d). The card closes with either a proposed amendment to ADR-0007 opening lane (d) behind stated coverage and quality thresholds (paired with the required `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` line 22 amendment adding a vehicle-owner's-manual section), or a documented rejection with the measured evidence that led there; in no case does it ship extractor output into production data.

> **2026-08-02 — ADR-0007 posture.** ADR-0007 was accepted-in-part for lane **(b)** on 2026-08-02 and the DOS-M03-007 §FR-2 procurement spike is authorized. This card is a parallel research spike, not a substitute. It cannot promote lane (a) or lane (e) into a shipping sourcing lane; the only door it can open is the lane (d) revisit Q1 explicitly leaves ajar ("a subsequent ADR may revisit adding verification if the spike's coverage or extraction-quality evidence is thin"). Any change to the lane decision or to `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` still goes through the ADR channel. See [ADR-0007](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0007-manufacturer-oil-schedule-sourcing.md).

## Why this matters and what it unblocks
The 2026-08-02 lane (b) selection leaves the project with a single sourcing path for OEM-basis oil-service rows, and ADR-0007's own "Consequences" acknowledge lane (b)'s remaining open questions Q3 (coverage floor), Q4 (behavior for uncovered vehicles), Q5 (budget ceiling), and Q6 (rights-revocation behavior). This spike measures whether an independent, deterministic extraction path over publicly-published owner's manuals is technically and economically viable as the verification arm of a lane (d) hybrid, using the discrete-factual-values scope already green under `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md`. Favorable measurements unblock an ADR-0007 amendment proposal and a `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` amendment proposal; unfavorable measurements close the parallel path and confirm lane (b) as sole. It does not unblock DOS-M03-007 publication, does not weaken any DOS-M03-010 QA blocker, and is not on the DOS-M03-000 epic exit path.

## Context and source of truth
- Master prompt section 4.4: the documented-facts lane permits normalizing discrete facts from public OEM owner's manuals, maintenance booklets, and warranty/maintenance guides in original UI wording, with no vendored PDFs, prose, table layouts, photographs, or logos.
- `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` § "Oil-manufacturer publications — approved for independent factual extraction" (line 22): authorizes the analogous pattern for lubricant-manufacturer publications; it does **not** itself authorize extraction from vehicle owner's manuals, and its amendment to add an explicit vehicle-owner's-manual section is the policy prerequisite ADR-0007 names for any lane (a)-derived path.

- [ADR-0007](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0007-manufacturer-oil-schedule-sourcing.md) header: "Lane **(b)** — commercial license — is the chosen sourcing lane" and "Lanes **(a), (c), (d), (e)** remain not selected"; ADR-0007 Q1: "Lane (d)'s hybrid is not selected; a subsequent ADR may revisit adding verification if the spike's coverage or extraction-quality evidence is thin."
- ADR-0007 "Alternatives already considered" and lane (e) clause: "automated retrieval is prohibited" against OEM-owned surfaces (including owner-account portals) "on the same footing that ADR-0003's 'scrape publicly displayed OEM material' branch is ruled out"; DOS-M03-001 AC-10: "No issue in M03 uses `scrape` as an acquisition method unless a publisher has explicitly authorized it in writing."

## Included
- Selecting a stratified sample from the 33,273-configuration shipped corpus stratified by make, model year cohort (rolling 30 years), fuel type, and electrification, and characterizing per-stratum availability, format, and page-count of publicly-published owner's manuals.
- Measuring, for the sample, the fraction of manuals that are (a) publicly retrievable by a method already approved on the corresponding per-source `config/data_sources.yml` row, (b) machine-parseable enough for deterministic extraction, and (c) covered by a `review_status` in `approved` or `approved_with_conditions`.
- Building an extractor prototype in a spike branch that emits the discrete factual field set enumerated in `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` — viscosity grade, oil capacity (with context), initial/repeat interval miles/months, severe-service alternative, oil-life-monitor presence, applicability qualifiers — and nothing else.
- Measuring the deterministic-parse property (same manual PDF + same extractor version → byte-identical extracted row set) and, where OCR is required, the character-level accuracy on a labelled ground-truth subset before any field-level extraction is scored.
- Measuring false-negative ("did not find") and false-positive ("found the wrong value") rates against a hand-labelled ground truth for the sample, per field, per stratum.
- Running a timed second-reviewer pilot over at least ten configurations to measure minutes-per-configuration for the `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` § extraction lane, and projecting staff-days against a stated coverage floor for comparison with ADR-0007's lane (b) profile.
- Producing candidate per-manufacturer disposition rows in a scratch copy of `config/data_sources.yml` for DOS-M03-001 to review; the spike does not itself promote sources.
- Producing an amendment proposal for `docs/architecture/ADR-0007-manufacturer-oil-schedule-sourcing.md` opening lane (d) behind numeric thresholds, and an amendment proposal for `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` line 22 adding an explicit vehicle-owner's-manual section, OR a documented rejection with the measured evidence.

## Excluded
- Scraping OEM owner-account websites (MyFord/FordPass, Toyota Owners, MyChevrolet, MyBMW, etc.) or any other OEM-owned surface; ruled out by ADR-0007 lane (e) pending written OEM authorization and by DOS-M03-001 AC-10.
- Scraping publicly displayed OEM material without written authorization; ruled out by DOS-M03-001 AC-10 and ADR-0003's rejected branch.
- Redistributing full-text manuals, manual prose, source-specific table layouts, photographs, product-label artwork, or vendored PDFs; ruled out by `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` "Never copy" list.
- Using manufacturer or certification logos, including the API donut, starburst, or shield, or any trade dress; ruled out by `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` trademark restrictions.
- Nearest-match inference across trims, engines, or model families to pad coverage; ruled out by `RECOMMENDATION_CLAIMS_POLICY.md` and reiterated in ADR-0007's "What every lane must preserve."
- Collapsing normal-vs-severe schedules or converting oil-life-monitor policies to invented miles/months; ruled out by INV-21, the DDL `condition` CHECK, and DOS-M03-007 §Excluded.
- Crowd-sourced or user-contributed schedules; ruled out by ADR-0007 lane (c).
- Bypassing DOS-M03-001 for expediency; ingest of any spike-produced row is out of scope regardless of measurement outcome.
- Reopening the lane decision itself. This card can only propose an amendment to ADR-0007; it cannot promote lane (a) or lane (e) into a shipping sourcing lane by its own authority.
- Shipping extractor code to `main`, enabling any DOS-M03-007 adapter, contributing rows to any DOS-M03-010 build, or flipping any production feature flag.

Note preserved from the catalog: human-reviewed extraction of approved discrete factual values into original fields remains the DOS-M03-007 §FR-9 lane-(a) reference protocol; this card measures whether automation can front-run and shrink — but never replace — the second-reviewer step required there.

## Functional requirements
- FR-1: The spike measures the deterministic-parse property — same manual input plus same pinned extractor version yields byte-identical extracted rows on repeat runs — on every configuration in the stratified sample, and reports the failure count and root-cause categorization.
- FR-2: For image-based or partially-image-based PDFs, the spike measures OCR character-level accuracy against a labelled ground-truth subset before any field-level accuracy is scored; the report separates OCR error from downstream extraction error.
- FR-3: The spike measures per-field false-negative ("did not find") and false-positive ("found the wrong value") rates against a hand-labelled ground truth, for each field enumerated in `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` (viscosity grade, oil capacity, initial/repeat interval miles/months, severe-service alternative, oil-life-monitor presence, applicability qualifiers), per stratum.
- FR-4: The spike enumerates extraction failure modes as distinct categories with counts — "did not find," "found the wrong value," "OCR failure," "policy-prohibited output attempted and blocked," "structural parse failure" — rather than reporting only an aggregate accuracy figure.
- FR-5: The spike measures second-reviewer minutes-per-configuration on a timed pilot of at least ten configurations under the `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` § extraction lane and DOS-M03-007 §FR-9 protocol, and projects staff-days for a stated coverage floor against ADR-0007's lane (b) headcount profile.
- FR-6: Every experimental row emitted by the spike extractor carries the exact provenance fields DOS-M03-007 §FR-5 and §FR-6 require plus the `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` per-row provenance fields (`product_url` or `data_sheet_url`, `source_observed_at`, `source_type`, reviewer), plus extractor version and per-field confidence; rows missing any required field are quarantined, not emitted.
- FR-7: The spike measures, over the stratified sample of the 33,273 shipped configurations, the fraction of owner's manuals that are (a) publicly retrievable by a method already approved on the corresponding `config/data_sources.yml` row, (b) machine-parseable enough for deterministic extraction under FR-1, and (c) covered by a `review_status` in `approved` or `approved_with_conditions` per DOS-M03-001 FR-4 — three fractions reported separately, never conflated.
- FR-8: The spike emits zero rows into any production catalog surface (`vehicle_configurations`, `maintenance_schedules`, `oil_requirements`), the DOS-M03-007 ingest adapter, the DOS-M03-010 build, or any published `catalog-manifest.json`; all spike outputs are labeled synthetic-fixture and remain in the spike branch off `main`.
- FR-9: The spike honors the `RECOMMENDATION_CLAIMS_POLICY.md` prohibition on inferring a manufacturer interval from a generic model family or a nearest engine/configuration match; no coverage number in the report includes rows generated by generalizing across trims, engines, or families, and the extractor code has no code path that constructs such rows.
- FR-10: The spike honors INV-21 and the DDL `condition` CHECK constraint ∈ `normal`/`severe`/`flexible` — normal and severe are reported and measured separately, never collapsed — and oil-life-monitor schedules are reported as OLM policy rather than converted to invented miles/months, matching DOS-M03-007 FR-4.
- FR-11: The spike closes with per-candidate-manufacturer disposition entries in a scratch copy of `config/data_sources.yml` populating all six dimensions of the `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` source-disposition schema; `review_status` stays at `pending` unless DOS-M03-001 explicitly promoted the row, and the spike never sets `approved` or `approved_with_conditions` itself.
- FR-12: The spike closes with exactly one of two disposition outputs — (a) a proposed amendment PR against `docs/architecture/ADR-0007-manufacturer-oil-schedule-sourcing.md` opening lane (d) behind numeric coverage and quality thresholds derived from FR-3, FR-5, and FR-7 measurements, paired with a proposed amendment PR against `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` line 22 adding an explicit vehicle-owner's-manual section that mirrors the "Oil-manufacturer publications" row; or (b) a documented rejection recording the measured evidence and leaving lane (b) as the sole sourcing lane.

## Technical implementation contract
### Components and ownership boundaries
- Spike extractor prototype (owned by the data workstream, lives in a spike branch off `main`, never merged): manual-fetch adapter reading only from rights-approved retrieval methods; field-extraction pipeline emitting the `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` factual field set; per-run provenance packager; deterministic-parse harness.
- Ground-truth labelling workflow (owned by the domain reviewer): hand-labelled reference values for the sample used to score FR-3 and FR-4; annotations stored outside the compiled catalog.
- Timed second-reviewer pilot workflow (owned by the domain reviewer, framed to the DOS-M03-007 §FR-9 protocol): captures minutes-per-configuration for the FR-5 measurement.
- Scratch `config/data_sources.yml` fork for the FR-11 candidate rows; DOS-M03-001 owns the final matrix.
- ADR-0007 and `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` amendment PRs (owned by the ADR/policy workstream once measurements land).

### Data and persistence
- Retrieved manual copies land in the DOS-M03-003 landing zone with receipts, subject to the retrieval-method approval status on the corresponding `config/data_sources.yml` row.
- Extracted rows land in a spike-branch staging table validated against DOS-M03-002 schemas with full `_provenance` (including `field_path` and page/section locators); the table is labeled synthetic-fixture and excluded from any production build.
- Ground-truth annotations and per-run measurement CSVs live under `docs/research/DOS-M03-011/` in the spike branch; they are not compiled into any shipped catalog.
- No provider payload, no licensed corpus, and no OEM-authenticated-account content is retrieved or stored under this card.

### Interfaces and events
- Input: publicly-published owner's manual documents retrieved only via retrieval methods already approved on their per-source `config/data_sources.yml` row; the stratified-sample manifest referencing configurations by `external_vehicle_key` through DOS-M03-006 crosswalks.
- Output: the spike report at `docs/research/DOS-M03-011-owner-manual-extraction-spike.md`; the scratch `config/data_sources.yml` candidate rows; the FR-12 amendment PRs or the documented-rejection entry; the per-run measurement CSVs; per-configuration extracted-row JSON with full provenance.
- Gate signals: none. This card emits no publishable rows and flips no feature flag. Its output is read by DOS-M03-001 (for disposition rows) and by the ADR/policy workstream (for amendment PRs), and nothing else.

### Failure and recovery behavior
- Deterministic-parse failure (repeat runs disagree byte-for-byte for the same input and extractor version) is recorded and quarantined per FR-1 and root-cause-categorized; the failing rows do not appear in coverage numerators.
- OCR failure below the FR-2 character-level threshold marks the configuration OCR-unrecoverable; downstream field-level scores exclude it and the failure is tallied separately per FR-4.
- Provenance-missing rows are quarantined and never emitted, per FR-6.
- Any attempt by the extractor to construct a nearest-match row, a normal/severe collapse, an OLM-to-miles conversion, or verbatim manual prose is a hard error and blocks the row from emission, per FR-9 and FR-10.
- Rights or acquisition-method changes on a `config/data_sources.yml` row mid-spike stop retrieval from that source; already-retrieved copies are re-evaluated against the new disposition and discarded if the new status forbids retention.

### Security and privacy
- Retrieved manuals are held only in the access-controlled DOS-M03-003 landing zone; no manual PDFs, prose, images, or logos are committed to Git, published in the report, or included in fixtures.
- The report cites at most short factual field values and page/section locators (per `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` provenance rules) and reproduces no source prose, images, logos, or trade dress.
- No personal data is involved; reviewers are identified by role/identity per team policy, not more.
- No provider payloads, credentials, contract documents, or licensed corpora appear anywhere in the spike; this card does not touch the lane (b) MOTOR spike's materials.

### Accessibility and performance
N/A for accessibility — build-side spike. Performance: extractor throughput is secondary to correctness and deterministic-parse; runtime measurements are recorded for the FR-5 staff-days projection but no runtime budget is defended.

### Data change specifics
- Source/version/license: each retrieval binds to a `source_key` on the scratch `config/data_sources.yml`; retrieval only proceeds where an existing DOS-M03-001 disposition permits the method; every manual copy is pinned to its published revision in provenance; the six-dimension source disposition schema (`copyright_basis`, `acquisition_basis`, `redistribution_basis`, `trademark_posture`, `claim_posture`, `review_status`) is populated for each candidate row.
- Mapping: manual factual values → `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` factual field set → DOS-M03-007 §FR-5 and §FR-6 shapes; the extractor emits into DOS-M03-002 vocabulary and never invents fields.
- Stable keys: `external_schedule_key` and `external_requirement_key` derived from documented natural keys per DOS-M03-002; `external_vehicle_key` linkage via DOS-M03-006 crosswalks — the spike does not fabricate keys.
- Constraints/indexes: DOS-M03-002 positivity, miles-or-months, and DDL `condition` CHECK constraints apply to extracted rows in the spike-branch staging table; rows violating any check quarantine.
- Idempotency: re-running the extractor over the same manual SHA-256 and pinned extractor version yields byte-identical extracted rows; deviation is a recorded FR-1 failure.
- Rejected-row policy: schema, positivity, provenance, "Never copy," nearest-match, normal/severe-collapse, and OLM-conversion violations all quarantine with a categorized reason per FR-4; nothing silently dropped.
- Checksums: raw manual SHA-256 recorded via the DOS-M03-003 landing-zone receipts; extractor-version hash pinned per run; extracted-row-set hash recorded per run for the FR-1 comparison.
- Coverage report: FR-7's three separate fractions (retrievable, machine-parseable, rights-covered) reported per stratum and in aggregate, alongside FR-3 per-field FN/FP rates and FR-5 minutes-per-configuration.
- Fixture strategy: all spike output is synthetic-fixture labeled and excluded from production builds; DOS-M03-010 must not accept spike-branch fixtures into any released `catalog-manifest.json`.
- Update cadence: single-run spike; no scheduled refresh; if a manual revision changes mid-spike, the spike re-pins and re-runs the affected configuration and records both runs.
- Rollback/recovery: the spike branch is discarded on rejection; on acceptance-in-principle, the amendment PRs proceed through their own review and no spike code is merged to `main` — subsequent implementation lives in a future card.

- Risk: the stratified sample is too small to distinguish "extraction is deterministic but the corpus is uncoverable" from "the corpus is coverable but the extractor is nondeterministic"; mitigated by FR-2/FR-3/FR-7 being scored on separate axes with per-stratum breakdowns, per FR-4.
- Risk: OCR quality on scanned older manuals dominates the FR-3 failure rate and masks true extraction quality; mitigated by FR-2 measuring OCR separately before field-level scoring.
- Risk: retrieval methods approved for a manufacturer today are revised or withdrawn during the spike, invalidating already-retrieved copies; mitigated by the Security and privacy re-evaluation rule and by pinning `source_observed_at` on every provenance row.
- Risk: measurable results tempt the team to ship rows before the `FACTUAL_USE_AND_MARKS_POLICY.md` line 22 amendment lands; mitigated by FR-8 (zero-emission constraint) and by AC-3 (build-test guard).
- Risk: reviewers under-count second-review minutes on the timed pilot because the pilot is small; mitigated by FR-5 requiring at least ten configurations and by declaring the staff-days projection as a projection, not a commitment.
- Decision: the card is a spike and closes with a disposition (amendment or rejection), not with shipped adapters or production rows.
- Decision: the card cannot alter the ADR-0007 lane decision by itself; the only decision output it can produce is a proposed amendment PR that goes through the ADR channel.
- Decision: OEM owner-account portals and OEM-owned web surfaces remain out of scope; ADR-0007 lane (e) still governs and no retrieval attempt against those surfaces is authorized here.
- Open question: what per-field FN/FP rate is the minimum threshold ADR-0007's amendment should require before a hybrid lane (d) is opened? Answered by the FR-3 measurement and the FR-12 amendment PR wording, not preset here.
- Open question: what coverage floor (FR-7 (c) fraction) should the amendment name? Answered by the FR-7 measurement and the FR-12 amendment PR wording, in coordination with ADR-0007's own Q3.
- Open question: whether any of the FR-11 candidate rows will pass DOS-M03-001 promotion to `approved` or `approved_with_conditions` on their own merits; the spike proposes, DOS-M03-001 decides.
