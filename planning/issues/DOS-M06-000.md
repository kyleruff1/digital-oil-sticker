## Outcome

Digital Oil Sticker converts the selected interval, service history, manually sampled odometer history, and an optional declared typical-driving baseline into a deterministic, explainable estimate. It identifies the earlier of the time and projected-mileage thresholds and schedules private, on-device reminders through `mob_notify`; it never requires a user account, remote job, APNs/FCM push service, or a running/backgrounded BEAM process.

This epic tracks milestone M06 ("M06 — Forecasting and local reminders") to its exit gate: `DOS-M06-001` through `DOS-M06-007` are closed; every forecast is reproducible from saved inputs and algorithm version; confidence/outlier handling is visible; a user can enable, inspect, change, disable, and test reminders; scheduled notifications fire on physical iOS and Android devices with the app foregrounded, backgrounded, and killed; launch/resume and date/time/time-zone changes converge the OS schedule to the local desired plan.

**Epic safety statement (binding on all children):** The estimate is a reminder aid, not a diagnostic, warranty, lubricant-compatibility determination, or substitute for the owner's manual or in-vehicle oil-life monitor (OLM). If an OLM or manual calls for earlier service, that earlier instruction wins. Never convert an OLM percentage into miles or days without a sourced manufacturer algorithm for that exact vehicle.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

M06 delivers the product's core promise: the digital sticker becomes an active reminder instead of a passive record. The non-negotiable product rules require that a plan is due at the earlier of its sourced calendar threshold and projected mileage threshold (rule 6: "Earlier threshold wins"), that the app never claims to know the current odometer (rule 3: "No telematics"), and that v1 uses operating-system scheduled local notifications with no APNs/FCM push infrastructure, device tokens, notification server, or accounts (rule 8: "Local notifications only in v1"). This milestone is where those rules become verified behavior.

M06 is the last feature milestone on the MVP critical path. It unblocks M07 ("Hardening and release") — specifically the release-gate issues `DOS-M07-004`, `DOS-M07-006`, and `DOS-M07-007` that its children block — and it establishes the forecast policy versioning and reminder-capacity foundations that `DOS-M08-003` and `DOS-M08-004` extend post-MVP. Without a closed M06 gate, beta cannot begin, because notifications amplify the forecast and an unexplained or unreliable forecast would violate the product's explainability contract.

## Excluded

- User accounts, remote databases, cloud sync, or any authentication (non-negotiable rule 1).
- Remote jobs, remote computation, or server-side forecasting of any kind.
- APNs/FCM push infrastructure, `MobNotify.register_push`, `mob_push`, device tokens, or a notification server (non-negotiable rule 8).
- Any requirement for a running or backgrounded BEAM process for reminders to fire.
- Machine learning, location/GPS, telematics, weather/road-condition inference, and automatic OLM interpretation (excluded by `DOS-M06-001`).
- GPS/background tracking, calendar scanning, fuel data, automatic car sync, and claims that driving style can be diagnosed from mileage alone (excluded by `DOS-M06-002`).
- Email/SMS delivery, exact-to-the-minute delivery guarantees, and exact alarms (excluded by `DOS-M06-005`).
- Background polling, an Android foreground service, or a promise that JavaScript/BEAM code runs while killed (excluded by `DOS-M06-006`).
- Claims of guaranteed delivery, a server delivery receipt, marketing prompts, and remote observability (excluded by `DOS-M06-007`).
- Multi-vehicle reminder allocation across notification slots (deferred to M08 per `DOS-M06-006`; internals must remain multi-vehicle-safe per rule 9).
- Converting an OLM percentage into miles or days without a sourced manufacturer algorithm for that exact vehicle.

## Dependency rationale

Position in the M00→M08 DAG (master prompt §12): M05 → M06 → M07. M06 consumes the single-vehicle MVP surfaces built in M05 — recorded service events, odometer readings and their constraint/discontinuity rules, the dashboard, and settings — and cannot produce a meaningful forecast or reminder plan before they exist. M06's own exit gate feeds M07 ("Hardening and release"): `DOS-M06-006` and `DOS-M06-007` block `DOS-M07-004` and `DOS-M07-007`, and `DOS-M06-007` also blocks `DOS-M07-006`. Post-MVP expansion depends on M06's frozen policy and capacity foundations: `DOS-M06-001`/`DOS-M06-003` block `DOS-M08-003`, and `DOS-M06-005`/`DOS-M06-006` block `DOS-M08-004`. The epic itself carries no `blockedBy` entries; the epic-level DAG is encoded by the roadmap assembler.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- Risk: `mob_notify`/Mob 0.7.20 compatibility or Android reboot-rescheduling gaps could surface late; `DOS-M06-005` pins and verifies the plugin and `DOS-M06-006` explicitly tests reboot/package replacement, with a documented native remediation or release blocker as the fallback.
- Risk: OS delivery is best-effort and may be delayed by power-saving modes; the product guarantees correct scheduling intent and reconciliation, not exact display time (`DOS-M06-007`), and the in-app due state never depends on notification delivery.
- Risk: forecast ambiguity leaking into UI code; mitigated by the write-the-policy-first ordering (`DOS-M06-001` blocks all implementation children).
- Decision (made, binding): deterministic explainable arithmetic, not machine learning; local notifications only; no background BEAM dependency.
- Open question: none at epic level; child-level open questions are tracked in each child issue.
