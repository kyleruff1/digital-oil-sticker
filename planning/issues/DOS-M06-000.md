> **RESCOPED FOR BROWSER MVP — 2026-08-01.** [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) established the browser-first MVP. This card is retained with the same identity; its Outcome and FRs below are the rescoped spec. The pre-pivot spec is preserved at the bottom under "Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)". Disposition per [PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md](../../docs/governance/PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md#issue-by-issue-classification): _The forecasting half survives intact; the notification half narrows to in-app due/overdue state, with OS delivery moved to the native re-entry and Web Push to a spike._

## Outcome

Digital Oil Sticker converts the selected interval, service history, manually sampled odometer history, and an optional declared typical-driving baseline into a deterministic, explainable forecast, and surfaces the earlier of the time and projected-mileage thresholds as **in-app due/overdue state** rendered on every LiveView mount and after every acknowledged mutation. Reminder intent — whether the user wants to be nudged, and the lead time — persists in the origin-scoped IndexedDB `reminders` store defined by [ADR-0004 §"Storage layout"](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md#storage-layout) alongside `vehicles`, `events`, `readings`, and `prefs`, versioned by `meta.schema_version` with forward-only client-side migrations. The server persists no personal rows and holds hydrated state only in `socket.assigns` for the LiveView connection lifetime.

Per [CONSTITUTION.md](../../docs/product/CONSTITUTION.md) revision 2.0.0 §6 INV-17 (amended), OS-scheduled local notifications are unavailable in a browser and are no longer part of the MVP; the MVP delivers due/overdue state in the app only, and INV-25 copy discipline forbids any surface that would imply otherwise. OS-delivered scheduling (`mob_notify` and reconciliation across DST, lifecycle, and killed-app states) is deferred to the native re-entry recorded in [ADR-0004 §"Trigger conditions that revive the native track"](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md#trigger-conditions-that-revive-the-native-track). Web Push is deferred to the go/no-go spike `DOS-M09-010`, with its remote-infrastructure and third-party-endpoint conflicts explicitly recorded rather than resolved by ticket.

This epic tracks milestone M06 to its browser-first exit gate: the six in-scope children (`DOS-M06-001`, `DOS-M06-002`, `DOS-M06-003`, `DOS-M06-004`, `DOS-M06-005`, `DOS-M06-007`) are closed under their own rescoped or surviving dispositions from PIVOT-2026-08-01; every forecast is reproducible from saved inputs and algorithm version; confidence and outlier handling are visible; a user can enable, inspect, change, disable, and test in-app reminder preferences; due/overdue state is visible on every mount and recomputes on the INV-24.1 hydration round trip and after every INV-24.2 mutation acknowledgement; and no `mob_notify` binding, push subscription, VAPID key, device token, third-party delivery service, or backgrounded process is present anywhere in the milestone.

**Epic safety statement (binding on all children):** The estimate is a reminder aid, not a diagnostic, warranty, lubricant-compatibility determination, or substitute for the owner's manual or in-vehicle oil-life monitor (OLM). If an OLM or manual calls for earlier service, that earlier instruction wins. Never convert an OLM percentage into miles or days without a sourced manufacturer algorithm for that exact vehicle.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

M06 delivers the product's core promise for the browser MVP: the digital sticker becomes an active due/overdue indicator instead of a passive record. The non-negotiable product rules survive verbatim — a plan is due at the earlier of its sourced calendar threshold and projected mileage threshold (rule 6: "Earlier threshold wins"), the app never claims to know the current odometer (rule 3: "No telematics"), and no accounts, remote push infrastructure, device tokens, or third-party delivery services enter the picture. What changed is *how the user learns* the plan is due: in the browser MVP they learn by opening the app, and every surface says so plainly (INV-25, honest storage- and delivery-loss messaging).

M06 remains on the MVP critical path. Under the pivot it consumes:

- **M05** (rescoped single-vehicle browser MVP) for the recorded events, odometer readings, and dashboard surfaces on which the forecast is drawn.
- **M09** (hosted browser platform and anonymous client storage — the new milestone proposed in PIVOT-2026-08-01) for the IndexedDB schema (`DOS-M09-001`), storage adapter (`DOS-M09-002`), hydration protocol (`DOS-M09-003`), and impersonal read-only server-side catalog query layer (`DOS-M09-005`).
- **M04** (rescoped) for the browser `UserStore` transactional semantics on which `reminders` writes rely (`DOS-M04-005`).

It unblocks **M07** (rescoped hardening and public web launch) — specifically the rescoped `DOS-M07-004` (browser/engine QA replacing the killed-app matrix), `DOS-M07-006` (invite-linked beta over hosted staging), and `DOS-M07-007` (staged Fly deploy and custom-domain cutover) — and it establishes the forecast-policy versioning and reminder-preference foundations that `DOS-M08-003` extends post-MVP. `DOS-M08-004` (per-vehicle fairness within OS pending-notification limits) is DEFER per PIVOT-2026-08-01 and no longer chains from this epic. Without a closed M06 gate, beta cannot begin, because an unexplained forecast would violate the product's explainability contract even in a browser.

## Excluded

- User accounts, remote databases, cloud sync, or any authentication (non-negotiable rule 1; INV-3 preserved).
- Remote jobs, remote computation, or server-side forecasting of any kind.
- **`mob_notify`, `MobNotify.register_push`, `mob_push`, APNs/FCM push infrastructure, device tokens, or a notification server** — none exist in the browser MVP (INV-17 amended). Any use of the `mob_notify` symbol is a release blocker.
- **Web Push, service workers used for push, VAPID keys, browser-vendor push endpoints, and server-held push subscriptions** — explicitly deferred to the go/no-go spike `DOS-M09-010` (INV-19 amended). No MVP copy may imply that anything arrives while the app is closed (INV-25).
- Any requirement for a running or backgrounded process — Elixir, JavaScript, or otherwise — to compute or deliver due state.
- Reconciliation of OS-scheduled notification requests across DST, lifecycle, boot receivers, or killed-app states (`DOS-M06-006`, DEFER).
- Any server-side personal row. `usage_profiles`, `odometer_readings`, `forecast_snapshots`, `reminder_rules`, and `scheduled_notifications` **do not exist as server-side Ecto tables** in the browser MVP (ADR-0004 §Decision 4). Forecast snapshots are computed on demand from IndexedDB-hydrated inputs and held only in `socket.assigns`; `scheduled_notifications` has no MVP home at all.
- The typed `DeviceCommandBroker` / `Mob.Screen` / `Mob.Socket` bridge path from master prompt §4.1/§7 — the browser MVP has no on-device bridge (ADR-0004 §Decision 1).
- Machine learning, location/GPS, telematics, weather/road-condition inference, and automatic OLM interpretation (excluded by `DOS-M06-001`).
- Email/SMS delivery and any exact-delivery-time guarantee.
- Multi-vehicle reminder allocation across notification slots (deferred to `DOS-M08-004`, DEFER; internals must remain multi-vehicle-safe per rule 9).
- Converting an OLM percentage into miles or days without a sourced manufacturer algorithm for that exact vehicle.

## Technical implementation contract

### Components and ownership boundaries

N/A — epic-level tracking issue. Browser-first component ownership per child: forecast policy and pure engine in `DOS-M06-001` / `DOS-M06-003` (`DigitalOilSticker.Forecast` — plain Elixir, no `Phoenix.LiveView` dependency, per ADR-0004 §Decision 6 and its static-check enforcement); capture and presentation LiveViews in `DOS-M06-002` / `DOS-M06-004`; desired-reminder-plan pure function and in-app rendering in `DOS-M06-005`; in-app control center in `DOS-M06-007`. All personal-data writes flow through the `DOS-M09-002` browser storage adapter and the `DOS-M09-003` hydration/mutation protocol; there is no `DeviceCommandBroker`, `Mob.Screen`, or `Mob.Socket` in the MVP deployment.

### Data and persistence

N/A — epic-level tracking issue. The milestone's persistence surface is the [ADR-0004 §"Storage layout"](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md#storage-layout) IndexedDB layout (`meta`, `vehicles`, `events`, `readings`, `reminders`, `prefs`) written through the storage adapter and versioned by `meta.schema_version` with forward-only client-side migrations owned by `DOS-M09-001`. Forecast snapshots are computed on demand and held only in `socket.assigns` (ADR-0004 §Decision 4); they are never persisted server-side. The pre-pivot `UserRepo` tables named in master prompt §8 (`usage_profiles`, `odometer_readings`, `forecast_snapshots`, `reminder_rules`, `scheduled_notifications`) are not the M06 surface in the browser MVP; `scheduled_notifications` has no MVP home at all.

### Interfaces and events

N/A — epic-level tracking issue. The binding cross-child interfaces are the pure `forecast/2` contract (`DOS-M06-003`), the in-process `forecast_changed(vehicle_id, revision)` signal that triggers a due-state re-render in the LiveView, and the INV-24.2 `local_store:put` / `local_store:ack` envelope carrying reminder-intent upserts (per ADR-0004 §"Payload envelope"). There are no typed `DeviceCommandBroker` command envelopes in the MVP; any child that needs a boundary between the pure engine and the LiveView expresses it as a plain Elixir function contract.

### Failure and recovery behavior

N/A — epic-level tracking issue. Children define forecast snapshot recomputation on stale input (`DOS-M06-003`); the failed-write path per ADR-0004 §"Mutation round trip" for reminder-intent writes that time out or fail — the optimistic value stays visible but visibly marked "Not saved to this browser", the user is told the truth, and export is offered (`DOS-M06-005` / `DOS-M06-007`); and INV-24.3 through INV-24.8 handling for pre-hydration, storage-denied, quota-exhausted, eviction, cleared-storage, and multi-tab conflict states.

### Security and privacy

Epic-level constraint: no push tokens, no push subscriptions, no third-party delivery endpoints, no server-side reminder rows, no telemetry. In-app rendered reminder text defaults to generic wording and never includes exact mileage, notes, VIN, oil/filter purchase history, or catalog IDs (this constraint survives verbatim from the pre-pivot spec: rendered strings pass through Phoenix templates and could reach a crash reporter if one were ever added, which ADR-0004 §"Third-party error reporting is specifically prohibited" forbids). All hydration and mutation payloads containing reminder data are excluded from server logs by the LiveView `handle_event` filter allowlist and the `DOS-M09-008` log-redaction test.

### Accessibility and performance

N/A — epic-level tracking issue. `DOS-M06-004` carries the WCAG 2.2 AA contract for forecast presentation, verified against the browser assistive-technology matrix in `DOS-M09-009` / rescoped `DOS-M07-002`. `DOS-M06-007` carries the reminder-preference accessibility audit for browser AT (keyboard-only path included, which the pre-pivot mobile bar did not cover). `DOS-M06-003`'s worst-supported-history benchmark is measured against the re-ratified CONSTITUTION §9 page-load / time-to-hydrated / LiveView round-trip / client-storage-footprint budgets rather than the retired on-device budgets.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- **Risk (new, browser-first, primary):** Users may not accept a product that never notifies them. In-app due state is a materially weaker delivery model than OS-scheduled local notifications, and this is the "core product bet" of the pivot per ADR-0004 §Risks R11. Mitigation: INV-25 honest copy so the deficit is stated rather than disguised; `DOS-M09-010` PWA/Web Push spike as the escalation path; native re-entry (ADR-0004 §"Trigger conditions that revive the native track") as the ultimate escalation.
- **Risk (browser eviction):** IndexedDB eviction of infrequently-visited sites (ADR-0004 §Risks R1) disproportionately affects the quarterly usage cadence typical of oil reminders. Mitigation: `navigator.storage.persist()` request in `DOS-M09-002`, the `:data_missing` state and export nudges, and the honest storage-status UI. Reminders are among the smallest records so this is not a quota risk — it is an eviction risk.
- **Risk (hydration flash):** A user with an existing garage seeing a "no reminders yet" empty state during hydration would read it as data loss. Mitigation: FR-13 and INV-24.3 forbid it structurally; enforcement is in the `DOS-M09-003` conformance suite.
- **Risk (forecast ambiguity leaking into UI):** mitigated by write-the-policy-first ordering (`DOS-M06-001` blocks all implementation children); survives the pivot unchanged.
- **Risk (retired 2026-08-01):** The pre-pivot risk on `mob_notify` / Mob 0.7.20 compatibility and Android reboot-rescheduling gaps is removed — `mob_notify` is out of MVP scope (INV-17 amended). Preserved verbatim in the historical spec for the native re-entry.
- **Decision (made, binding, browser-first):** deterministic explainable arithmetic, not machine learning; in-app due/overdue state only; no OS notifications; no push; no server-side personal record; no background process of any kind.
- **Open question (referred to M09):** whether measured browser eviction (`DOS-M09-009`) and R11 user response justify accelerating `DOS-M09-010` (PWA + Web Push go/no-go) inside the beta window. Not resolved at M06 close; recorded for M07/M09.

## Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)

### Outcome (pre-pivot)

Digital Oil Sticker converts the selected interval, service history, manually sampled odometer history, and an optional declared typical-driving baseline into a deterministic, explainable estimate. It identifies the earlier of the time and projected-mileage thresholds and schedules private, on-device reminders through `mob_notify`; it never requires a user account, remote job, APNs/FCM push service, or a running/backgrounded BEAM process.

This epic tracks milestone M06 ("M06 — Forecasting and local reminders") to its exit gate: `DOS-M06-001` through `DOS-M06-007` are closed; every forecast is reproducible from saved inputs and algorithm version; confidence/outlier handling is visible; a user can enable, inspect, change, disable, and test reminders; scheduled notifications fire on physical iOS and Android devices with the app foregrounded, backgrounded, and killed; launch/resume and date/time/time-zone changes converge the OS schedule to the local desired plan.

**Epic safety statement (binding on all children):** The estimate is a reminder aid, not a diagnostic, warranty, lubricant-compatibility determination, or substitute for the owner's manual or in-vehicle oil-life monitor (OLM). If an OLM or manual calls for earlier service, that earlier instruction wins. Never convert an OLM percentage into miles or days without a sourced manufacturer algorithm for that exact vehicle.

### Why this matters and what it unblocks (pre-pivot)

M06 delivers the product's core promise: the digital sticker becomes an active reminder instead of a passive record. The non-negotiable product rules require that a plan is due at the earlier of its sourced calendar threshold and projected mileage threshold (rule 6: "Earlier threshold wins"), that the app never claims to know the current odometer (rule 3: "No telematics"), and that v1 uses operating-system scheduled local notifications with no APNs/FCM push infrastructure, device tokens, notification server, or accounts (rule 8: "Local notifications only in v1"). This milestone is where those rules become verified behavior.

M06 is the last feature milestone on the MVP critical path. It unblocks M07 ("Hardening and release") — specifically the release-gate issues `DOS-M07-004`, `DOS-M07-006`, and `DOS-M07-007` that its children block — and it establishes the forecast policy versioning and reminder-capacity foundations that `DOS-M08-003` and `DOS-M08-004` extend post-MVP. Without a closed M06 gate, beta cannot begin, because notifications amplify the forecast and an unexplained or unreliable forecast would violate the product's explainability contract.
