> **RESCOPED FOR BROWSER MVP — 2026-08-01.** [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) established the browser-first MVP. This card is retained with the same identity; its Outcome and FRs below are the rescoped spec. The pre-pivot spec is preserved at the bottom under "Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)". Disposition per [PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md](../../docs/governance/PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md#issue-by-issue-classification): _The "recurring" prompt has no background scheduler in a browser, so recurring sampling must be respecified as an on-visit prompt driven by elapsed time since the last sample._

## Outcome

In the hosted browser MVP, users can supply an initial weekly-driving estimate and add accurate odometer samples through Phoenix LiveView capture flows whose only durable store is the user's own IndexedDB. Capture explains why the app asks, previews the effect of each sample, and reports eligibility exactly as the frozen `DOS-M06-001` policy dictates. The "recurring reading" nudge is respecified as an **on-visit elapsed-time check** evaluated at mount/hydration — there is no background scheduler, no timer, no OS notification, and no server-side personal record of any usage profile or odometer reading. Every capture mutation is a `pushEvent` → server-compute → `local_store:put` → hook-acknowledge round trip against the hydration protocol ratified by [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) §"The client-storage + LiveView hydration protocol"; the capture surface honestly renders `:hydrating`, `:empty`, `:data_missing`, `:storage_unavailable`, and reconnect states rather than showing stale or fabricated values.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

The forecast engine (`DOS-M06-003`, SURVIVES) still needs two inputs — a declared weekly baseline and eligible odometer observations — to have anything to work with. In the browser MVP those inputs live in the user's IndexedDB and reach the server only as transient values in `socket.assigns` for the socket's lifetime (ADR-0004 decision §4). If capture is written as if a `UserRepo` still existed, or as if a browser process could run a background timer between visits, the resulting system either creates a server-side personal record (breaking INV-3 as preserved by ADR-0004) or is built against a browser capability that does not exist (silently no-ops, giving users an absent "reminder" that never fires).

This card supplies the browser-first capture paths the surviving downstream cards consume: `DOS-M06-003` (pure engine recomputation) needs realistic hydrated inputs to recompute against, `DOS-M06-004` (present forecast/confidence/warnings, SURVIVES) needs the capture states to render, and `DOS-M06-005` (rescoped: desired-reminder plan rendered as in-app due/overdue state, with OS delivery deferred to the native re-entry per pivot) consumes the same inputs. It also anchors the hydration/mutation contract for a whole class of user records (usage profiles and odometer readings) inside the schema owned by `DOS-M09-001` and the adapter built by `DOS-M09-002`, driven through the round trip specified by `DOS-M09-003`.

## Included

- Declared weekly-distance capture rendered in a LiveView step (post vehicle/last-service save) with direct weekly entry plus the workdays × round-trip and per-day/per-month helpers, showing the computed weekly value before commit.
- Odometer add/edit/delete in the LiveView surface (in/alongside `MileageLive` per §7), with preview of preceding and following readings and the resulting delta, defaulting to today's local date but allowing a past nonfuture date.
- Sample-quality feedback after save mapping engine reason codes to plain language: `eligible`, `too_close`, `stale_gap`, `other_segment`, `outlier`.
- **On-visit sampling suggestion** driven purely by elapsed time computed at mount/hydration against the most-recent-eligible reading in the hydrated payload (FR-5). No scheduler, no timer, no queued job.
- Client-side persistence of `usage_profiles` and `odometer_readings` in IndexedDB via the `DOS-M09-002` adapter, using the object-store layout defined by `DOS-M09-001`.
- Mutation round trip end-to-end for every capture write: `pushEvent` → server compute in assigns → `local_store:put` → hook `readwrite` transaction with compare-and-set on `meta.seq` → `local_store:ack` → server clears pending write and renders saved state.
- Honest state rendering for every capture surface: `:hydrating` skeleton (never a fake empty garage), `:empty`, `:data_missing` (boot hint said `has_data` but hydration returned zero), `:storage_unavailable` (IndexedDB refused), session-only banner when the fallback ladder collapsed to socket-lifetime state, and `phx-disconnected` styling on reconnect.
- Multi-tab conflict handling for capture writes via the `BroadcastChannel("dos_local_store")` and the compare-and-set on `meta.seq`; a losing tab surfaces the conflict rather than silently discarding the user's edit.
- Reconnect resilience: an in-flight capture at the moment a socket drops is re-driven from IndexedDB on reconnect (client is source of truth) or, if not yet committed to storage, is surfaced as a "not saved to this browser" state per ADR-0004 §"Mutation round trip".
- INV-7 honest-degradation copy on the capture surfaces: catalog-query failure (from `DOS-M09-005`) is rendered as such, socket-down disables saves rather than pretending to persist, and quota-exceeded on a reading write yields a specific message plus export.

## Excluded

- Any background scheduler, timer, worker, cron, or `setInterval` that fires between visits — the browser has no such capability under this MVP and pretending it does creates a false promise. (Retires the pre-pivot FR-5 timer framing outright.)
- OS notifications of any kind. `mob_notify` is a native binding that does not exist in a browser; the notification half of `DOS-M06-005` is rescoped away from this card and any residual native delivery is deferred to the native re-entry.
- Web Push. It requires a service worker, a VAPID keypair, a browser-vendor push service, and a server-held per-device subscription — the last of which conflicts with ADR-0004 decision §4. Web Push is tracked as an explicit go/no-go spike at `DOS-M09-010`; it is neither planned nor implied by this card.
- Any service-worker or PWA offline behavior. The MVP requires connectivity (INV-7 as amended); a PWA is a separate deferred decision.
- Any server-side `UserRepo`, ETS table, cache, or log line that would durably hold a usage profile or odometer reading — prohibited by ADR-0004 decision §4 and by the enforcement tests in ADR-0004 §"Enforcement" (AC-5, AC-8 below).
- GPS/background tracking, calendar scanning, fuel data, automatic car sync, and any claim that driving style can be diagnosed from mileage alone — carried over unchanged.
- Forecast arithmetic itself (owned by `DOS-M06-001`/`DOS-M06-003`).

## Functional requirements

- **FR-1 (declared usage capture).** After the vehicle and last-service records are hydrated into assigns, the LiveView renders a typical-usage step offering direct weekly distance, a workdays × round-trip helper, a per-day helper, a per-month helper, and an explicit "I don't know / skip". The computed weekly value is shown before commit. Only the final canonical weekly value (integer base units), the input unit, and an enumerated `input_method` are persisted; helper sub-inputs are discarded after computation. Persistence is a mutation round trip against the `vehicles` or `prefs` store as defined by `DOS-M09-001`; the server never durably holds the value.
- **FR-2 (expectation setting).** The capture surface explains that two readings at least seven days apart begin to personalize the estimate and that roughly six weeks of recent evidence can replace the declared baseline, under `DOS-M06-001`. It never promises a particular confidence level before data qualifies, and it never claims data will be retained across browser storage clearing (INV-5 as strengthened by ADR-0004).
- **FR-3 (reading entry).** "Add current odometer" defaults to today's local date and accepts a past nonfuture date. Preceding and following readings and the resulting delta are shown before commit. Constraints and discontinuity behavior are reused from `DOS-M05-004`; validation runs client-side in the adapter and is re-executed server-side on the hydrate/mutate path (there are no IndexedDB constraints to rely on for foreign-key or check semantics).
- **FR-4 (sample-quality feedback).** After the mutation round trip acknowledges, the surface displays the engine's classification for the saved reading — `eligible`, `too_close`, `stale_gap`, `other_segment`, or `outlier` — with plain-language copy. "Outlier" is stated as "unusual compared with recent samples," not "wrong"; the user may correct/delete the source but cannot force a result to high confidence.
- **FR-5 (on-visit sampling suggestion — REPLACES the pre-pivot recurring-reminder FR-5, addressing sweep finding 1).** On any LiveView `mount`/hydration where (a) `assigns.local_state == :loaded`, (b) the current forecast confidence for the vehicle is `low` or `medium` per `DOS-M06-001`, and (c) the most recent eligible odometer reading in the hydrated payload is at least 7 days old (or none exists), the surface renders a non-blocking in-app suggestion to take a new reading. The check is a pure function of hydrated assigns evaluated at mount. **There is no scheduler, no timer, no queued job, no `setInterval`, no `setTimeout`, no OS notification path, and no Web Push. The suggestion is dismissible for the current visit; dismissal is stored ephemerally in `socket.assigns` and dies with the socket** (dismissal is not persisted to IndexedDB, because a persisted dismissal would be a durable behavioral record without user value). The 7–14+ day window described by the pivot's finding-1 language is the eligible-suggestion window: once the elapsed time exceeds 14 days the surface may escalate copy urgency, but it remains an on-visit render. Constitution 2.0.0 INV-17 (as amended by ADR-0004) is respected structurally: the browser cannot fire an OS notification and this FR does not attempt one.
- **FR-6 (atomic invalidation via the mutation round trip).** Every input change to a usage profile or odometer reading commits through `pushEvent` → server compute in assigns → `local_store:put` with `{mutation_id, seq, upserts, deletes}` → hook `readwrite` transaction across the affected stores plus `meta` with compare-and-set on `meta.seq` → `local_store:ack`. On ack the server clears the pending write, marks any cached forecast/desired-plan values in assigns as invalidated, and enqueues recomputation through `DOS-M06-003`. Double-submit produces exactly one persisted reading because the `mutation_id` is idempotent through the hook transaction. If no ack arrives within 2 000 ms or the ack reports an error (quota exceeded, private-browsing throw, compare-and-set conflict), the surface renders a persistent, non-dismissible "Not saved to this browser" state on the affected record, keeps the optimistic value visible but marked unsaved, and offers export; nothing is silently retried and nothing is invisibly rolled back.
- **FR-7 (unit correctness).** Helper conversions and canonical storage are deterministic in miles and kilometers per §8 boundary-conversion; converting for display and back never drifts. Conversion is a pure function shared between the LiveView surface and the client adapter so both sides of the mutation round trip agree on the canonical value.
- **FR-8 (honest states).** The capture surface accurately represents each forecast-input state — declared baseline only, observations only, both, or intentionally neither — and each browser-side state — `:hydrating`, `:empty`, `:data_missing`, `:storage_unavailable`, session-only fallback, and `phx-disconnected` reconnect. **No state may render "You have no readings yet" while `local_state == :hydrating`** (ADR-0004 §"First-paint and empty-state rules"); this is a returning-user data-loss illusion and is prohibited.
- **FR-9 (INV-7 honest degradation on catalog and socket failure — NEW; addresses sweep finding 2).** When the read-only server-side catalog query layer (`DOS-M09-005`) fails, the capture surface renders "catalog unavailable" — not a stale placeholder, not a fabricated interval, not "no data" masquerading as "no readings needed." When the LiveView socket is down, mutation controls are visibly disabled with `phx-disconnected` styling; the surface does not accept a save that cannot round-trip. When a queued mutation is orphaned by a LiveView reconnect (server-side `pending_writes` cleared by remount), the client re-drives from IndexedDB, and any mutation that never reached the hook is surfaced as "not saved to this browser" rather than dropped silently. Each of these degradation paths is testable and each has ratified copy from `DOS-M09-004`.
- **FR-10 (no server-side personal record; enforcement-testable).** No usage profile, odometer reading, dismissal, `mutation_id`, `tab_id`, VIN, or note text authored or handled by this card may be persisted server-side (no Ecto row, no ETS entry, no external store, no `Logger` line, no error-page assigns dump). The values exist server-side only as transient `socket.assigns` for the connection lifetime. This FR is verified mechanically by ADR-0004 §"Enforcement" (single `CatalogRepo` opened read-only; scripted-session log-scanning test).

## Technical implementation contract

### Components and ownership boundaries

- **LiveView surfaces.** The typical-usage capture step and the odometer check-in flow render inside the hosted Phoenix application on Fly.io behind `digitaloilsticker.com`. They render `assigns.local_state == :hydrating` as a skeleton during the static render and while awaiting the hook's `local_store:hydrate` event, transition to `:loaded`/`:empty`/`:data_missing`/`:storage_unavailable` per ADR-0004 §"First-paint and empty-state rules", and never render onboarding-from-zero while hydrating.
- **Client hook `LocalStore` (owned by `DOS-M09-002`).** This card reuses the hook; it does not fork it. The hook opens `dos_local`, hydrates via one `readonly` transaction across `vehicles`, `readings`, `prefs`, and `meta`, pushes `local_store:hydrate`, and applies writes via one `readwrite` transaction with compare-and-set on `meta.seq`.
- **Domain contexts.** Reading and usage-profile write construction, validation, invalidation-marking, and recompute-intent live in plain Elixir domain modules with no `Phoenix.LiveView` or `Plug` dependency (ADR-0004 decision §6, enforced by static check). The LiveView modules render results and orchestrate the mutation round trip; the domain modules never call the adapter.
- **Engine coupling.** The sample-quality feedback consumes reason codes from `DOS-M06-003`'s post-recompute classification for the saved reading; this card owns only the plain-language mapping.

### Data and persistence

- `usage_profiles`: stored in the client `prefs`/`vehicles` object stores per the layout owned by `DOS-M09-001`. Fields: canonical weekly miles (integer base units), input unit, enumerated `input_method` (`direct_weekly`, `per_day`, `per_month`, `workdays_roundtrip`), effective timestamps. Helper sub-inputs (round-trip distance, workdays, etc.) are discarded after computation.
- `odometer_readings`: client `readings` object store keyed by client-generated UUIDv4. Fields per master prompt §8: vehicle ID, observed local date (past nonfuture allowed), normalized odometer in integer base units, input unit, source `manual`, validity/supersession fields. Edits create supersession lineage rather than destructive updates; §9's never-silently-delete rule is enforced in the adapter and re-validated server-side on the hydrate path.
- Idempotent submission: each mutation carries a `mutation_id` UUID and a monotonic `seq`; the hook transaction is idempotent by `mutation_id`, so double-submit produces exactly one persisted reading (AC-6).
- **No server-side persistence for any of the above.** Values exist server-side only in `socket.assigns` for the socket's lifetime. `filter_parameters` is configured so no hydrate/mutate/import payload reaches logs; `debug_errors: false` in production so assigns never render into an error page.

### Interfaces and events

- Reading and usage-profile mutations render optimistically with a "saving" affordance, place the pending write in `assigns.pending_writes` keyed by `mutation_id`, and push `local_store:put`. On `local_store:ack {status: "ok"}` the server clears the pending write, marks the vehicle's cached forecast/desired-plan values in assigns as invalidated, and enqueues recomputation via `DOS-M06-003`; on ack failure (`quota_exceeded`, `conflict`, `error`) the surface renders the "Not saved to this browser" state per FR-6.
- The local `odometer_recorded` event (§7 event contract) is emitted with the stable local `reading_id` and reason codes, not display strings, so the presentation layer (`DOS-M06-004`) can render honestly across languages and rev the engine without a copy rewrite.
- The on-visit suggestion (FR-5) is emitted as an assign, not a push; there is no timer registered anywhere in the LiveView, and grep evidence is part of the release gate.

### Failure and recovery behavior

- A failed capture leaves no partial state. If the server-side transaction that stages the mutation in assigns crashes before `push_event`, the record is not shown as saved. If the hook transaction fails, FR-6's "Not saved to this browser" state renders. If recomputation fails after a successful save, the reading remains, the previous forecast is visibly invalidated (never a mix of revisions), and recomputation retries on the next relevant trigger per `DOS-M06-003`.
- Constraint violations (future date, out-of-segment contradiction, implausible value per `DOS-M05-004` rules) are rejected with specific, recoverable messages. Quarantined values follow §9 (retained, never silently deleted); the hydration validator preserves unknown per-record keys per ADR-0004 §"Validation".
- Reconnect: pending writes in `assigns.pending_writes` are cleared by remount; the client re-drives from IndexedDB (client is source of truth for anything committed). A save that never reached IndexedDB before the disconnect surfaces as "not saved to this browser."
- Session-only fallback (IndexedDB blocked): capture is still functional against `socket.assigns`, but a persistent banner from `DOS-M09-004` states that nothing is being saved and offers export prominently; nothing here silently falls back to `localStorage` or server storage (ADR-0004 §"Private browsing and blocked storage").

### Security and privacy

- INV-3, INV-4, INV-5 preserved without qualification. No third-party analytics, tag manager, error reporter, or CDN loads on the capture surfaces (CSP enforces this structurally, per ADR-0004 §"Content Security Policy").
- No trip address, GPS coordinate, sensor permission, geolocation prompt, or camera/microphone/payment permission is requested — the `Permissions-Policy` header denies these globally.
- No session-cookie usage beyond the CSRF token and `live_socket_id`; capture never writes a stable identifier of any kind.
- Server logs on capture routes MUST NOT contain: VIN, odometer values, service dates, note text, custom vehicle names, `mutation_id`, `tab_id`, or the vehicle a session selected. Enforcement: `filter_parameters` set, a Logger filter drops `handle_event` params by default with an explicit non-personal allowlist, and the ADR-0004 §"Enforcement" scripted-session log-scanning test is a release gate.
- INV-22 (hosted transport security, per ADR-0004): TLS via Fly-managed certificates, `check_origin` restricted to production hostnames, `Plug.SSL` with `rewrite_on: [:x_forwarded_proto, :x_forwarded_host, :x_forwarded_port]`, CSRF verified on socket connect.

### Accessibility and performance

- Capture forms meet WCAG 2.2 AA (verified against browser assistive technology per rescoped `DOS-M07-002`): labeled inputs, visible focus, error text programmatically associated, status feedback announced to AT, no color-only eligibility signaling, and no keyboard trap.
- Delta preview and helper calculator render from hydrated assigns without additional server round trips beyond the mutation itself; hydration must be sub-second on a cold connection per ADR-0004 R2 so the skeleton state does not dominate the first impression.
- The on-visit suggestion (FR-5) is computed in-place from hydrated assigns; no additional query, no additional round trip.

## UX and content states

- **Pre-hydration state.** Skeleton — never a "no vehicles yet" or "no readings yet" flash. Uses copy from `DOS-M09-004`.
- **`:empty` state.** Explicit first-run copy that never implies a server-side backup; explains that data is held in this browser only and that export is the only recovery.
- **`:data_missing` state.** Boot hint said `has_data` but hydration returned zero. Explicit warning per ADR-0004 §"First-paint rules"; offers import; does not render an onboarding flow that would overwrite what the user thought they had.
- **`:storage_unavailable` / session-only banner.** Distinct from `:empty`. Uses ratified copy from `DOS-M09-004` stating that nothing is being saved and that closing the tab loses everything; export is offered prominently.
- **Typical-usage step (FR-1).** Entry state (direct weekly or helper), computed-value preview ("we'll use N miles/week"), skip state ("I don't know / skip" with neutral explanation), saved state.
- **Expectation copy (FR-2).** Two readings at least seven days apart begin personalization; roughly six weeks of recent evidence can replace the declared baseline; never promises a confidence level in advance; never implies data is backed up.
- **Reading entry (FR-3).** Default-today, past-date, preview showing preceding/following readings and delta, validation-error states (future date, segment contradiction, implausible value), pending-save ("saving to this browser…"), success, and FR-6 unsaved state.
- **Post-save feedback (FR-4).** `eligible`, `too_close`, `stale_gap`, `other_segment`, `outlier` — each with plain-language copy; outlier says "unusual compared with recent samples," not "wrong."
- **On-visit suggestion (FR-5).** Non-blocking banner rendered only when the elapsed-time and low/medium-confidence conditions hold at mount; silent otherwise; dismissible for the visit; ephemeral dismissal; never labels itself a "reminder," "notification," or "alert" (those words imply an OS-level delivery that does not exist here).
- **Reconnect state.** `phx-disconnected` styling per ADR-0004 §"WebSocket and LiveView considerations"; mutation controls disabled; hydrated DOM retained (not torn down to skeleton).
- **Catalog-unavailable state (FR-9).** Rendered when `DOS-M09-005` returns an error; explicitly says the catalog is unavailable, does not fabricate an interval, does not silently show cached-but-stale values.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC-2. Weekly helper conversions are deterministic in miles and kilometers and round-trip without drift, verified by property tests.
- [ ] AC-3. The eligibility/rejection/outlier reason displayed after each sample equals the `DOS-M06-001` engine output code and its ratified plain-language mapping — verified against golden-vector-derived cases.
- [ ] AC-4. Editing or deleting a contributing reading updates the forecast and reminder-plan revision exactly once through the mutation round trip; no double-recompute, no dropped invalidation.
- [ ] AC-5. No trip address, GPS coordinate, camera/microphone/payment permission, or undeclared sensor permission is requested; the `Permissions-Policy` header enforces the denial. A privacy data inventory diff records no new sensitive class.
- [ ] AC-6. Double-submit of a reading (client retry, network hiccup, `Enter`+click) produces exactly one persisted reading in IndexedDB, verified by the `mutation_id` idempotence check.
- [ ] AC-7 (REPLACED per sweep finding 1). The on-visit "take another reading" suggestion appears only at mount/hydration and only when confidence is `low`/`medium` **and** the most recent eligible reading is at least 7 days old (or none exists), and NEVER creates any OS notification, Web Push, service-worker notification, `setTimeout`, `setInterval`, cron job, or queued background task. Grepping the capture LiveView tree for `setInterval`, `setTimeout`, `Notification`, `serviceWorker`, `PushManager`, or `showNotification` returns zero hits.
- [ ] AC-8 (NEW, enforcement). No usage profile, odometer reading, dismissal, `mutation_id`, `tab_id`, or VIN authored by this card is persisted server-side. Verified by (i) the ADR-0004 §"Enforcement" test asserting the deployed application's Ecto repo list is exactly `[CatalogRepo]` opened read-only, and (ii) the scripted-session log-scanning test that drives hydrate → capture mutations → export → clear → import and asserts no odometer value, note, or vehicle name appears in any captured log line.
- [ ] AC-9 (NEW, honest degradation). Each capture surface renders the ratified copy for `:hydrating`, `:empty`, `:data_missing`, `:storage_unavailable`, session-only, `phx-disconnected`, and `catalog_unavailable` states, per `DOS-M09-004`; no surface renders an empty state while hydrating and no surface renders a fabricated catalog value when the query layer errors.
- [ ] AC-10 (NEW, mutation round trip). ## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- **Risk (R-A):** users perceive a hosted URL as implying server-side backup and are surprised when clearing browser data erases their captures. Mitigations: FR-2 and FR-8 copy, `DOS-M09-004` ratified state copy, INV-5 grep gate on prohibited words ("backup", "synced", "safe", "we'll remember", "your account") in capture surfaces, and export offered on every unsaved and session-only path.
- **Risk (R-B):** browser storage eviction of infrequently-used sites (ADR-0004 R1) is precisely the profile for this product — a user who checks their oil quarterly is the eviction-target archetype. Partial mitigation: `navigator.storage.persist()` on the first meaningful write (owned by `DOS-M09-002`), the `:data_missing` state so eviction never disguises as an empty garage, and export nudges after meaningful writes. Full mitigation is the deferred PWA decision (`DOS-M09-010`).
- **Risk (R-C):** hydration latency inflates first-meaningful-paint for capture surfaces (ADR-0004 R2). Mitigation: the skeleton state must not last visibly long; sub-second hydration is a budget owned by the rescoped `DOS-M07-003`.
- **Risk (R-D):** on-visit sampling suggestion (FR-5) reads as under-delivery to users used to OS reminders. Mitigation: the suggestion never labels itself a "reminder" or "notification" (words that imply background delivery); FR-2 sets the expectation up front; the rescoped `DOS-M06-005` renders due/overdue state whenever the app is opened so the user sees the state without needing a nudge; Web Push remains a separately-considered spike at `DOS-M09-010`.
- **Risk (R-E):** multi-tab captures against a shared IndexedDB can race. Mitigation: compare-and-set on `meta.seq` inside the same transaction, `BroadcastChannel` re-hydration on remote writes, and last-write-wins at record granularity with the loss made visible (ADR-0004 §"Multi-tab consistency"); recorded as an open question in ADR-0004 R4 rather than presented as a general solution.
- **Decision (inherited):** the app never estimates driving from sensors; all usage data is user-declared or user-entered (Excluded list).
- **Decision (pivot-forced):** the recurring nudge is on-visit only, not a timer. Any future "background" reminder path is a separate ADR (not a ticket) because it would require a service worker, Web Push, and a server-held subscription — the last of which conflicts with ADR-0004 decision §4.
- **Open question:** whether the escalated copy at >14 days elapsed (FR-5) should also downgrade the displayed confidence level even without a recompute trigger. Resolved by product review before close; the current default is no — confidence is authored by `DOS-M06-001` and this card does not override it.

## Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)

> This section preserves the card as chartered before the 2026-08-01 pivot to browser-first, and is retained for historical readability of the ADR record. The Outcome above is authoritative.

### Outcome
Users can provide an initial weekly-driving estimate and periodically add accurate odometer samples with enough guidance to improve the forecast over time. Capture flows explain why the app asks, show the effect of each sample, and keep every input honest about its eligibility under the `DOS-M06-001` policy.

### Why this matters and what it unblocks
The algorithm cannot estimate when a mileage threshold will be reached without a usage prior or observations. Thoughtful capture reduces noisy samples and explains why the app asks. This issue supplies the two forecast inputs the policy blends — the declared weekly baseline (`usage_profiles`) and recurring odometer observations (`odometer_readings`) — and therefore directly gates `DOS-M06-003` (the engine needs real capture paths to recompute against), `DOS-M06-004` (presentation must reflect capture states), and reminder accuracy overall.

### Context and source of truth
- Master prompt §3, rule 3 ("No telematics"): the app never claims to know the current odometer; users enter service mileage and later readings manually.
- Master prompt §6 "Primary user journey", step 7: "A later odometer check-in refines the estimate and reconciles local reminders."
- Master prompt §7: expected LiveViews/components include `MileageLive` and consistent empty/error/recovery components; the local event contract includes `odometer_recorded` and `forecast_changed`.
- Master prompt §8: `odometer_readings` (UUID, vehicle ID, observed instant/local date, normalized odometer, input unit, source `service_event` or `manual`, validity/supersession fields, index `(vehicle_id, observed_at)`) and `usage_profiles` (vehicle ID, user baseline distance/week, unit, severe-use answers and selected condition, effective timestamps); integer base units for persisted mileage with boundary conversion so switching miles/kilometres never loses precision.
- Master prompt §9: readings with negative distance, duplicate-time contradictions, implausible values, or explicit supersession are rejected or quarantined, never silently deleted; "Establish a user-entered baseline distance/week as a prior."
- `DOS-M06-001` (the forecast policy): eligibility windows, outlier semantics, evidence/confidence rules that this issue's feedback must echo exactly.
- Roadmap catalog entry "DOS-M06-002 — Capture typical usage and recurring odometer samples" in the master prompt (the canonical scope statement expanded here).

### Included
- Declared weekly distance capture: direct entry of a typical weekly driving distance, stored canonically.
- Optional miles-per-day or miles-per-month helper converted to weekly, plus a workdays × round-trip-distance helper, with the computed weekly value shown before saving.
- Sample reminders inside the app: a local, in-app "take another reading" suggestion (no OS notifications from this issue).
- Add/edit/delete odometer readings with preview of neighboring readings and resulting delta.
- Sample-quality feedback after save: eligible, too close, stale-gap, other-segment, or outlier status with plain-language explanation.
- Reset/discontinuity handling, reusing all constraints and discontinuity behavior from `DOS-M05-004`.
- Atomic invalidation of the persisted forecast and desired notification plan on every input change, with local recomputation enqueued.

### Excluded
- GPS/background tracking.
- Calendar scanning.
- Fuel data.
- Automatic car sync.
- Claims that driving style can be diagnosed from mileage alone.
- OS notification scheduling of any kind (the in-app suggestion never creates OS notifications until the user separately enables them in `DOS-M06-005`).
- Forecast arithmetic itself (owned by `DOS-M06-001`/`DOS-M06-003`; this issue only captures inputs and displays engine-produced eligibility results).

### Preconditions
- `DOS-M05-001` is closed (the M05 foundation this UI builds on).
- `DOS-M05-004` is closed: odometer-reading constraints and discontinuity/reset behavior exist and are the single source of validation rules reused here.
- `DOS-M06-001` is approved: eligibility, outlier, and confidence vocabulary is frozen so feedback copy can match engine output exactly.
- Product approval of capture wording (inherited from the `DOS-M06-001` default input and wording approval).

### Functional requirements
- FR-1 (declared usage capture): Ask for typical usage after the vehicle and last service are saved, with "I don't know / skip" available. Support direct weekly distance and helpers such as workdays × round-trip distance plus other weekly miles; show the computed weekly value before saving. Store the final canonical weekly miles and the declared input method, not sensitive trip locations.
- FR-2 (expectation setting): Explain that two readings at least seven days apart begin to personalize the estimate and roughly six weeks of recent evidence can replace the declared baseline under `DOS-M06-001`. Never promise a particular confidence before data qualifies.
- FR-3 (reading entry): "Add current odometer" defaults to today's local date but allows a past nonfuture date. Show the preceding and following reading and resulting delta before commit. Reuse all constraints/discontinuity behavior from `DOS-M05-004`.
- FR-4 (sample-quality feedback): After save, display whether the sample is eligible, too close, stale-gap, in another odometer segment, or currently considered an outlier. "Outlier" means "unusual compared with recent samples," not "wrong"; the user may correct/delete the source but may not force a result to high confidence.
- FR-5 (in-app sampling suggestion): Offer a local in-app "take another reading" suggestion after 7–14 days only while confidence is low/medium; do not create OS notifications until the user separately enables them.
- FR-6 (atomic invalidation): All input changes atomically invalidate the persisted forecast and desired notification plan, then enqueue local recomputation. Double-submit produces one reading.
- FR-7 (unit correctness): Helper conversions and canonical storage are deterministic in miles and kilometers; converting a value for display and back never drifts, per the §8 boundary-conversion rule.
- FR-8 (honest states): The UI accurately represents each forecast-input state — declared baseline only, observations only, both, or intentionally neither — without implying missing data is an error.

### Technical implementation contract
### Components and ownership boundaries

- LiveView surfaces: the typical-usage capture step (post vehicle/last-service save) and the odometer check-in flow (in/alongside `MileageLive` per §7), plus the in-app suggestion component. All validation and eligibility semantics come from domain contexts; LiveView modules render results and never implement their own arithmetic (§9).
- Domain ownership: reading CRUD and constraint enforcement live in the context that `DOS-M05-004` established; usage-profile writes live beside it; forecast invalidation/recompute intent is delegated to the `DOS-M06-003` application service.
- Sample-quality feedback displays engine-produced reason codes from `DOS-M06-001`/`DOS-M06-003`; this issue owns the plain-language mapping, not the classification.

### Data and persistence

- `usage_profiles` (§8): store final canonical weekly miles (integer base units), the input unit, and the declared input method (direct weekly, per-day helper, per-month helper, workdays × round-trip helper); preserve effective timestamps. No trip locations, addresses, or route data are ever stored.
- `odometer_readings` (§8): UUID, vehicle ID, observed local date (past nonfuture allowed), normalized odometer in integer base units, input unit, source `manual`, validity/supersession fields; edits create supersession lineage rather than destructive updates, consistent with §9's never-silently-delete rule.
- Double-submit protection: idempotent submission (client token or equivalent) guarantees one persisted reading per user action (FR-6).
- Forecast and desired-plan invalidation markers are persisted in the same transaction as the input change so no window exists where stale derived data appears authoritative.

### Interfaces and events

- Reading and usage-profile mutations return typed results and emit the local `odometer_recorded` event (§7 event contract) with stable local IDs and reason codes, not display strings.
- Each committed input change enqueues local recomputation through the `DOS-M06-003` service, which emits `forecast_changed(vehicle_id, revision)` downstream; this issue never computes or publishes forecast values itself.
- The sample-quality feedback consumes the engine's post-recompute classification for the saved reading (eligible / too close / stale-gap / other segment / outlier, with reason codes).

### Failure and recovery behavior

- A failed save leaves no partial state: reading, invalidation marker, and recompute intent commit atomically or not at all (FR-6).
- If recomputation fails after a successful save, the reading is preserved, the previous forecast remains visibly stale/invalidated (never a mix of revisions), and recomputation retries on next relevant trigger per `DOS-M06-003`.
- Constraint violations (future date, out-of-segment contradiction, implausible value per `DOS-M05-004` rules) are rejected with specific, recoverable messages; quarantined values follow §9 (retained, never silently deleted).
- Offline is the normal case: every flow works with no network, per non-negotiable rule 2.

### Security and privacy

- No trip address, GPS coordinate, or undeclared sensor permission is captured — a hard acceptance criterion (AC-5), verified by a privacy data inventory diff.
- Odometer values, notes, and vehicle identifiers are redacted from logs and fixtures per §7 production-shell rules.
- The declared input method is stored as an enumeration only; helper sub-inputs (for example, round-trip distance) are not retained after the canonical weekly value is computed, minimizing stored behavioral detail.

### Accessibility and performance

- Capture forms meet the app's accessibility baseline: labeled inputs, visible focus, error text programmatically associated with fields, status feedback announced to assistive technology, and no color-only eligibility signaling.
- Delta preview (preceding/following reading) renders from indexed queries (`(vehicle_id, observed_at)` per §8) and stays responsive for the worst supported history size.
- The helper calculator updates the computed weekly value without full-page reloads and remains usable at large text sizes.

### UX and content states
- Typical-usage step: entry state (direct weekly value or helper), computed-value preview state ("we'll use N miles/week"), skip state ("I don't know / skip" with a neutral explanation of what improves without it), and saved state.
- Expectation copy state (FR-2): explains two readings at least seven days apart begin personalization and roughly six weeks of recent evidence can replace the declared baseline; never promises a confidence level in advance.
- Reading entry: default-today state, past-date state, preview state showing preceding/following readings and delta, validation-error states (future date, segment contradiction, implausible value), and success state.
- Post-save feedback states: eligible, too close, stale-gap, another odometer segment, currently considered an outlier — each with plain-language explanation; outlier copy says "unusual compared with recent samples," not "wrong."
- In-app suggestion state: appears after 7–14 days only while confidence is low/medium; silent otherwise; dismissible without penalty.
- Input-state summary: declared-only, observations-only, both, intentionally neither — each accurately described (AC-1).
