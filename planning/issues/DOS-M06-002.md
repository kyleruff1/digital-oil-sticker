## Outcome

Users can provide an initial weekly-driving estimate and periodically add accurate odometer samples with enough guidance to improve the forecast over time. Capture flows explain why the app asks, show the effect of each sample, and keep every input honest about its eligibility under the `DOS-M06-001` policy.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

The algorithm cannot estimate when a mileage threshold will be reached without a usage prior or observations. Thoughtful capture reduces noisy samples and explains why the app asks. This issue supplies the two forecast inputs the policy blends — the declared weekly baseline (`usage_profiles`) and recurring odometer observations (`odometer_readings`) — and therefore directly gates `DOS-M06-003` (the engine needs real capture paths to recompute against), `DOS-M06-004` (presentation must reflect capture states), and reminder accuracy overall.

## Context and source of truth

- Master prompt §3, rule 3 ("No telematics"): the app never claims to know the current odometer; users enter service mileage and later readings manually.
- Master prompt §6 "Primary user journey", step 7: "A later odometer check-in refines the estimate and reconciles local reminders."
- Master prompt §7: expected LiveViews/components include `MileageLive` and consistent empty/error/recovery components; the local event contract includes `odometer_recorded` and `forecast_changed`.
- Master prompt §8: `odometer_readings` (UUID, vehicle ID, observed instant/local date, normalized odometer, input unit, source `service_event` or `manual`, validity/supersession fields, index `(vehicle_id, observed_at)`) and `usage_profiles` (vehicle ID, user baseline distance/week, unit, severe-use answers and selected condition, effective timestamps); integer base units for persisted mileage with boundary conversion so switching miles/kilometres never loses precision.
- Master prompt §9: readings with negative distance, duplicate-time contradictions, implausible values, or explicit supersession are rejected or quarantined, never silently deleted; "Establish a user-entered baseline distance/week as a prior."
- `DOS-M06-001` (the forecast policy): eligibility windows, outlier semantics, evidence/confidence rules that this issue's feedback must echo exactly.
- Roadmap catalog entry "DOS-M06-002 — Capture typical usage and recurring odometer samples" in the master prompt (the canonical scope statement expanded here).

## Included

- Declared weekly distance capture: direct entry of a typical weekly driving distance, stored canonically.
- Optional miles-per-day or miles-per-month helper converted to weekly, plus a workdays × round-trip-distance helper, with the computed weekly value shown before saving.
- Sample reminders inside the app: a local, in-app "take another reading" suggestion (no OS notifications from this issue).
- Add/edit/delete odometer readings with preview of neighboring readings and resulting delta.
- Sample-quality feedback after save: eligible, too close, stale-gap, other-segment, or outlier status with plain-language explanation.
- Reset/discontinuity handling, reusing all constraints and discontinuity behavior from `DOS-M05-004`.
- Atomic invalidation of the persisted forecast and desired notification plan on every input change, with local recomputation enqueued.

## Excluded

- GPS/background tracking.
- Calendar scanning.
- Fuel data.
- Automatic car sync.
- Claims that driving style can be diagnosed from mileage alone.
- OS notification scheduling of any kind (the in-app suggestion never creates OS notifications until the user separately enables them in `DOS-M06-005`).
- Forecast arithmetic itself (owned by `DOS-M06-001`/`DOS-M06-003`; this issue only captures inputs and displays engine-produced eligibility results).

## Preconditions

- `DOS-M05-001` is closed (the M05 foundation this UI builds on).
- `DOS-M05-004` is closed: odometer-reading constraints and discontinuity/reset behavior exist and are the single source of validation rules reused here.
- `DOS-M06-001` is approved: eligibility, outlier, and confidence vocabulary is frozen so feedback copy can match engine output exactly.
- Product approval of capture wording (inherited from the `DOS-M06-001` default input and wording approval).

## Functional requirements

- FR-1 (declared usage capture): Ask for typical usage after the vehicle and last service are saved, with “I don't know / skip” available. Support direct weekly distance and helpers such as workdays × round-trip distance plus other weekly miles; show the computed weekly value before saving. Store the final canonical weekly miles and the declared input method, not sensitive trip locations.
- FR-2 (expectation setting): Explain that two readings at least seven days apart begin to personalize the estimate and roughly six weeks of recent evidence can replace the declared baseline under `DOS-M06-001`. Never promise a particular confidence before data qualifies.
- FR-3 (reading entry): “Add current odometer” defaults to today's local date but allows a past nonfuture date. Show the preceding and following reading and resulting delta before commit. Reuse all constraints/discontinuity behavior from `DOS-M05-004`.
- FR-4 (sample-quality feedback): After save, display whether the sample is eligible, too close, stale-gap, in another odometer segment, or currently considered an outlier. “Outlier” means “unusual compared with recent samples,” not “wrong”; the user may correct/delete the source but may not force a result to high confidence.
- FR-5 (in-app sampling suggestion): Offer a local in-app “take another reading” suggestion after 7–14 days only while confidence is low/medium; do not create OS notifications until the user separately enables them.
- FR-6 (atomic invalidation): All input changes atomically invalidate the persisted forecast and desired notification plan, then enqueue local recomputation. Double-submit produces one reading.
- FR-7 (unit correctness): Helper conversions and canonical storage are deterministic in miles and kilometers; converting a value for display and back never drifts, per the §8 boundary-conversion rule.
- FR-8 (honest states): The UI accurately represents each forecast-input state — declared baseline only, observations only, both, or intentionally neither — without implying missing data is an error.

## Technical implementation contract

### Components and ownership boundaries

- LiveView surfaces: the typical-usage capture step (post vehicle/last-service save) and the odometer check-in flow (in/alongside `MileageLive` per §7), plus the in-app suggestion component. All validation and eligibility semantics come from domain contexts; LiveView modules render results and never implement their own arithmetic (§9).
- Domain ownership: reading CRUD and constraint enforcement live in the context that `DOS-M05-004` established; usage-profile writes live beside it; forecast invalidation/recompute intent is delegated to the `DOS-M06-003` application service.
- Sample-quality feedback displays engine-produced reason codes from `DOS-M06-001`/`DOS-M06-003`; this issue owns the plain-language mapping, not the classification.

### Data and persistence

- `usage_profiles` (§8): store final canonical weekly miles (integer base units), the input unit, and the declared input method (direct weekly, per-day helper, per-month helper, workdays × round-trip helper); preserve effective timestamps. No trip locations, addresses, or route data are ever stored.
- `odometer_readings` (§8): UUID, vehicle ID, observed local date (past nonfuture allowed), normalized odometer in integer base units, input unit, source `manual`, validity/supersession fields; edits create supersession lineage rather than destructive updates, consistent with §9's never-silently-delete rule.
- Double-submit protection: idempotent submission (client token or equivalent) guarantees one persisted reading per user action (FR-6).
- Forecast and desired-plan invalidation markers are persisted in the same transaction as the input change so no window exists where stale derived data appears authoritative.

### Interfaces and events

- Reading and usage-profile mutations return typed results and emit the local `odometer_recorded` event (§7 event contract) with stable local IDs and reason codes, not display strings.
- Each committed input change enqueues local recomputation through the `DOS-M06-003` service, which emits `forecast_changed(vehicle_id, revision)` downstream; this issue never computes or publishes forecast values itself.
- The sample-quality feedback consumes the engine's post-recompute classification for the saved reading (eligible / too close / stale-gap / other segment / outlier, with reason codes).

### Failure and recovery behavior

- A failed save leaves no partial state: reading, invalidation marker, and recompute intent commit atomically or not at all (FR-6).
- If recomputation fails after a successful save, the reading is preserved, the previous forecast remains visibly stale/invalidated (never a mix of revisions), and recomputation retries on next relevant trigger per `DOS-M06-003`.
- Constraint violations (future date, out-of-segment contradiction, implausible value per `DOS-M05-004` rules) are rejected with specific, recoverable messages; quarantined values follow §9 (retained, never silently deleted).
- Offline is the normal case: every flow works with no network, per non-negotiable rule 2.

### Security and privacy

- No trip address, GPS coordinate, or undeclared sensor permission is captured — a hard acceptance criterion (AC-5), verified by a privacy data inventory diff.
- Odometer values, notes, and vehicle identifiers are redacted from logs and fixtures per §7 production-shell rules.
- The declared input method is stored as an enumeration only; helper sub-inputs (for example, round-trip distance) are not retained after the canonical weekly value is computed, minimizing stored behavioral detail.

### Accessibility and performance

- Capture forms meet the app's accessibility baseline: labeled inputs, visible focus, error text programmatically associated with fields, status feedback announced to assistive technology, and no color-only eligibility signaling.
- Delta preview (preceding/following reading) renders from indexed queries (`(vehicle_id, observed_at)` per §8) and stays responsive for the worst supported history size.
- The helper calculator updates the computed weekly value without full-page reloads and remains usable at large text sizes.

## UX and content states

- Typical-usage step: entry state (direct weekly value or helper), computed-value preview state ("we'll use N miles/week"), skip state (“I don't know / skip” with a neutral explanation of what improves without it), and saved state.
- Expectation copy state (FR-2): explains two readings at least seven days apart begin personalization and roughly six weeks of recent evidence can replace the declared baseline; never promises a confidence level in advance.
- Reading entry: default-today state, past-date state, preview state showing preceding/following readings and delta, validation-error states (future date, segment contradiction, implausible value), and success state.
- Post-save feedback states: eligible, too close, stale-gap, another odometer segment, currently considered an outlier — each with plain-language explanation; outlier copy says “unusual compared with recent samples,” not “wrong.”
- In-app suggestion state: appears after 7–14 days only while confidence is low/medium; silent otherwise; dismissible without penalty.
- Input-state summary: declared-only, observations-only, both, intentionally neither — each accurately described (AC-1).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC-1: A user may forecast from a declared baseline, observations, both, or intentionally neither, and the UI accurately explains each state.
- [ ] AC-2: Weekly helper conversions are deterministic in miles and kilometers and round-trip without drift.
- [ ] AC-3: Eligibility/rejection/outlier reason displayed after each sample matches `DOS-M06-001` output.
- [ ] AC-4: Editing or deleting a contributing reading updates the forecast and reminder-plan revision exactly once.
- [ ] AC-5: No trip address, GPS coordinate, or undeclared sensor permission is captured.
- [ ] AC-6: Double-submit of a reading produces exactly one persisted reading.
- [ ] AC-7: The in-app "take another reading" suggestion appears only in the 7–14-day window with low/medium confidence and never creates an OS notification.

## Test plan

### Automated

- LiveView/component tests for the capture step, reading entry, preview, and every feedback state.
- Helper conversion property tests: miles/kilometers determinism and round-trip-without-drift (AC-2).
- Duplicate-submit test proving one persisted reading (AC-6).
- Out-of-order and reset/discontinuity E2E flows reusing `DOS-M05-004` fixtures.
- Integration tests asserting that add/edit/delete each trigger exactly one forecast and reminder-plan revision update (AC-4), and that feedback codes equal engine output for golden-vector-derived cases (AC-3).

### Manual/device matrix

- Copy/usability review with sample novice users: can they explain what the app asked for and why, and what the post-save feedback means.
- Privacy data inventory diff reviewed and attached, confirming no new sensitive data classes or permissions (AC-5).
- Offline pass on at least one physical iOS and one physical Android device: full capture flows in airplane mode, including large-text accessibility spot checks.

## Rollout and rollback

- Ships inside the normal app build; no separate rollout artifact and no feature flag is required by the catalog.
- Rollback = reverting the capture UI in a subsequent build. Persisted `usage_profiles` and `odometer_readings` rows follow the §8 `UserRepo` contract and remain valid data for any prior UI; supersession lineage guarantees no destructive loss to roll back from.

## Dependency rationale

- Blocked by `DOS-M05-001`: the foundational M05 app surface this capture UI extends.
- Blocked by `DOS-M05-004`: reading constraints and discontinuity behavior are reused wholesale (FR-3); duplicating or forking them would create divergent validation.
- Blocked by `DOS-M06-001`: feedback must match frozen policy output codes (AC-3); building capture before the vocabulary exists would force copy and contract rework.
- Blocks `DOS-M06-003` and `DOS-M06-004`, and reminder accuracy generally: the engine's recompute triggers and the presentation's contributing-data summary both consume the capture paths and states created here.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- Risk: over-asking for readings annoys users and produces noisy compliance; mitigated by the bounded 7–14-day, low/medium-confidence-only suggestion rule (FR-5).
- Risk: feedback copy drifting from engine reason codes as the policy evolves; mitigated by rendering from the machine-readable codes (AC-3) rather than hand-maintained parallel copy.
- Decision (inherited): the app never estimates driving from sensors; all usage data is user-declared or user-entered (rule 3, Excluded list).
- Open question: final wording of the helper labels and skip explanation — resolved by the copy/usability review evidence before close; the structure and states above are not open.
