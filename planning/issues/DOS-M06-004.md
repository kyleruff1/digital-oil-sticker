## Outcome

The digital sticker shows the earlier service trigger, separates observed facts from estimates, and lets a user understand exactly why the date and confidence changed. Every rendered state maps to a `DOS-M06-001` result; safety language about the oil-life monitor (OLM) and owner's manual is permanent and reviewed.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

A mathematically correct date can still mislead. Explainability and safety language are required before notifications amplify the result. This issue is the gate between the engine (`DOS-M06-003`) and reminders (`DOS-M06-005`): notifications may not be enabled until users can see what the estimate is based on, how confident it is, and that the vehicle's own monitor and manual take precedence (non-negotiable rule 7). It also gates beta, because the presentation is what legal/product review approves.

## Context and source of truth

- Master prompt §3, rule 6 ("Earlier threshold wins") and rule 7 ("Vehicle controls prevail"): the app clearly labels its date as an estimate and tells the user to follow the vehicle indicator and owner documentation when they disagree.
- Master prompt §6 "Required terminology": "Estimated due date" for mileage projection with a confidence label; keep oil-life monitor, calendar interval, mileage interval, normal service, and severe service distinct.
- Master prompt §7: expected LiveViews/components include `StickerLive`, `DueStatus`, `ConfidenceBadge`, and consistent empty/error/recovery components.
- Master prompt §9: confidence is categorical and explainable, not a probability; overdue and unknown mileage-date semantics; the oil-life-monitor rule "adds a prominent monitor instruction and may prevent the app from presenting a false exact date."
- `DOS-M06-001` (frozen vocabulary: causes, confidence tiers, downgrade reasons, exclusion codes) and `DOS-M06-003` (revision-consistent results this screen renders).
- Roadmap catalog entry "DOS-M06-004 — Present forecast, confidence, uncertainty, and OLM/manual warnings" in the master prompt (the canonical scope statement expanded here).

## Included

- Due cause: whether time, mileage, or both currently drive the estimate.
- Confidence label with fixed copy and exposed exact downgrade reason(s).
- Contributing data summary: observed facts with their dates, separated from computed facts.
- As-of dates on every observed value.
- Excluded/unusual sample disclosure with details and edit navigation.
- Setup/stale states: missing-data calls to action and stale-input presentation.
- Uncertainty presentation: the percentile-based recent-driving range and the "rough estimate" fallback.
- Permanent OLM/owner-manual warning adjacent to status and in reminder settings.

## Excluded

- Prescriptive mechanical advice.
- “Safe to drive” language.
- Warranty claims.
- Invented OLM conversions (an OLM alert/percentage is stored only as an optional dated note and is never transformed into mileage/date or used to override the threshold automatically).
- Notification scheduling or permission UI (owned by `DOS-M06-005`; this issue only supplies the warning copy that reminder settings reuse).
- Any local arithmetic — every number and code renders from `DOS-M06-003` results.

## Functional requirements

- FR-1 (headline and earlier-wins framing): Lead with “Estimated oil change by [date]” and “whichever comes first: [time target] or [mileage target/projected crossing].” Identify whether time, mileage, or both currently drive the estimate. When only time is schedulable, say the mileage threshold remains unknown until a reading/usage value is added.
- FR-2 (observed versus computed separation): Display observed facts with dates: last oil change and odometer, latest odometer and as-of date, selected interval/source. Display computed facts separately: effective weekly mileage (rounded for display), projected mileage-threshold date, time target, forecast policy version in details, and confidence.
- FR-3 (fixed confidence copy): Confidence copy is fixed: High = recent, consistent observations over at least six weeks; Medium = some recent observations but limited coverage; Low = declaration-only, sparse, stale, unstable, or highly variable evidence. Expose exact downgrade reason(s), not a generic score.
- FR-4 (uncertainty presentation): If four or more retained rates exist, derive an explanatory variability window by recomputing mileage arrival using the retained 25th and 75th percentile rates; label it a recent-driving range, not a statistical guarantee. If a bound is zero or insufficient, omit the date range. For lower-data states, show “rough estimate” and do not invent ±days.
- FR-5 (excluded-sample disclosure): Surface excluded intervals as “unusual recent periods not used in the primary average,” list their date ranges/rates in details, and provide edit navigation. Never delete or hide them.
- FR-6 (OLM/manual warning): Show the reviewed warning adjacent to status and in reminder settings: “Use your owner's manual and vehicle oil-life monitor. If either tells you to service sooner, follow the earlier instruction.” If the user records an OLM alert/percentage, store it only as an optional dated note; do not transform it into mileage/date or override the threshold automatically.
- FR-7 (status vocabulary and color safety): Never use green/“good” to imply mechanical safety. Status terms are `setup needed`, `estimate available`, `due soon`, `due`, `past due by recorded/estimated data`, and `mileage timing unknown`.
- FR-8 (estimate labeling): An estimated current mileage is never rendered with the same label/style as a manually observed odometer; projections are always visually and programmatically distinguished from observations.
- FR-9 (navigable explainability): From the estimate, a user can reach every contributing editable input (service event, readings, declared usage, interval selection) and return without losing state.

## Technical implementation contract

### Components and ownership boundaries

- LiveView surfaces: the digital-sticker screen (`StickerLive` per §7) plus `DueStatus` and `ConfidenceBadge` components and a forecast-details view for policy version, reasons, and excluded intervals.
- All values, causes, confidence tiers, and reason codes come from `DOS-M06-003` results; this issue owns only mapping codes to reviewed copy and layout. No arithmetic, thresholding, or percentile math is implemented in LiveView — the 25th/75th percentile recomputation (FR-4) is requested from the Forecasting engine/service as an explanatory output, not computed in the view.
- The OLM/manual warning component is a single shared component consumed here and by `DOS-M06-005` reminder settings, so the reviewed text cannot fork.

### Data and persistence

- Reads `forecast_snapshots`-backed results via the Forecasting service at one revision; never mixes fields from different revisions (inherits `DOS-M06-003` AC-4).
- The optional OLM alert/percentage note persists as a dated user note attached to the vehicle/cycle (per §8 note semantics); no derived fields are computed from it.
- No new tables. Display rounding is presentation-only and never written back.

### Interfaces and events

- Subscribes to `forecast_changed(vehicle_id, revision)` to refresh; renders only after loading the complete new revision.
- Edit navigation targets the `DOS-M06-002` capture flows and M05 service-event/interval surfaces using local route state that preserves and restores scroll/focus (FR-9).
- Emits no domain events; it is a pure consumer.

### Failure and recovery behavior

- Missing/corrupt snapshot: the screen shows a recovery state while `DOS-M06-003` self-heals by recomputation; it never renders partial numbers.
- `:insufficient_interval` and `:missing_service` results render as `setup needed` with a specific call to action, not as errors.
- Stale-input presentation: when the latest observation is old, the as-of date makes staleness visible; confidence downgrade reasons explain it (FR-3); the screen never silently hides an outdated basis.
- Time/date context changes trigger re-render through the standard recompute path; the screen never caches yesterday's "today".

### Security and privacy

- No VIN, notes content, or precise history beyond what the user entered is surfaced on the sticker headline; details views show user-entered data only on the user's device.
- Screenshots/fixtures for tests use synthetic data per §7 redaction rules.
- No network calls; all rendering is offline-capable (rule 2).

### Accessibility and performance

- Text zoom: the screen remains usable and complete at large accessibility text sizes.
- Noncolor status: every status term (FR-7) is conveyed by text and iconography, never color alone; no green/"good" mechanical-safety implication.
- Heading order and focus: logical heading hierarchy; focus management preserves position on revision refresh and returns correctly from edit navigation.
- Dynamic announcements: forecast changes and status transitions are announced to assistive technology.
- Performance: rendering a new revision is a single consistent update; details views lazy-load excluded-interval lists for large histories.

## UX and content states

- Headline states: estimate available (with earlier-wins framing and cause), time-only schedulable (mileage threshold visible but unknown date, with the FR-1 explanation), `setup needed` (missing service baseline or interval), `mileage timing unknown`.
- Status states (exact vocabulary, FR-7): `setup needed`, `estimate available`, `due soon`, `due`, `past due by recorded/estimated data`, `mileage timing unknown`.
- Confidence states: High/Medium/Low with fixed copy (FR-3) and expandable exact downgrade reasons.
- Uncertainty states: recent-driving range shown (≥4 retained rates, valid bounds), range omitted (zero/insufficient bound), “rough estimate” (lower-data states).
- Disclosure states: excluded intervals list ("unusual recent periods not used in the primary average") with date ranges/rates and edit links; forecast policy version and as-of dates in details.
- Safety states: OLM/manual warning visible in due, not-due, incomplete, and notification settings states — permanent, not dismissible out of existence.
- Overdue state: past-due status persists per FR-7 wording; the projected crossing date remains available for explanation (per `DOS-M06-001` FR-7).

## Test plan

### Automated

- Golden-vector component tests: each `DOS-M06-001` vector renders its approved fixture (AC-1) — target, cause, confidence, reasons, call to action.
- Copy matrix tests: fixed confidence copy, status vocabulary, and the exact OLM/manual warning string are asserted against the reviewed source of truth.
- Screenshot/visual regression suite across headline, status, confidence, uncertainty, disclosure, and safety states in light/dark and at large text sizes.
- Navigation tests proving edit round-trips preserve state (AC-2).
- Accessibility assertions: heading order, labels, focus management, and announcement hooks.

### Manual/device matrix

- Screen-reader transcript on at least one physical iOS device (VoiceOver) and one physical Android device (TalkBack) covering estimate, due, overdue, and setup states.
- Design/legal/product approval walkthrough of all safety and status copy, recorded as sign-off (AC-4).
- Usability session showing users can name which trigger comes first (time or mileage) after viewing the screen.
- Text-zoom sweep at maximum supported accessibility sizes on both platforms.

## Rollout and rollback

- Ships inside the normal app build; the screen replaces/extends the M05 dashboard presentation for forecast content.
- Rollback: reverting the presentation build restores the prior dashboard; no persisted data depends on this issue (the OLM note field follows the §8 `UserRepo` contract and is forward-compatible). Reviewed copy is versioned with the fixtures so a rollback restores a previously approved copy set, never an unreviewed mixture.

## Dependency rationale

- Blocked by `DOS-M05-005`: this issue extends the existing dashboard/sticker surface rather than inventing a parallel one.
- Blocked by `DOS-M06-001`: fixtures and copy are keyed to the frozen vocabulary and golden vectors (AC-1).
- Blocked by `DOS-M06-002`: contributing-input navigation (AC-2) needs the capture destinations to exist.
- Blocked by `DOS-M06-003`: the screen renders revision-consistent engine output; building against a mock engine would let fixtures drift from reality.
- Blocks enabling notifications in `DOS-M06-005` and beta: reminder settings reuse the reviewed warning component, and notifications may not amplify an estimate users cannot yet understand.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- Decision (fixed by catalog): confidence copy is fixed text, not a score; status vocabulary is a closed six-term set; the OLM/manual warning is permanent and verbatim as reviewed.
- Decision (fixed by catalog): the variability window is explanatory (25th/75th percentile recomputation), labeled a recent-driving range, never a statistical guarantee.
- Risk: legal/product review may require copy adjustments late; mitigated by reviewing the copy matrix early against fixtures rather than after full implementation.
- Risk: fixture explosion across state combinations; mitigated by deriving fixtures mechanically from the golden vectors instead of hand-authoring.
- Open question: exact visual treatment distinguishing estimated from observed values (typography/badge choice) — resolved in design review; the requirement itself (AC-3) is not open.
