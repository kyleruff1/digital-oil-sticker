> **RESCOPED FOR BROWSER MVP — 2026-08-01.** [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) established the browser-first MVP. This card is retained with the same identity; its Outcome and FRs below are the rescoped spec. The pre-pivot spec is preserved at the bottom under "Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)". Disposition per [PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md](../../docs/governance/PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md#issue-by-issue-classification): _Becomes in-app reminder preferences plus a due-state correctness gate; the OS delivery-reliability portion of the gate moves to the native re-entry._

## Outcome

Users and QA have one browser-side place to manage reminder preferences (which lead thresholds are enabled, in-app display formatting, privacy preview) and to see the current in-app due/overdue state, and there is a specified correctness gate proving that state is right after cold hydration, after reconnect, across timezone/DST/system-clock changes, and after every forecast revision. The MVP promise is **in-app due state, permission-independent**, per amended INV-17 — the OS delivery-reliability half of the pre-pivot gate is not attempted here and is parked with the deferred native re-entry.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

Under [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) the browser has no `mob_notify`, no OS-scheduled local notifications, and no killed-app delivery to reconcile. In-app due/overdue state **is** the whole reminder promise (CONSTITUTION 2.0.0 §0.2 LD-1, INV-17 amended). Because LiveView renders from server assigns and every reconnect re-runs the hydration protocol ([ADR-0004 §"The client-storage + LiveView hydration protocol"](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md), INV-24), the due-state a user sees depends entirely on the hydration seam being correct. This card is the correctness gate for that seam as it applies to reminders, and the preferences surface that lets users configure what they see.

This card closes M06 for the browser MVP. It feeds the rescoped release-gate cards `DOS-M07-004` (browser/engine/OS QA matrix), `DOS-M07-006` (privacy-preserving closed beta), and `DOS-M07-007` (staged Fly deploy and rehearsed rollback) — but only for the browser-side evidence they now consume; the OS-delivery evidence they were originally written against has been moved out per PIVOT-2026-08-01 and is not produced here. That change is what fixes the pre-pivot dependency chain: leaving OS-delivery evidence gated on this card would leave the release train permanently red because the evidence is unproducible without physical iOS and a Mac (per [ADR-0004 §"The native gate cannot complete as written"](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md)).

## Included

- A browser reminder-preferences surface (LiveView) that lets a user enable/disable individual lead thresholds, set the in-app display format for due/overdue state, and preview the redacted copy (no private fields per INV-4, §"Server logs MUST NOT contain" list in ADR-0004).
- Explicit rendering of the three in-app due states — due-soon, due, past-due — with copy that does not imply anything arrives while the app is closed.
- Persistence of reminder rules and preferences through the ADR-0004 hydration protocol: the `reminders` object store (keyed by `reminder_id`, UUIDv4) and the `prefs` singleton in IndexedDB `dos_local`; every write is the specified `pushEvent → hook write-back → ack` round trip.
- The specified correctness protocol: cold-load hydration, reconnect re-hydration, timezone/DST/system-clock robustness, forecast-revision replan visibility, multi-tab consistency, and storage-unavailable/private-browsing/data-missing states rendered honestly.
- Explicit retirement notes for the pre-pivot OS delivery-reliability content (see FR-2, FR-9, AC-7) so downstream cards do not silently inherit obligations that no longer belong here.

## Excluded

- OS-scheduled local notifications. `mob_notify` and every native scheduling adapter — deferred to the native re-entry (PIVOT-2026-08-01, ADR-0004 §"Trigger conditions that revive the native track").
- Killed-app delivery testing, permission-recovery flows tied to OS notification permission, and OS-delivered-time observability. All three were premises of pre-pivot FR-6/AC-3/AC-4 and are retired here (they cannot occur in a browser MVP).
- Web Push, service workers used for push, and any server-held per-device push subscription. Explicitly deferred to `DOS-M09-010` (PWA/Web Push go/no-go spike); adopting Web Push conflicts with the no-server-side-personal-record stance and requires its own ADR (ADR-0004 §"Consequences → Lost — honestly").
- Any server-side reminder record. Reminder rules and preferences live in IndexedDB only; the server holds them in `socket.assigns` for one socket lifetime and never elsewhere (INV-3, INV-24.1, ADR-0004 §"Enforcement").
- Multi-vehicle reminder views (M08).
- Server-side delivery receipts and remote observability of the reminder pipeline. INV-4 forbids the telemetry surface these would require.

## Functional requirements

- **FR-1 (reminder preferences surface).** The reminder-preferences LiveView renders the currently enabled lead thresholds, the in-app display format for due/overdue state, and the redacted privacy preview. It presents the current in-app due state (due-soon / due / past-due / none) alongside the next-forecast-due date derived from the current forecast revision. The surface renders from `socket.assigns` populated via the hydration protocol; nothing is polled and no data is fetched from a server-side personal record (there is none — INV-3, INV-24.1).
- **FR-2 (retire pre-pivot FR-6 — OS delivery worksheet).** The pre-pivot FR-6 ("requested UTC/local fire time, OS-delivered time when observable, app state, power-saving mode, permission … the product guarantees correct scheduling intent/reconciliation, not exact display time") is **retired** for the browser MVP because no OS scheduling occurs. The QA correctness protocol here records browser-appropriate fields only (see FR-6/FR-7 below and AC-4). Any UI copy that could imply out-of-app delivery is prohibited — an "expect a notification"-style phrase is a copy defect.
- **FR-3 (deep links to current truth).** In-app due tiles, banners, and reminder-preference entry links resolve through current-truth routing to the current vehicle's forecast state at render time; a stale URL never rewrites maintenance facts and never shows a value contradicted by IndexedDB.
- **FR-4 (permission-independent in-app due state).** The in-app due/overdue state is authoritative and MUST render correctly with no notification permission ever requested (there is none to request). This restates INV-17 (amended): in-app state is the whole promise, notifications are not a supplement here because there are no notifications.
- **FR-5 (due-soon definition and persistence).** Due-soon is defined by the user's earliest enabled lead threshold; if no lead is enabled, use 14 days for the in-app status only. A past-due state persists until a new oil change is recorded or the interval/profile changes; dismissing a banner is presentation state only and never rewrites maintenance facts (INV-5, ADR-0004 §"Storage layout" — `reminders` and `events` are separate stores).
- **FR-6 (hydration correctness gate — replaces pre-pivot FR-6).** On cold LiveView mount and on every reconnect, the in-app due state rendered from `socket.assigns` MUST equal the state computed from the IndexedDB payload passed through the current `Forecasting` domain. Hydration is idempotent (ADR-0004 §"Enforcement" idempotence test). Interactive reminder controls MUST NOT accept input before `assigns.local_state` transitions out of `:hydrating` (INV-24.3). The three distinct pre-hydration states `:hydrating`, `:empty`, and `:storage_unavailable` (and the fourth explicit `:data_missing` state when `dos_boot_state == "has_data"` but hydration returns zero records) MUST render differently; showing a returning user an empty-garage flash is a release blocker (ADR-0004 §"First-paint and empty-state rules").
- **FR-7 (timezone/DST/system-clock correctness).** The in-app due state MUST be correct across timezone changes, DST transitions, and system-clock adjustments made between the last mutation and the current render. The domain forecast produces UTC anchors; the presentation layer resolves them against the browser's current IANA time zone at render time and re-computes on `Time.zone` change events (from the LiveView JS hook watching `Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`). No stored value is a wall-clock local timestamp.
- **FR-8 (forecast-revision correctness).** Recording an oil change updates the domain forecast, writes the new events/reminders through the mutation round trip (INV-24.2), and re-renders the due state within the same LiveView interaction — no reload, no app restart. Optimistic UI is permitted only if a failed `local_store:ack` visibly reverts the change (INV-24.2, ADR-0004 §"Mutation round trip"). The pre-pivot AC-2 ("without requiring an app restart") is preserved in its browser form; there is no "restart" in a browser, so the browser-equivalent obligation is that the current LiveView reflects the new state before the user's next interaction.
- **FR-9 (retirement — killed-app and native adapter failure).** Pre-pivot AC-3 ("after native adapter failure") and pre-pivot AC-4 ("killed-app delivery" on physical iOS/Android) are **retired**. Neither state exists in the browser MVP: there is no native adapter, and a browser tab that is closed does not receive out-of-band delivery. These obligations move to the deferred native re-entry (ADR-0004 §"Trigger conditions that revive the native track"; PIVOT-2026-08-01, native re-entry holding set). This FR exists so future readers see the retirement is deliberate and is not lost in an edit.
- **FR-10 (two-tap reachability, AT-verified).** From the reminder-preferences surface, editing enabled leads, editing in-app display format, viewing the current forecast, and exporting the JSON payload are each reachable in at most two taps/keyboard actions with browser assistive technology enabled (screen reader + keyboard-only). Verified per the DOS-M09-009 browser/engine matrix and WCAG 2.2 AA rules the M02 rescope owns.
- **FR-11 (multi-tab consistency).** Two tabs of the origin sharing one IndexedDB MUST converge on the same in-app due state within one hydration round trip after either tab commits a reminder-preference or forecast-relevant write, per the `BroadcastChannel("dos_local_store")` + compare-and-set-on-`meta.seq` protocol in ADR-0004 §"Multi-tab consistency". A lost-update race MUST surface as the visible "reloaded from this browser's newer data" notice, not as silent divergence.
- **FR-12 (honest storage-loss messaging).** When IndexedDB is unavailable (private browsing, blocked storage) or has been evicted (`dos_boot_state == "has_data"` but hydrate returns empty), the reminder surface MUST render the explicit `:storage_unavailable` / `:data_missing` state — never an empty-garage or "no reminders yet" onboarding-from-zero flow. Export is offered prominently. Prohibited words in this UI carry over from ADR-0004 §"Export and import": "backup", "synced", "safe", "we'll remember", "your account". This restates INV-5 (amended emphasis) and INV-25.

## Technical implementation contract

### Components and ownership boundaries

- A reminder-preferences LiveView (module under `DigitalOilStickerWeb.Live.Reminders`) that mounts on the application shell after the `LocalStore` JS hook completes hydration. It reads `assigns.reminders`, `assigns.prefs`, and `assigns.forecast_state`; it does not fork forecasting or scheduling logic.
- Presentation-only components: the due-state view (renders due-soon / due / past-due copy from the `DOS-M06-004` status vocabulary) and the preferences form (renders enabled leads, in-app display format, privacy preview).
- The domain contexts (`DigitalOilSticker.Garage`, `.Forecast`) remain client-agnostic per ADR-0004 §Decision 6 and the static-check enforcement (§"Enforcement" bullet 2) — this LiveView is one client over them.
- The `LocalStore` JS hook (owned by `DOS-M09-002`/`DOS-M09-003`) is the only path in and out of IndexedDB.

### Data and persistence

- IndexedDB `dos_local`, stores `reminders` (per-`reminder_id` UUIDv4 records) and `prefs` (singleton) per ADR-0004 §"Storage layout". No new object stores are introduced by this card.
- The server persists nothing: no rows, no ETS entries, no cache. Personal reminder values live only in `socket.assigns` for the socket lifetime, per INV-3 and the ADR-0004 §"Enforcement" repo-list test (`[CatalogRepo]`, read-only).
- Banner dismissal is presentation state only (LiveView assigns), never written back to IndexedDB (FR-5).
- Export/import round-trips reminder rules and preferences through the ADR-0004 envelope shape; no separate serializer is defined here.

### Interfaces and events

- On `phx-mount`, the LiveView renders the skeleton with `assigns.local_state == :hydrating`.
- On `handle_event("local_store:hydrate", envelope, socket)`, the LiveView receives the reminder rules and preferences alongside the rest of the payload, validates via the ADR-0004 §"Validation" changeset, and transitions to `:loaded` / `:empty` / `:data_missing` / `:storage_unavailable`.
- On every reminder-preference mutation, the LiveView issues `local_store:put` with `{mutation_id, seq, upserts, deletes}` and waits ≤ 2000 ms for `local_store:ack` before rendering the saved state; a missed ack falls through to the persistent "Not saved to this browser" affordance (ADR-0004 §"Mutation round trip").
- The LiveView subscribes to the domain-side `forecast_changed` event (fired when a M05 oil-change service event is recorded) so the due-state view re-renders in the same LiveView interaction (FR-8).
- Timezone-change detection: the JS hook re-reads `Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone` on `visibilitychange` and `focus` and pushes a `local_store:tz_changed` event if it differs; the LiveView re-renders the due state (FR-7).

### Failure and recovery behavior

- `:storage_unavailable`, `:data_missing`, quota-exceeded, and `local_store:conflict` all render distinct, honest states per ADR-0004 §"Quota and eviction", §"Private browsing and blocked storage", and §"Multi-tab consistency". None of them silently degrade to an empty garage.
- Server-side validation failure of a reminder-rule record is non-fatal: the valid subset hydrates, the invalid record is held aside in the payload quarantine (ADR-0004 §"Validation"), and the UI shows what could not be read plus an export of the raw payload.
- `SECRET_KEY_BASE` rotation invalidates LiveView socket tokens; reconnects re-hydrate from IndexedDB. No reminder data is lost by a secret rotation (ADR-0004 §"Secrets").

### Security and privacy

- No third-party origin is contacted by this surface (INV-4; ADR-0004 §"Content Security Policy" — `connect-src 'self' wss://digitaloilsticker.com`).
- No reminder value, preference value, or forecast anchor appears in server logs. `handle_event` params for reminder events are dropped by the `filter_parameters`/Logger filter per ADR-0004 §"Logging".
- No stable per-user identifier is minted for the reminder surface (INV-3). The `tab_id` is ephemeral and never persisted.
- The reminder-privacy preview shows only user-meaningful state; the `docs/product` "disallowed private fields" list (exact mileage, notes, VIN, oil/filter purchase history, catalog IDs) never appears in reminder copy, logs, or the diagnostic export (AC-5).

### Accessibility and performance

- WCAG 2.2 AA over browser AT (screen reader + keyboard-only): logical heading order, focus management across state transitions, live-region announcements when the in-app due state transitions (`due-soon` → `due`, `due` → `past-due`), status conveyed by text and structure never color alone. Verified against the DOS-M02-002 rescope's design-system baseline.
- Two-tap reachability (FR-10) is verified with assistive technology enabled, not only mouse.
- First-paint payload budget applies (CONSTITUTION §9: first-visit ≤ 250 KiB compressed HTML+CSS+JS, total first-visit ≤ 500 KiB, zero third-party origins). Hydration for the reminders surface MUST NOT extend the p75 hydration budget of the shell.

## UX and content states

- Pre-hydration: `:hydrating` skeleton — never an empty-garage flash. Interactive controls are inert per INV-24.3.
- `:empty` (no reminders configured yet, no prior data): explicit "you have no vehicles" onboarding flow, distinct from `:hydrating` and from `:storage_unavailable`.
- `:loaded` primary states: due-soon (earliest enabled lead, or 14-day fallback if none enabled — in-app status only), due, past-due, none. Past-due persists until a new oil change or interval/profile change (FR-5).
- `:data_missing` (had data before, IndexedDB now empty): explicit "your browser storage appears to have been cleared" state with import offered — not an empty state (ADR-0004 §"First-paint and empty-state rules").
- `:storage_unavailable` (IndexedDB refused to open or probe failed): explicit "nothing is being saved and closing the tab loses everything" banner with export offered prominently (INV-5, INV-25, ADR-0004 §"Private browsing and blocked storage").
- Preferences form states: pre-hydration inert, saving (optimistic + affordance), saved, unsaved-honesty ("Not saved to this browser") on missed ack.
- The OLM/manual precedence warning remains visible in reminder-preferences contexts (inherited from `DOS-M06-004`).

## Test plan

### Automated

- LiveView tests for every primary state (`:hydrating`, `:empty`, `:loaded` with each of due-soon/due/past-due/none, `:data_missing`, `:storage_unavailable`), asserting the distinct render per ADR-0004 §"First-paint and empty-state rules".
- Hydration idempotence test: hydrate the same payload twice, assert identical assigns and zero `local_store:put` emissions (ADR-0004 §"Enforcement", AC-3).
- Mutation round-trip tests: reminder-preference change → `local_store:put` → simulated hook `local_store:ack` → saved state; and the ack-timeout path renders the "Not saved to this browser" affordance without silently retrying (INV-24.2).
- Multi-tab consistency test: simulate two sockets sharing storage; write in tab A, assert tab B rehydrates and its assigns converge within one round trip; assert compare-and-set-on-`meta.seq` aborts a stale write and surfaces the "reloaded from this browser's newer data" notice (FR-11).
- Timezone/DST/clock property tests: for a matrix of stored forecast anchors and observer timezones (including America/Los_Angeles across DST spring-forward and fall-back, and non-integer-hour zones), assert the rendered due-soon/due/past-due classification equals the domain-computed classification (FR-7).
- Forecast-revision replan test: record-oil-change service event fires `forecast_changed`, the LiveView re-renders, and the new due state matches the domain forecast (FR-8, AC-2).
- Log-scanning test from ADR-0004 §"Enforcement" run against a scripted reminder-heavy session, asserting no VIN / mileage / date / note / custom-name / `tab_id` / full-IP appears in any captured log line (AC-5).
- Export/import round trip test with hash check and replace/merge preview counts (AC-6).
- Accessibility structure tests: heading order, label association, live-region announcements on state transitions.

### Manual/browser matrix

- Browser/engine/OS correctness matrix per `DOS-M09-009` — hydration correctness on cold load, on reconnect after network drop, and after `SECRET_KEY_BASE` rotation; timezone/DST/system-clock correctness; multi-tab convergence; private-browsing and storage-blocked correctness; quota-exhausted behavior; export/import round trip. Every cell records browser, engine, OS, `persist_granted` result, hydration duration, and result. Any cell that mentions "killed-app delivery" is a defect (FR-9).
- Accessibility audit with browser AT (screen reader + keyboard-only) covering two-tap reachability (FR-10, AC-1).
- Usability check: users state the current due state and how to change enabled leads from the preferences surface alone (AC-1).
- Sign-off review by engineering, design, privacy, and QA.
- No physical native-device work: N/A — the physical-device matrix moved to the deferred native re-entry.

## Rollout and rollback

- Ships inside the normal Fly deploy image. There is no on-device or store-review path.
- A deploy drops every WebSocket; clients auto-reconnect with backoff and re-run hydration, which re-derives the in-app due state (ADR-0004 §"WebSocket and LiveView considerations"). The reminder surface MUST behave as if a reconnect is a routine event.
- Rollback is redeploying the previous image (ADR-0004 §"Catalog database on Fly"). No user data migrates because there is no server-side user data (INV-3). A rollback that reintroduces a pre-hydration-correctness regression fails AC-3 in the correctness matrix and is caught before the next release.
- Release gating: AC-3 / AC-4 / AC-5 / AC-6 results feed the rescoped `DOS-M07-004` browser/engine/OS matrix; the pre-pivot "physical device reliability worksheet" is not produced (AC-7).

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- Decision (fixed by ADR-0004 and CONSTITUTION 2.0.0 amended INV-17): the MVP ships in-app due state only. Notifications-out-of-app are not promised, not scheduled, and not implied by any copy. Any UI text that could read as "we'll alert you" is a copy defect.
- Decision (fixed by ADR-0004 §Decision 4 and §Enforcement): the server holds no reminder record. `assigns` for the socket lifetime is the only server-side presence, and the ADR-0004 repo-list test enforces it.
- Decision (fixed by PIVOT-2026-08-01 line 131 and this card's FR-9/AC-7): the OS delivery-reliability portion of the pre-pivot gate moves to the deferred native re-entry. It is not attempted here, and downstream cards inherit the reduced obligation, not the original one.
- Risk (ADR-0004 R1, restated with reminder-specific emphasis): browser eviction of infrequently-visited site storage means the user's reminder rules can vanish along with their garage. Mitigations already in the architecture: `navigator.storage.persist()`, honest storage-status UI, the `:data_missing` state, export nudges after meaningful writes. This card MUST NOT soften any of those.
- Risk (ADR-0004 R2, restated): hydration round trip is per-mount and per-reconnect; a slow or flaky hydration is directly visible as reminder-state churn. AC-3 measures this per cell; failing cells feed back into DOS-M09-009 and the shell's hydration budget.
- Risk (ADR-0004 R4): multi-tab last-write-wins at record granularity. For reminder preferences the loss is small (a re-choice of enabled leads); it is made visible per FR-11. If a real workflow reveals a materially wrong outcome, escalate to a card in M09 (per ADR-0004 R4 open question).
- Risk: users may still expect notifications despite honest copy. Beta triage (DOS-M07-006) must feed observed misunderstandings back into the copy owned by DOS-M06-004 / this card, rather than into a scope expansion that would reintroduce Web Push through the back door (that path requires DOS-M09-010 and its own ADR).
- Open question: whether the browser tz-change hook (FR-7) can rely on `visibilitychange` + `focus` alone or needs a periodic re-check. To be measured against the DOS-M09-009 matrix; if a periodic re-check is required its cadence and cost are recorded here.

## Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)

> This section preserves the card as chartered before the 2026-08-01 pivot to browser-first, and is retained for historical readability of the ADR record. The Outcome above is authoritative.

### Outcome (pre-pivot)

Users and QA have one place to inspect the next reminder, permission/application status, last reconciliation, and recovery actions; the integrated forecast-to-killed-app journey meets a documented reliability bar. The control center tells the truth about what is scheduled, what failed, and what the user can do — and the milestone's end-to-end reliability protocol passes on physical devices before beta.

### Why this matters and what it unblocks (pre-pivot)

Scheduling APIs can accept a request that later becomes stale, denied, or OS-delayed. Truthful status and an end-to-end gate are needed before beta. This issue closes milestone M06: it is the last child before the epic exit gate and it blocks the M07 release-gate issues `DOS-M07-004`, `DOS-M07-006`, and `DOS-M07-007`. Without it, users cannot distinguish "reminders are working" from "reminders silently failed," and QA has no documented protocol to certify reminder reliability for release.

### Context and source of truth (pre-pivot)

- Master prompt §3, rule 8 (local notifications only) and rule 7 (vehicle controls prevail — the control center inherits the OLM/manual precedence copy from `DOS-M06-004`).
- Master prompt §6, step 6 ("Denial never blocks the product") and step 8 ("Recording a new oil change closes the previous cycle, starts a new one, recomputes the forecast, and replaces obsolete notification requests atomically").
- Master prompt §7: `PermissionPrimer`, `DueStatus`, and consistent empty/error/recovery components; the redacted-acknowledgement and no-telemetry rules that shape local diagnostics.
- Master prompt §8: `scheduled_notifications` and `reminder_rules` fields (platform state, last error/reconciliation time, enabled flag) that back the status display.
- `DOS-M06-004` (status vocabulary and in-app due presentation), `DOS-M06-005` (desired plan, permission states, privacy preview, Test action), and `DOS-M06-006` (reconciliation states, journal, Retry semantics) — this issue is their integrating surface.
- Roadmap catalog entry "DOS-M06-007 — Complete the reminder control center and end-to-end reliability gate" in the master prompt (the canonical scope statement expanded here).

### Included (pre-pivot)

- Reminder summary: status, next reminder, cause/confidence, enabled leads, privacy preview, last successful reconciliation time.
- Edit/disable/test actions with honest result reporting.
- Permission/settings recovery flows.
- Current desired-versus-applied status presentation.
- Local tap history (bounded journal from `DOS-M06-006`).
- Overdue in-app fallback independent of notification permission.
- The release test protocol (documented QA reliability worksheet and matrix).

### Excluded (pre-pivot)

- A server delivery receipt.
- Marketing prompts.
- Remote observability.
- New scheduling/reconciliation mechanics (owned by `DOS-M06-005`/`DOS-M06-006`; this issue presents and exercises them).
- Multi-vehicle reminder views (M08).

### Preconditions (pre-pivot)

- `DOS-M06-004` is closed (status vocabulary, due presentation, and reviewed warning copy exist).
- `DOS-M06-005` is closed (desired plan, permission flow, privacy preview, Test action, and settings exist).
- `DOS-M06-006` is closed (reconciliation states, journal, and Retry semantics exist).
- Physical iOS and Android devices at the minimum and current supported OS versions are available for the integrated matrix.
- Sign-off owners identified for engineering, design, privacy, and QA.

### Functional requirements (pre-pivot)

- FR-1 (status summary): Show Notifications Off / Permission Needed / Scheduled / Needs Attention, next semantic local date/time, forecast cause/confidence, enabled leads, privacy preview, and last successful reconciliation time. Put detailed native IDs/logs only in local diagnostics.
- FR-2 (honest disable): "Disable" first updates desired state and attempts cancellation. If native cancellation fails, state that an old reminder may remain and provide Retry/settings guidance; do not claim success solely because the toggle changed.
- FR-3 (deep links and atomic cycle turnover): Every reminder and overdue banner deep-links to current vehicle truth. After the user records an oil change, immediately create a new forecast plan and cancel old due reminders in the same workflow boundary plus reconciliation.
- FR-4 (in-app state independence): The app always shows an in-app due/due-soon state derived from the forecast, regardless of permission. Notifications supplement this state and are not the only maintenance indication.
- FR-5 (due-soon definition and persistence): Define due-soon presentation from the user's earliest enabled lead; if no lead is enabled, use 14 days for in-app status only. A past date remains visible until a new oil change is recorded or the interval/profile changes; dismissing a banner does not rewrite maintenance facts.
- FR-6 (QA protocol): The QA protocol records requested UTC/local fire time, OS-delivered time when observable, app state, power-saving mode, permission, timezone, app version, forecast revision, and result. OS delivery may be delayed; the product guarantees correct scheduling intent/reconciliation, not exact display time.
- FR-7 (two-tap reachability): From the control center, the appropriate action for each state (enable, open permission recovery, retry reconciliation, edit leads/time, run test) is reachable in at most two taps.

### Technical implementation contract (pre-pivot)

#### Components and ownership boundaries (pre-pivot)

- A reminder control center LiveView surface composed from existing pieces: `DOS-M06-005` settings (leads, delivery time, privacy preview, Test), `DOS-M06-006` reconciliation status/Retry, and `DOS-M06-004` status vocabulary and warning component. It reads state; it does not fork scheduling or reconciliation logic.
- The overdue/due-soon in-app banner logic derives strictly from the current forecast revision (via `DigitalOilSticker.Forecasting` results) plus reminder-rule settings; no arithmetic in the view.
- Local diagnostics (native IDs, journal detail, per-ID errors) live behind a separate diagnostics view, not the primary summary (FR-1).
- The release test protocol document is a QA-owned deliverable versioned with the app.

#### Data and persistence (pre-pivot)

- Reads `reminder_rules`, `scheduled_notifications`, the reconciliation journal, and forecast snapshots; writes only through existing `DOS-M06-005`/`DOS-M06-006` service APIs (enable/disable, retry, test).
- Banner dismissal is presentation state only; it never rewrites maintenance facts (FR-5) — a dismissed banner's underlying due state persists in domain data.
- The local tap history view renders the bounded journal from `DOS-M06-006`; no new journal tables.

#### Interfaces and events (pre-pivot)

- Subscribes to `forecast_changed` and `reminders_reconciled` to keep the summary current without polling.
- Record-oil-change integration: the M05 service-event flow triggers, in the same workflow boundary, forecast recompute (`DOS-M06-003`), replanning (`DOS-M06-005`), and reconciliation (`DOS-M06-006`) so obsolete due reminders are retired without an app restart (FR-3, AC-2).
- Deep links from notifications and banners resolve through current-truth routing (inheriting `DOS-M06-005` FR-13 and `DOS-M06-006` FR-8).

#### Failure and recovery behavior (pre-pivot)

- Needs Attention state surfaces `DOS-M06-006` partial-failure results with a Retry action and honest wording; success is claimed only after journaled convergence (FR-2).
- Permission Needed state routes to the `DOS-M06-005` recovery flow (settings guidance or proven settings deep-link) and rechecks on return/resume.
- Native adapter failure never corrupts the in-app due state: the forecast-derived banner remains correct with permission denied and after adapter failure (AC-3).
- Malformed/stale deep links open home safely; the control center renders from current data even when the journal shows historical errors.

#### Security and privacy (pre-pivot)

- The summary shows only user-meaningful state; detailed native IDs/logs stay in local diagnostics (FR-1). No notification copy, log, or diagnostic export reveals disallowed private fields — exact mileage, notes, VIN, oil/filter purchase history, and catalog IDs stay out (AC-5, inheriting `DOS-M06-005` FR-5).
- No remote observability, telemetry, or server delivery receipts; all status derives from local journals and platform callbacks (Excluded list; §7 no-telemetry rule).
- The QA worksheet contains device/test metadata only, no personal data.

#### Accessibility and performance (pre-pivot)

- The control center meets the app accessibility baseline: status conveyed by text and structure (never color alone), logical heading order, focus management across state changes, screen-reader operable actions, and dynamic announcements when status transitions (for example, Scheduled → Needs Attention).
- The permission/recovery accessibility audit is a required evidence item for this issue.
- Two-tap reachability (FR-7) is verified with assistive technology enabled, not only touch.
- Summary rendering is immediate from local state; journal/diagnostic detail lazy-loads.

### UX and content states (pre-pivot)

- Primary states: Notifications Off (with enable path), Permission Needed (with recovery guidance), Scheduled (next semantic local date/time, cause/confidence, enabled leads, privacy preview, last successful reconciliation time), Needs Attention (nonalarming wording, Retry, settings guidance).
- Disable flow states: desired-state-updated → cancellation confirmed, or cancellation-failed honesty state ("an old reminder may remain") with Retry/settings guidance (FR-2).
- In-app due states (independent of permission): due-soon (earliest enabled lead, or 14 days when none enabled — in-app status only), due, past-due persisting until a new oil change or interval/profile change; dismissal hides the banner without rewriting facts (FR-4, FR-5).
- Tap history: bounded local list of received/handled taps with links to current truth.
- Test action states: rate-limited, clearly labeled test, honest result reporting (inherited from `DOS-M06-005` FR-12).
- The OLM/manual precedence warning remains visible in reminder settings contexts (inherited from `DOS-M06-004` FR-6).

### Acceptance criteria (pre-pivot)

- AC-1: A user can explain whether reminders are off, blocked, scheduled, or need repair and can reach the appropriate action in at most two taps.
- AC-2: Record-oil-change flow retires every obsolete future request and schedules the new plan without requiring an app restart.
- AC-3: In-app due state remains correct with permission denied and after native adapter failure.
- AC-4: Integrated matrix passes on the minimum and current supported iOS/Android versions and at least one physical device per OS, including killed-app delivery.
- AC-5: No notification copy, log, or diagnostic export reveals disallowed private fields.
- AC-6: The documented QA protocol (FR-6 fields) is executed and archived as the release reliability worksheet, with every deviation dispositioned.

### Test plan (pre-pivot)

#### Automated (pre-pivot)

- End-to-end forecast-edit-to-native-schedule tests: edit a forecast input, assert replanning, reconciliation, and control-center status updates through the fake adapter; record-oil-change turnover retires obsolete requests and schedules the new plan in one workflow (AC-2).
- Control-center LiveView tests for every primary, disable-flow, and in-app due state, including permission-denied and adapter-failure correctness of the due banner (AC-3).
- Notification content snapshot tests re-run at the integrated level to prove no disallowed private fields in copy, logs, or diagnostic export (AC-5).
- Due-soon boundary tests: earliest-enabled-lead derivation and the 14-day in-app default when no lead is enabled (FR-5).
- Deep-link routing tests for reminder taps and overdue banners to current vehicle truth (FR-3).

#### Manual/device matrix (pre-pivot)

- Actual-device reliability worksheet on the minimum and current supported iOS/Android versions, at least one physical device per OS: for each cell record requested UTC/local fire time, OS-delivered time when observable, app state (foreground/background/killed), power-saving mode, permission, timezone, app version, forecast revision, and result (FR-6, AC-4) — including killed-app delivery.
- Permission/recovery accessibility audit with screen readers on both platforms, including two-tap reachability with assistive technology (AC-1, FR-7).
- Usability check: users state whether reminders are off, blocked, scheduled, or need repair from the summary alone (AC-1).
- Sign-off review by engineering, design, privacy, and QA.
