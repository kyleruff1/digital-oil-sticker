## Outcome

The single-vehicle app is accessible, private, secure, supportable, performant, migration-safe, and demonstrably reliable on the declared iOS/Android matrix. Signed release builds and truthful store/Netlify materials can move through closed beta to staged production without introducing accounts or behavioral telemetry.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

M05 and M06 deliver a functionally complete single-vehicle MVP with forecasting and reminders, but "functionally complete" is not "releasable." M07 converts working software into a shippable product: it proves the privacy promises the product makes, certifies accessibility, enforces performance budgets against the real embedded-BEAM architecture, executes the cross-platform QA matrix that host ExUnit cannot cover, hardens CI and the supply chain so release artifacts are trustworthy, runs a privacy-preserving closed beta, and stages the production release with rehearsed rollback. This epic is the launch gate for v1 and the baseline that M08 post-MVP migrations build on. Nothing in M08 may begin against an unreleased, unhardened baseline.

## Included

Child issues tracked by this epic:

- `DOS-M07-001` — Threat-model and lock down the local-only privacy boundary: reviewed threat model, privacy data inventory, loopback hardening, retention/deletion policy, and store disclosure matrix.
- `DOS-M07-002` — Meet the mobile accessibility and inclusive-design release bar: VoiceOver/TalkBack, large text, contrast, reduced motion, focus, and target-size certification of every critical workflow.
- `DOS-M07-003` — Enforce startup, interaction, storage, memory, and battery budgets: measured budgets for the BEAM + Repo + loopback Phoenix + LiveView/WebView architecture with the full starter catalog and maximum supported local history.
- `DOS-M07-004` — Execute the cross-platform offline, migration, and notification QA matrix: risk-based device/OS/app-state/storage/locale/timezone/upgrade matrix on simulators/emulators and physical devices.
- `DOS-M07-005` — Establish CI, supply-chain, migration, and signed-build quality gates: PRs and release tags cannot produce distributable artifacts without repeatable quality/security checks across BEAM, assets, SQLite, native shells, dependencies, and secrets.
- `DOS-M07-006` — Run a privacy-preserving closed beta and defect triage program: TestFlight/Play testing tracks with explicit, minimal, actionable feedback and diagnostics.
- `DOS-M07-007` — Prepare, stage, monitor, and recover the production release: signed builds, Netlify static content, store metadata/disclosures, staged rollout, and rehearsed forward-fix/rollback.

## Excluded

- Accounts, cloud sync, remote telemetry, advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, and push infrastructure (non-negotiable rules 1, 8; child exclusions in DOS-M07-001 and DOS-M07-006).

- Legal guarantees outside reviewed counsel.
- Paid marketing and server telemetry.
- Any feature scope not stated in a child issue's Included list (change-control rule 10).

## Dependency rationale

Position in the M00→M08 DAG: M07 is blocked by M06 (forecasting and reminders must exist before they can be hardened, beta-tested, and released) and by M03 (release claims, data-quality sign-off, store disclosures, and the bundled catalog all depend on rights-cleared data). M07 blocks M08: post-MVP expansion (multi-vehicle UI, signed catalog updates, VIN scan) builds on the released v1 baseline, its store presence, and its migration history. Within the milestone, children 001–003 harden distinct release dimensions, 004 consolidates them into matrix evidence, 005 secures the build path, 006 validates with humans, and 007 ships — reflected in the child-level blockedBy edges.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- Risk: store policy requirements (target SDK, privacy manifests, closed-testing prerequisites) change between planning and release day; children 006 and 007 mandate re-verification against current policy immediately before acting rather than freezing stale assumptions.
- Risk: Mob 0.7.20 pre-1.0 status may surface late platform defects during the matrix or beta; severity gates (no open S1/S2) protect the release rather than schedule pressure.
- Risk: performance budgets may be missed by the embedded runtime; DOS-M07-003 requires reporting actuals and an approved ADR for any accepted exception rather than hiding the OTP runtime cost.
- Decision (recorded per child): OS backup posture (DOS-M07-001), reference devices and budget exceptions (DOS-M07-003), support matrix and waivers (DOS-M07-004), beta exit thresholds (DOS-M07-006), rollout stages (DOS-M07-007).
- Open question: none at epic level; child open questions are tracked in their own sections.
