## Outcome

Invited testers exercise real maintenance/reminder workflows through TestFlight and Google Play testing tracks, while feedback and diagnostics remain explicit, minimal, and actionable.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

Native killed-app reminders, catalog comprehension, real driving samples, and accessibility need longitudinal human validation. A structured beta prevents feedback from becoming unbounded scope. Lab evidence (DOS-M07-004) proves the app behaves; only humans over weeks can prove the forecast is comprehensible, reminders arrive in real life, and privacy copy is believable. The beta go/no-go report is a required input to the production release (`DOS-M07-007`).

## Functional requirements

- FR-1: Recruit a documented cross-section of iOS/Android versions, vehicle years/configurations, miles/km users, low/high driving patterns, and assistive-technology users. Do not collect more demographic data than necessary.
- FR-2: Verify the current Google account-specific closed-testing requirement immediately before scheduling; if applicable, plan tester count/duration with buffer rather than encoding a possibly changing number as product logic.
- FR-3: Beta onboarding says this is prerelease, explains local-only data/export/uninstall risk, warns to follow manual/OLM, and gives urgent safety/support guidance. Testers use nonfabricated vehicle data only if comfortable and may use supplied fixtures instead.
- FR-4: Missions cover exact vehicle selection/provenance, first service, three spaced readings where time permits, changing confidence, time-vs-mileage cause, notification grant/deny, background/killed delivery, timezone/clock change if feasible, record-new-service cancellation, export/import, reset, and accessibility.
- FR-5: Feedback form asks build/device/OS, expected/actual, reproduction, severity impact, and consent before attaching the redacted local diagnostic export. Users review files before sending through their chosen external channel; the app never uploads automatically.
- FR-6: Triage twice weekly: safety/data-loss/privacy/security first, then core correctness, access, reliability, and polish. Feedback that requests new scope becomes a separately labeled discovery issue and cannot enter the RC without change control.
- FR-7: Enforce exit thresholds: all planned critical missions completed by representative devices; no S1/S2 open; forecast comprehension target defined and met; notification schedule/delivery intent failures understood; import/upgrade success demonstrated; support/privacy copy issues resolved. Record denominators, not only anecdotes.
- FR-8: Publish a beta policy covering tester data handling, access, retention, and offboarding, and close/retain tester data according to it.

## Technical implementation contract

### Components and ownership boundaries

- Beta program artifacts (plan, cohort matrix, missions, feedback template, SLA, triage log, go/no-go report): owned by the QA workstream with product participation in triage.
- Distribution: TestFlight and Google Play closed-testing track configuration owned by release management using DOS-M07-005 signed artifacts; no side-channel APK/IPA distribution.
- App changes: none required beyond what already shipped; the redacted diagnostic export from M06/DOS-M07-001 is reused as-is. Any app change requested by beta findings flows through normal change control and the CI gates.
- Support route: a monitored contact channel with the stated SLA; ownership named before invitations.

### Data and persistence

- Tester-provided data lives on tester devices (local-first, unchanged). The program stores only: feedback submissions, consented diagnostic files, triage records, and minimal tester contact/cohort attributes (FR-1 minimality).
- Diagnostic exports are redacted per the DOS-M07-001 allowlist; a redaction audit verifies actual files.
- Retention: program data is closed/retained per the published beta policy (FR-8) and never becomes production analytics.

### Interfaces and events

- Distribution interface: TestFlight and Play closed-testing tracks; build promotion follows the DOS-M07-005 protected pipeline.
- Feedback interface: the FR-5 form plus the tester's chosen external channel for consented file sharing; the app itself has no upload path.
- Triage interface: each report is mapped to a build and classified (defect with DOS-M07-004 severity, research note, duplicate, or out-of-scope discovery item) within the stated SLA.

### Failure and recovery behavior

- Data-loss or privacy-affecting reports jump the queue (FR-6 order) and can pause the beta: a documented halt path exists (pull the build from tracks, notify testers, provide export/recovery guidance).
- If a tester's local data is at risk from a defect, support guidance prioritizes export before any suggested remediation, consistent with the no-silent-reset rule.
- Failed store-track review/processing is handled with buffer time (FR-2) rather than schedule-driven shortcuts.

### Security and privacy

- No telemetry, analytics, session replay, or automatic log upload — the app never transmits diagnostics; only testers do, deliberately, after review (FR-5).
- Tester communications and stored program data are access-controlled to the named program owners; demographic collection is minimized (FR-1).
- Beta builds contain the same hardened configuration as release candidates (no debug backdoors for beta).
- Offboarding closes access and disposes of program data per the published policy (FR-8, AC-5).

### Accessibility and performance

- The cohort includes assistive-technology users (from DOS-M07-002 FR-9), and missions include accessibility validation with consent.
- Beta feedback on responsiveness is triaged against the DOS-M07-003 budgets; a reproducible budget miss on a supported device is a defect, not an anecdote.

## UX and content states

- Beta onboarding content (prerelease status, local-only data/export/uninstall risk, follow manual/OLM warning, urgent safety/support guidance) — written for this program; plain language; accessible.
- Feedback template states (complete/incomplete, consent given/withheld for diagnostics).
- The app under test presents its normal production UX; supplied fixture vehicles are available for testers uncomfortable using real data.
- Support responses follow the SLA and never promise production features (Excluded: production promises).

## Test plan

### Automated

- N/A — program-execution issue; the distributed builds already carry the automated coverage from DOS-M07-004/DOS-M07-005. Program-side automation is limited to feedback intake/triage tracking (report-to-build mapping completeness checks).

### Manual/device matrix

- Dry run: program owners complete every mission end-to-end on Tier-1 devices using the actual tester instructions before invitations go out.
- Redaction audit: inspect a sample of real consented diagnostic exports for allowlist compliance.
- Track verification: install from TestFlight and the Play closed track on physical devices; confirm build/version mapping.
- Mission coverage tracking across the cohort matrix (device/OS/vehicle/driving-pattern/assistive-technology segments).
- Triage cadence held twice weekly with logged outcomes; SLA adherence sampled.

## Rollout and rollback

Beta rollout is invitation-based and staged by cohort; expansion only after the intake/triage loop demonstrably works. Rollback = halting the beta: remove or stop distributing the build on both tracks, notify testers with guidance (including export instructions if data is at risk), and record the halt cause. A new beta build re-enters through the DOS-M07-005 pipeline and DOS-M07-004 smoke evidence. Offboarding at program end follows the published policy.

## Dependency rationale

- `DOS-M05-007`, `DOS-M06-007`: testers must exercise the finished MVP and forecasting/reminder experience; a beta on incomplete flows wastes the one-shot attention of recruited humans.
- `DOS-M07-001`: externally distributing a build with an unverified privacy boundary would contradict the product's core promise and store declarations.
- `DOS-M07-002`: assistive-technology testers require the accessibility bar to be met first.
- `DOS-M07-003`: known performance regressions would pollute all feedback.
- `DOS-M07-004`: matrix evidence for the distributed build separates "new human finding" from "known lab gap".
- `DOS-M07-005`: only signed, gate-passing artifacts may reach external devices.
- This issue blocks `DOS-M07-007`: the go/no-go report and resolved beta findings are required release inputs.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- Risk: Google's account-specific closed-testing requirements (tester counts/duration) may change; FR-2 mandates verification immediately before scheduling and buffer planning instead of encoding a number.
- Risk: low tester engagement leaves missions uncompleted; mitigated by cohort buffer, mission scoping ("three spaced readings where time permits"), and denominator tracking that exposes gaps honestly.
- Risk: feedback scope creep; FR-6's change-control rule routes new scope to labeled discovery issues outside the RC.
- Decision required: the forecast comprehension target (metric and threshold) must be defined before invitations so the exit evaluation is predetermined (FR-7).
- Decision required: support SLA values and triage-owner roster.
- Open question: feasibility of timezone/clock-change missions for real testers ("if feasible"); where infeasible, the DOS-M07-004 lab evidence remains the authority and the mission is marked lab-covered.
