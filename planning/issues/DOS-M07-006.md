> **RESCOPED FOR BROWSER MVP — 2026-08-01.** [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) established the browser-first MVP. This card is retained with the same identity; its Outcome and FRs below are the rescoped spec. The pre-pivot spec is preserved at the bottom under "Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)". Disposition per [PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md](../../docs/governance/PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md#issue-by-issue-classification): _Beta distribution moves from TestFlight/Play tracks to an unlisted staging hostname or invite link, and defect intake must not collect browser storage contents._

## Outcome

Invited testers exercise real garage, forecast, in-app due-state, and export/import workflows against a rehearsed, unlisted **staging hostname of the hosted Phoenix LiveView application** — reached through a one-off invite link, not a store track — while feedback and diagnostics remain deliberate, minimal, and never sweep up the anonymous personal records the browser holds locally. A written go/no-go report compares the beta evidence against predetermined exit thresholds and lists residual risks and their owners, and is the required input into the release-cutover work in [DOS-M07-007](DOS-M07-007.md).

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

The browser MVP's central product bets — that catalog comprehension, honest storage-loss messaging, and in-app due/overdue state are enough for a quarterly-usage product, and that users accept a hosted-URL delivery model without reading "hosted" as "my data is on their server" (ADR-0004 R11, INV-25) — cannot be validated by lab evidence alone. They need real people, over weeks, on their own browsers, on their own devices, exposed to the honest degradation shapes ADR-0004 §"Consequences — Lost" enumerates (browser storage eviction, private-browsing, quota, multi-tab, reconnect, deploy-drop of every socket). Lab evidence in the rescoped [DOS-M07-004](DOS-M07-004.md) matrix proves the app behaves under those conditions; only humans over calendar weeks can prove the product is comprehensible, the copy is believed, and the anonymous-client-storage story survives contact with users who did not read the ADR.

A structured beta bounds this: it prevents feedback from becoming an unowned wishlist, it maps every finding to a build and a severity, and it produces the predetermined go/no-go artifact the rescoped release cutover in [DOS-M07-007](DOS-M07-007.md) needs to justify pointing `digitaloilsticker.com` at the deploy. It also completes the trust half of the rescoped [DOS-M07-001](DOS-M07-001.md) threat model: a public host with anonymous client storage can only claim in the field what a real invited cohort has tried to break; without the beta the "no server-side personal record" claim is a design intent, not a lived one.

## Excluded

- **No TestFlight, no Google Play closed testing, no store tracks of any kind.** Native distribution is retired for the MVP (INV-19 amended); the beta ships from a hosted URL. All references to store review, tester counts required by Google's closed-testing policy, and TestFlight/Play track configuration are removed and preserved only in the historical section (sweep finding 1).
- **No collection of the tester's browser storage contents.** Defect intake does not accept raw IndexedDB exports, `dos_local` envelopes, per-vehicle records, event/reading rows, or any file that would materially reconstruct the user's garage on a triager's machine. The opt-in diagnostic summary is the *only* attachment channel and is redaction-bounded by [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md) FR-11 (sweep finding 5, per the pivot disposition line quoted in the banner).
- **No embedded analytics, session replay, third-party error reporting, tag manager, or automatic log upload.** INV-4, INV-22, and ADR-0004 §"Security and privacy boundary" all bind; third-party error reporting is specifically prohibited because `socket.assigns` transiently holds the user's garage.
- **No OS-notification, killed-app-delivery, timezone/clock-change, or background-scheduled reminder missions.** `mob_notify` and OS-scheduled local notifications do not exist in the browser (INV-17 amended); rescoped [DOS-M06-006](DOS-M06-006.md) is deferred to a native re-entry; there is nothing scheduled to reconcile. The rescoped mission set exercises only in-app due/overdue state (sweep finding 2).
- **No "accessibility with consent" cohort framed against native VoiceOver/TalkBack.** Accessibility validation runs against the browser assistive-technology matrix owned by rescoped [DOS-M07-002](DOS-M07-002.md); testers use browser AT and desktop-keyboard-only paths, not native OS AT APIs (sweep finding 3).
- No production promises; no feature voting that bypasses roadmap governance; no user-generated content published off the tester's browser.
- No Web Push subscription, service-worker install, or PWA install path — all deferred per rescoped [DOS-M09-009](DOS-M09-009.md).

## Functional requirements

- **FR-1: Cohort recruitment matrix (browser MVP).** Recruit a documented cross-section of the [DOS-M09-008](DOS-M09-008.md) browser support matrix (evergreen Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari on desktop; iOS Safari 16+ and Android Chrome on mobile) crossed with real vehicle years/configurations within INV-8's rolling 30-year U.S. window, low/high driving patterns, and browser-assistive-technology users (screen reader on macOS/iOS/Windows/Android, keyboard-only desktop, high-contrast/high-zoom, reduced motion). Demographic collection is minimized: cohort attributes recorded are browser + engine + OS + form factor, coarse driving band, and AT profile if applicable — no name, address, VIN, or persistent identifier tied to a person beyond the invitation channel. _Retires the pre-pivot FR-1 iOS/Android version framing and its "collect no more demographic data than necessary" clause is carried forward verbatim in intent._
- **FR-2: RETIRED — Google account-specific closed-testing rules do not apply.** The pre-pivot FR-2 required verifying the Google account-specific closed-testing tester count and duration immediately before scheduling and planning with buffer. Under the browser MVP there is no Google Play track, no Apple TestFlight track, no store review, no tester-count-and-duration policy, and no scheduling contingency tied to a store's evolving rules (INV-19 amended, ADR-0004 §"Decision" 1). The retirement is recorded here rather than silently deleted so that no future ticket revives it (sweep finding 1). The buffer-planning wisdom the pre-pivot FR-2 encoded — reserve schedule slack for platform-side unknowns — carries over into FR-3 as buffer against Fly-side deploy/DNS/TLS anomalies on the staging hostname, and into FR-8's evaluation window.
- **FR-3: Distribution via unlisted staging hostname on Fly (browser MVP).** The beta build is promoted through the [DOS-M07-005](DOS-M07-005.md) signed pipeline into a **distinct Fly.io application** (separate app name, separate release identifiers, separate secrets from production) served on a **non-guessable staging hostname** under the [DOS-M09-006](DOS-M09-006.md) Fly/Netlify domain boundary (e.g. an unpublished subdomain of `digitaloilsticker.com`). Access is invite-only: the invite link either carries a signed one-off entry token that is exchanged for a session-scoped access grant on the LiveView socket connect, or the hostname sits behind an IP allowlist / HTTP basic-auth gate scoped to the invitation channel — the exact mechanism is chosen at implementation time from options that (a) do not create a durable per-person server record (INV-3, INV-23), (b) do not carry a personal parameter in the request stream (INV-26), and (c) do not exempt the beta hostname from INV-22 (HTTPS/WSS, HSTS, CSP, `check_origin`, session-cookie rules). The staging hostname is **not** linked from production copy or search-engine-indexed content (`X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` plus a `robots.txt` that disallows it, and no inbound link from `digitaloilsticker.com` or the Netlify static content). No side-channel APK, IPA, or installer of any kind is distributed. _Directly replaces the pre-pivot FR-2/FR-3/"Distribution" description of TestFlight and Google Play closed-testing tracks and the "no side-channel APK/IPA" clause (sweep finding 1)._
- **FR-4: Beta onboarding content (browser MVP).** Onboarding states, in plain language, all of the following on a screen the tester must read before invitation acceptance: (a) this is a pre-release build; (b) all personal records live in this browser only, and clearing browser data, storage eviction, private-browsing sessions, switching browsers, or switching devices will lose them, with no server copy and no recovery beyond the export in [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md) (INV-5, INV-25, INV-23); (c) the app never notifies while the tab is closed and there is no background delivery of any kind (INV-17 amended); (d) the vehicle owner's manual and any oil-life monitor prevail if they conflict with the app (INV-14); (e) urgent safety issues go to a named support channel with the SLA in FR-7. Testers use their real vehicles' data only if they are comfortable; supplied non-fabricated INV-15-clean fixture vehicles are offered as an alternative and the fixture provenance is stated. _Consolidates the pre-pivot FR-3 onboarding requirements and rewrites its "local-only data/export/uninstall" language for the browser-storage loss modes above._
- **FR-5: Scripted missions (browser MVP).** The mission set exercises the flows that the browser MVP actually ships and drops the flows that no longer exist:
  - Included missions: hydration from empty and populated states (INV-24.1/.3, [DOS-M09-002](DOS-M09-002.md)); exact-vehicle selection with provenance and INV-11 support-status labels; first service and three spaced readings where time permits; forecast confidence transitions and their explainability copy; in-app due/overdue rendering and the honesty statement that nothing arrives while the tab is closed; export → clear-browser-data → import round trip in the tester's real browser ([DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md)); reset with the category-and-count preview; a private-browsing invocation on a supported browser to observe the storage-unavailable state (INV-24.6, [DOS-M09-003](DOS-M09-003.md)); a multi-tab mission (two tabs, one edits, the other observes the `BroadcastChannel` update, INV-24.7); a socket-disconnect-and-reconnect mission (throttle, sleep/wake, or a deploy during the tester's session) that must not lose data (INV-24.1, INV-7 amended); a browser-assistive-technology walkthrough per the AT profile the tester was recruited under.
  - Excluded missions: notification grant/deny, background delivery, killed-app delivery, timezone or clock-change reconciliation of scheduled notifications, and any OS-notification-permission-flow variant — none apply in the browser MVP (INV-17 amended). Rescoped [DOS-M06-006](DOS-M06-006.md) is deferred to native re-entry; nothing scheduled exists in the browser to reconcile. _Directly replaces the pre-pivot FR-4 mission list (sweep finding 2)._
- **FR-6: Feedback intake and prohibition on browser-storage collection (browser MVP).** The feedback form collects: build (release identifier from [DOS-M07-005](DOS-M07-005.md)/[DOS-M09-005](DOS-M09-005.md)), browser + engine + version, OS, viewport size, AT profile if applicable, expected vs. actual, reproduction steps, and severity impact. It **MUST NOT** ask for, accept, or provide any upload widget for: the tester's IndexedDB database, a `dos_local` envelope, an export file produced by [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md), a screenshot that visibly contains VIN/odometer/service dates/note text/custom vehicle names, or any material that would let a triager reconstitute the tester's garage. Testers who want to share their state with support are asked to describe the shape (counts, symptoms) rather than the contents, and the form's copy states this explicitly. The **only** permitted diagnostic attachment is the opt-in redacted `.txt` summary produced by rescoped [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md) FR-11 (record counts, browser identification, `navigator.storage.estimate()` output, `meta.persist_granted`, hydration timings — no personal record contents). Testers review the text before sending it through their chosen external channel; the app itself has no upload path and never transmits diagnostics automatically (INV-4). _Directly implements the pivot disposition line's second half — "defect intake must not collect browser storage contents" — and retires the pre-pivot FR-5 assumption that a redacted local diagnostic export was itself the attachment (sweep finding 5)._
- **FR-7: Triage cadence, severity rules, and change control.** Triage twice weekly. Order of business: (1) data-loss, privacy, or security findings first — any report suggesting a personal value reached a log line, an error page, a server-side row, or another origin jumps every other queue and can pause the beta (see FR-9 halt path); (2) correctness of the surviving MVP capability set — hydration honesty (INV-24.3), export/import (INV-5), catalog resolve (INV-11/INV-15), in-app due state (INV-17 amended); (3) accessibility and browser-matrix findings; (4) polish. Feedback that requests new scope becomes a separately labeled discovery issue outside the RC and cannot enter the release without an explicit change-control decision. Support SLA and triage-owner roster are named before invitations go out. _Carries pre-pivot FR-6 forward with the queue-jump rule updated to the hosted threat surface._
- **FR-8: Predetermined exit thresholds (browser MVP), evaluated with recorded denominators.** The go/no-go compares evidence to the following thresholds, agreed and written down **before invitations** so evaluation is not post-hoc:
  - **Missions:** every mission in FR-5 completed end-to-end by at least one representative tester in each Tier 1 cell of the [DOS-M09-008](DOS-M09-008.md) matrix, with denominators (missions attempted / missions completed per cell) recorded.
  - **Severity:** zero open S1/S2 defects on the beta build; every S3 has an owner and a written disposition (fix, defer with rationale, discovery item).
  - **Hydration and forecast comprehension:** a predetermined comprehension metric and threshold for the forecast/confidence and in-app due-state copy is defined and met (the specific metric — e.g. a scripted-question success rate — is decided before invitations under "Decisions" below).
  - **Storage-loss messaging:** at least one tester per cell articulates in their own words that clearing browser data destroys the record and that there is no server copy (INV-5, INV-25); any tester who instead expresses a belief that the record is on the server is a defect in the [DOS-M09-003](DOS-M09-003.md) copy and blocks release.
  - **Export/import:** every tester who ran the round-trip mission recovered their state; any failure is a defect against [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md).
  - **Privacy on the wire:** no report of a network capture showing a personal parameter in a request (INV-26); a spot-check by the program owners against a live tester session confirms this before the go/no-go is signed.
  - **Logging:** the ADR-0004 §"Enforcement" scripted-session log gate (authored under [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md) FR-12, deployed under [DOS-M09-007](DOS-M09-007.md)) is green on the beta build and re-run against the exact release identifier the beta terminates on.
  _Replaces the pre-pivot FR-7 exit thresholds' notification-delivery and store-track clauses; carries forward the "record denominators, not only anecdotes" clause verbatim in intent._
- **FR-9: Halt path and beta policy (browser MVP).** A documented halt path exists: on a confirmed data-loss, privacy, or security finding, the program owners (a) stop routing new invitations, (b) pull the beta build's Fly application out of routing (scale-to-zero of the *staging* app only; production is unaffected), (c) notify existing testers with export guidance from [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md), and (d) record the halt cause. A new beta build re-enters through [DOS-M07-005](DOS-M07-005.md) and [DOS-M09-005](DOS-M09-005.md). A published beta policy covers tester consent, program-side data handling and access (contact metadata and cohort attributes only; no browser-storage contents held by construction, per FR-6), retention timelines, and offboarding. Offboarding closes access at the invite-gate mechanism chosen in FR-3, deletes program-held contact records per the policy retention window, and never becomes production analytics (INV-4). _Consolidates pre-pivot FR-8 and the pre-pivot Rollback/Rollout paragraphs, rewritten off store-track pull to Fly-side scale-to-zero of the staging app._
- **FR-10: Program-side integrity of the beta channel.** The staging application inherits every INV-22 boundary the production application enforces (HTTPS/WSS, HSTS after certificate verification, first-party CSP, `check_origin` restricted to the staging hostname, session-cookie rules, per-IP and per-socket rate limits) and the FR-3 invite-gate mechanism MUST NOT loosen any of them. LiveDashboard remains disabled on the staging app (ADR-0004 §"LiveDashboard and operator access"). Console access to the staging machine follows the same operational rule as production: no routine attachment to inspect a live tester's socket. If a support triager needs to inspect a tester's state, they ask the tester to run the FR-6 diagnostic summary and describe the shape — they never `fly ssh console` into an active session. _New under the pivot; there was no pre-pivot analogue because the pre-pivot beta ran on OS-sandboxed devices without a shared server surface._

## Technical implementation contract

### Components and ownership boundaries

- Beta program artifacts (plan, cohort matrix, missions script, feedback template, SLA table, triage log spreadsheet, published beta policy, go/no-go report) are owned by the QA workstream with product participation in triage. They live under `planning/beta/2026-mvp/` and are versioned in git alongside the release evidence.
- The staging Fly.io application (name, region, secrets, invite-gate configuration) is owned by release management using the [DOS-M07-005](DOS-M07-005.md) signed image and the [DOS-M09-005](DOS-M09-005.md) deploy pipeline. The application is a distinct Fly app from production and its release identifiers are recorded separately in the release ledger.
- No modifications to the application under test are required by this card beyond what already shipped under [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md) (the FR-11 opt-in diagnostic summary) and the M09 hardening set. If beta feedback requests an application change, it flows through the normal change control and re-enters through the CI gates.
- The support/feedback channel (contact ownership, SLA measurement, triage tool of choice) is named before invitations. Findings are transcribed into the triage log; the underlying channel is not archived as program evidence beyond what the SLA measurement requires (INV-4).
- **Deleted for the browser MVP:** any component that was to broker distribution through TestFlight, App Store Connect, Google Play Console, or a Play closed-testing track; any component that was to configure Google account-specific closed-testing tester counts; any component that was to gather a redacted diagnostic *file* from the tester's device and route it through a share sheet.

### Data and persistence

- Tester-provided personal records (garage, service history, odometer readings, reminder intent, preferences) live only in the tester's browser IndexedDB `dos_local` database (INV-23, ADR-0004 §"Storage layout"). The program stores **none** of it — not in a triage tool, not in an email archive, not in a spreadsheet, not in a screenshot.
- The program stores: feedback submissions (fields per FR-6, no personal record contents by construction), opt-in redacted diagnostic `.txt` summaries when the tester chose to attach one, triage records (report → build → severity → disposition mapping), and minimal cohort attributes (browser + engine + OS + form factor, coarse driving band, AT profile) linked to the invitation channel identity (email or handle) required to reach the tester.
- Retention: the published beta policy (FR-9) fixes the retention windows for each of the above categories and the offboarding procedure that terminates them. Nothing on the program side ever becomes production analytics.
- The staging Fly application, like production, has exactly one Ecto repo, `DigitalOilSticker.CatalogRepo`, opened read-only (ADR-0004 §"Decision" 4). The [DOS-M09-007](DOS-M09-007.md) release-gating test asserts this on the beta build.

### Interfaces and events

- **Distribution interface:** the FR-3 invite link → invite-gate mechanism → staging hostname session on the Fly application. Build promotion is a [DOS-M09-005](DOS-M09-005.md) deploy against the staging app with the [DOS-M07-005](DOS-M07-005.md) signed image; no external tooling is in the path.
- **Feedback interface:** the FR-6 form served either from the staging Netlify boundary ([DOS-M09-006](DOS-M09-006.md)) or from an owner-selected form tool — in either case the fields and prohibitions of FR-6 bind, and the tool is configured to disallow file attachments other than `.txt` (or disallow attachments entirely and instruct the tester to paste the summary inline). The tester's chosen channel for delivering the opt-in `.txt` summary (email, direct message, ticket comment) is theirs; the app itself never uploads.
- **Triage interface:** each report is mapped to a specific Fly release identifier and the browser + engine + OS + AT cell of the [DOS-M09-008](DOS-M09-008.md) matrix, and classified as one of {S1 defect, S2 defect, S3 defect, research note, duplicate, out-of-scope discovery item} within the FR-7 SLA.
- **Halt interface:** a named runbook lists the exact `fly` commands (scale-to-zero of the staging app), the invitation-channel notification template, and the export-guidance link from [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md).

### Failure and recovery behavior

- Data-loss or privacy findings jump the queue (FR-7) and can pause the beta (FR-9). A tester's local data being at risk from a defect means the support answer prioritizes running the [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md) export **before** any suggested remediation — consistent with the "export is the only recovery path" rule and INV-5/INV-25.
- Fly-side deploy, DNS, or TLS anomalies on the staging hostname (a certificate provisioning delay, a proxy misconfiguration, a stuck release) are handled with buffer time in the FR-8 evaluation window rather than schedule-driven shortcuts. The rescoped buffer inheritance from retired FR-2 lands here.
- A tester who cannot reach the staging hostname (invite-gate misconfigured, ad blocker eating the invite link, corporate firewall blocking WSS) is a **research signal about production reachability**, not just a support ticket: their case is triaged as a matrix finding as well as a support case.
- If the invite-gate mechanism itself is found to leak a personal parameter, a durable identifier, or a rate-limit bypass (contradicting FR-3's INV-3/INV-22/INV-26 constraints), that is an FR-9 halt event.

### Security and privacy

- No telemetry, no analytics, no session replay, no automatic log upload — the app never transmits diagnostics; the tester does, deliberately, after reviewing an opt-in `.txt` summary (INV-4, FR-6). Third-party error reporting is specifically prohibited (ADR-0004 §"No third-party anything") because `socket.assigns` transiently contains the tester's garage.
- Tester contact metadata and cohort attributes are access-controlled to named program owners; no ambient team-wide access. The published beta policy (FR-9) names the individuals and the offboarding disposition.
- The staging build carries the same hardened configuration as production release candidates: `debug_errors: false`, no LiveDashboard, no console-attach-to-live-session (FR-10, ADR-0004 §"LiveDashboard and operator access"). No debug backdoors are introduced for the beta.
- The invite-gate mechanism does not introduce a durable per-person server record (INV-3), does not carry a personal parameter in the request stream (INV-26), and does not weaken the INV-22 transport, CSP, session-cookie, or rate-limit boundaries (FR-3, FR-10).
- The staging hostname is not indexed by search engines and is not linked from production or Netlify static content (FR-3). A crawler that stumbles onto it hits the invite-gate mechanism first, not the LiveView.
- Logs on the staging app follow the ADR-0004 §"Logging" prohibitions and the [DOS-M09-007](DOS-M09-007.md) release-gating scripted-session assertion is re-run against the exact beta release identifier in FR-8.

### Accessibility and performance

- The cohort explicitly includes browser-AT users per FR-1, and the FR-5 mission set includes an AT walkthrough per profile. AT findings are triaged against the [DOS-M07-002](DOS-M07-002.md) WCAG 2.2 AA bar; a reproducible AT failure on a supported browser/AT combination is a defect, not an anecdote.
- Beta feedback on responsiveness is triaged against the [DOS-M07-003](DOS-M07-003.md) page-load, hydration, LiveView round-trip, and IndexedDB footprint budgets; a reproducible budget miss on a supported browser is a defect. Testers on marginal networks (mobile 3G/4G-class connections, coffee-shop Wi-Fi) are a valid signal about the LiveView round-trip cost that the ADR-0004 hosting decision makes central to the UX.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- **Risk (accepted).** A public hosted URL naturally reads to some users as "my data is on their server" (ADR-0004 R11). Mitigated by the FR-4 onboarding copy and the FR-8 storage-loss messaging paraphrase threshold, and by every INV-25 surface the app already ships. Not eliminated by any technical mechanism — it is a copy discipline the beta is designed to measure.
- **Risk (accepted).** Browser storage eviction (ADR-0004 R1) is the single largest product risk of the pivot, and the beta is exactly where its quarterly-usage profile might surface. A tester who returns after two weeks to find their garage evicted is a *research signal about R1*, not just a support ticket; triage treats it under FR-7's data-loss queue-jump rule.
- **Risk (accepted).** The invite-gate mechanism (FR-3) introduces a small operator surface distinct from production; a bug there could either lock legitimate testers out or admit uninvited traffic. Mitigated by the AC-2 end-to-end dry run and by the FR-10 constraint that the invite-gate MUST NOT weaken any INV-22 boundary.
- **Risk (open).** Some testers will attempt in good faith to attach browser-storage contents to feedback (thinking it helps triage). The FR-6 prohibition depends on program discipline to catch and delete on receipt; the attachment-type gate on the form is a mitigation but not a guarantee. Recorded here so the deletion-and-re-file procedure is treated as expected work, not an exception.
- **Risk (open).** Fly-side deploy, DNS, or TLS anomalies on the staging hostname could delay the beta window; the buffer clause inherited from retired FR-2 is the mitigation, but the residual risk is that the beta ships against a shorter effective window than planned.
- **Decision required.** The forecast/confidence and in-app due-state comprehension metric and threshold (FR-8) — the exact scripted-question form, the pass rate — must be decided and written down before invitations so the evaluation is predetermined.
- **Decision required.** The FR-3 invite-gate mechanism (signed-one-off-token vs. IP allowlist vs. HTTP basic-auth vs. combination) — chosen at implementation time against the FR-3 INV-3/INV-22/INV-26 constraints and recorded in AC-2's staging application configuration record.
- **Decision required.** Support SLA values (first-response, S1/S2 turnaround) and the named triage-owner roster.
- **Open question.** Whether a Tier 2 browser cell (older Safari, older Firefox ESR, an in-app browser variant) should be recruited into the beta or held for post-MVP; escalates to [DOS-M09-008](DOS-M09-008.md) support-tier policy.
- **Open question.** Whether the FR-6 opt-in `.txt` summary from [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md) FR-11 is worth its complexity in a beta of this size, or whether describing the shape in prose is sufficient. The rescoped [DOS-M05-007](DOS-M05-007.md) Risks section flags this same question in the reverse direction; a joint decision is recorded when this card and that card sign off.

Beta rollout is invitation-based and staged by cohort; expansion only after the intake/triage loop demonstrably works. Rollback = halting the beta: remove or stop distributing the build on both tracks, notify testers with guidance (including export instructions if data is at risk), and record the halt cause. A new beta build re-enters through the DOS-M07-005 pipeline and DOS-M07-004 smoke evidence. Offboarding at program end follows the published policy.

### Dependency rationale (pre-pivot)

- `DOS-M05-007`, `DOS-M06-007`: testers must exercise the finished MVP and forecasting/reminder experience; a beta on incomplete flows wastes the one-shot attention of recruited humans.
- `DOS-M07-001`: externally distributing a build with an unverified privacy boundary would contradict the product's core promise and store declarations.
- `DOS-M07-002`: assistive-technology testers require the accessibility bar to be met first.
- `DOS-M07-003`: known performance regressions would pollute all feedback.
- `DOS-M07-004`: matrix evidence for the distributed build separates "new human finding" from "known lab gap".
- `DOS-M07-005`: only signed, gate-passing artifacts may reach external devices.
- This issue blocks `DOS-M07-007`: the go/no-go report and resolved beta findings are required release inputs.

### Risks, decisions, and open questions (pre-pivot)

- Risk: Google's account-specific closed-testing requirements (tester counts/duration) may change; FR-2 mandates verification immediately before scheduling and buffer planning instead of encoding a number.
- Risk: low tester engagement leaves missions uncompleted; mitigated by cohort buffer, mission scoping ("three spaced readings where time permits"), and denominator tracking that exposes gaps honestly.
- Risk: feedback scope creep; FR-6's change-control rule routes new scope to labeled discovery issues outside the RC.
- Decision required: the forecast comprehension target (metric and threshold) must be defined before invitations so the exit evaluation is predetermined (FR-7).
- Decision required: support SLA values and triage-owner roster.
- Open question: feasibility of timezone/clock-change missions for real testers ("if feasible"); where infeasible, the DOS-M07-004 lab evidence remains the authority and the mission is marked lab-covered.
