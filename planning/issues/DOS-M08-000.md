## Outcome

A user can maintain many fully isolated vehicles, switch among them through an accessible tabbed garage, and receive correctly budgeted reminders for each. The data team can build, sign, publish, audit, roll back, and retire public catalog packs on Netlify; clients can verify and atomically activate them without risking private vehicle/history data or losing offline operation.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

M08 is the explicit post-MVP expansion milestone of the roadmap. The v1 product deliberately shipped a one-active-vehicle experience over multi-vehicle-safe internals (non-negotiable product rule 9) and a bundled, replace-on-app-update catalog only. This epic converts those reserved capabilities into shipped product:

- Multiple vehicles enabled in the UI, vehicle tabs/list switching, and per-vehicle reminders — the first item of the "Explicit post-MVP" scope boundary.
- Signed catalog updates from Netlify with atomic activation and rollback — the second item of that boundary, and the operational baseline every later incremental/delta catalog capability builds on.

Nothing after M08 exists in the bootstrap roadmap; completing this epic establishes the multi-vehicle product and the catalog-operations discipline (format contract, publishing pipeline, client updater) that all future catalog refreshes reuse.

- Cloud sync, shared garages, remote push, telematics, shop booking, commerce, ads, social features, and predictive maintenance beyond oil changes (master prompt §5) remain out of scope for M08 and the roadmap.

## Functional requirements

Entry-gate conditions:

- FR-1: M07 is closed: the production v1 schema, hardening gates, and release process are proven before any M08 child starts implementation.
- FR-2: No M08 capability has been backported into an MVP ticket; any MVP-era deviation discovered at entry is filed as a new issue, not absorbed silently (product rule 10).

Exit-gate conditions:

- FR-3: `DOS-M08-001` through `DOS-M08-007` are closed with attached evidence.
- FR-4: Multi-vehicle enablement is lossless for every v1 database: every historical single-vehicle database migrates with logically identical child records, and empty or preexisting-multiple-row databases have their specified deterministic result.
- FR-5: Tabs/garage manage supported vehicle counts accessibly, and all records, forecasts, and reminders remain vehicle-scoped.
- FR-6: Platform notification limits are enforced visibly through the deterministic allocator; no reminder coverage gap is silent.
- FR-7: Tampered, unsigned, or incompatible catalog packs are rejected by the client verifier; a valid Netlify pack can download, verify, activate, survive interruption, and roll back while the previous catalog remains usable offline.
- FR-8: `UserRepo` is never overwritten, migrated, or corrupted by any catalog operation delivered in this milestone.

## Dependency rationale

M08 is the terminal milestone of the M00→M08 DAG: M07 ("Hardening and release") → M08 ("Post-MVP expansion"). It must not begin before M07 because both workstreams depend on proven v1 baselines: the garage migration extends the production v1 `UserRepo` schema that M07 certified, and catalog operations extend the Netlify site and release controls from `DOS-M07-007`. Within the epic, `DOS-M08-001` gates the multi-vehicle chain (002 → 003 → 004) and `DOS-M08-005` gates the catalog chain (006 → 007); `DOS-M08-007` additionally consumes the multi-vehicle chain because catalog activation must preflight and remap saved vehicles and replan vehicle-scoped reminders. M08 items default to `Priority = P3 Low`, `Target = Post-MVP` until the v1 launch gate changes priority.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- Risk: singleton assumptions from M05 leaking into reused screens/contexts could corrupt cross-vehicle state; mitigated by the ownership audit and isolation property tests in `DOS-M08-001`/`DOS-M08-003`.
- Risk: OS pending-notification capacity is lower than the initial design budget; `DOS-M08-004` must verify against current `mob_notify`/OS behavior and use the lower value.
- Risk: format/signature mistakes in the pack contract are difficult to repair once clients ship; `DOS-M08-005` requires approval of the RFC and test vectors before updater implementation.
- Decision (deferred to children): approved maximum active garage count (50 proposed for product review) and visible tab threshold (5 proposed).
- Decision (deferred to `DOS-M08-003`): unit preference explicitly app-global or vehicle-scoped, by product decision and migration.
- Open question: post-M08 priority uplift timing — M08 stays `P3 Low` until the v1 launch gate changes priority.
