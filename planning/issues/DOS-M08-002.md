> **RESCOPED FOR BROWSER MVP — 2026-08-01.** [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) established the browser-first MVP. This card is retained with the same identity; its Outcome and FRs below are the rescoped spec. The pre-pivot spec is preserved at the bottom under "Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)". Disposition per [PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md](../../docs/governance/PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md#issue-by-issue-classification): _Respecify the tabbed pattern as an accessible web navigation pattern with real URLs per vehicle._

## Outcome

Users of the hosted browser application can add, identify, reorder, switch, archive, restore, replace, and delete vehicles inside an accessible web navigation pattern whose spine is **real per-vehicle URLs**. Selecting a vehicle changes the URL to `/garage/<vehicle_id>` (or an equivalent stable route keyed on the client-generated vehicle UUID from `DOS-M08-001`); that URL survives full-page reload, restores browser back and forward correctly, deep-links from an external tab or a shared link, and re-selects the same vehicle on LiveView reconnect after a network blip, sleep/wake, or a Fly.io deploy. The active vehicle is always obvious in the UI, and every mutating screen renders the active vehicle's identity near its title. The garage never lies about its state: during hydration nothing claims "no vehicles yet"; after hydration the loaded, empty, cleared-or-evicted, storage-unavailable, and session-only states are distinguishable (INV-24.3, INV-24.4, INV-24.6, INV-24.8, INV-25).

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

Multi-vehicle state is dangerous if users cannot tell which vehicle a mileage or service entry affects. In a hosted browser product this danger compounds: the URL is now a primary UI affordance — users share it, bookmark it, refresh it, and traverse it with the back button — and a navigation contract that ignores that surface will silently produce cross-vehicle writes when a bookmarked URL, a browser-restored session, or a reconnect lands on a stale active vehicle. The rescoped M08 milestone also runs against a genuinely new class of failures created by the pivot: pre-hydration first paint (INV-24.3), reconnect re-hydration (INV-7, INV-24.1), multi-tab consistency across a shared IndexedDB (INV-24.7), and storage-loss states that must never be shown as an empty garage (INV-24.4, INV-25). This card owns the garage navigation contract that those M09 mechanisms are rendered through, so it must be closed before `DOS-M08-003`'s per-vehicle isolation of history and forecasts can complete its user-facing surfaces.

## Included

- Real per-vehicle URLs keyed on the stable client-generated vehicle UUID from `DOS-M08-001`, honored on load, refresh, deep link, and browser back/forward.
- An accessible web navigation pattern for switching between active vehicles: a persistent vehicle switcher on the garage shell, an "All vehicles" index route, and a keyboard-first affordance for changing active vehicle.
- Add-vehicle flow driven by the impersonal catalog-query layer (INV-26), reusing the M05 catalog selection and provenance rules through their rescoped API.
- Active-context banner on every create/edit/service/reading screen.
- Manage/reorder/archive/delete flows (reorder via buttons/menu; drag is optional and never the only affordance).
- Empty-garage, cleared/evicted, storage-unavailable, and session-only garage states distinguishable in copy and behavior (INV-24.3/INV-24.4/INV-24.6/INV-24.8, INV-25).
- At-limit state driven by the persisted maximum active count from `DOS-M08-001`.
- Duplicate nickname disambiguation (append year/make/model).
- Deep links from external tabs, bookmarks, and shared links that explicitly select their vehicle by UUID.
- Persistence of active vehicle and ordering across ordinary browser reloads and LiveView reconnects, hydrated from IndexedDB per the ADR-0004 protocol.
- Multi-tab garage consistency: a switch/reorder/archive in one tab is reflected in others via the ADR-0004 `BroadcastChannel("dos_local_store")` mechanism (INV-24.7).
- Disconnected-shell behavior on the garage while the LiveView socket is down (ADR-0004 §"First-paint and empty-state rules"; INV-7).

## Functional requirements

- **FR-1 (replaces pre-pivot FR-1, addresses staleness finding 1):** The garage exposes each vehicle at a stable per-vehicle URL — canonical form `/garage/<vehicle_id>` — keyed on the client-generated UUIDv4 issued by `DOS-M08-001`. The URL is the source of truth for "which vehicle is active in this tab": on load, refresh, and back/forward the LiveView resolves the vehicle from the URL against the hydrated IndexedDB payload and renders that vehicle's panel; the URL is never derived from a positional index or a session cookie. Query strings and hashes carry no personal data (INV-26).
- **FR-2:** Switching vehicles is an accessible web navigation pattern with **no reliance on native tab-bar semantics**. Implementation MUST satisfy WCAG 2.2 AA (§9): full keyboard operability with a visible focus indicator, no keyboard traps, target size ≥ 24×24 CSS px, usable at 200% zoom and 320 CSS px reflow width. The pattern MUST render an accessible name for each vehicle in the form "Nickname, year make model" (or the disambiguated equivalent per FR-13) and MUST expose the active vehicle to browser assistive technology (VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS, Narrator) on the supported matrix (§9 browser support matrix, `DOS-M09-009`).
- **FR-3:** Browser history is a first-class concern. Vehicle switches use LiveView `push_patch`/`push_navigate` so browser back and forward move between vehicles as users expect, refresh restores the same vehicle, and bookmarked URLs open to the correct vehicle. External deep links and shared URLs are indistinguishable from internal navigation.
- **FR-4:** Deep-link resolution outcomes are exists-active (render), exists-inactive (make active, then render), archived (open the archived-vehicle detail with restore affordance), and unknown-or-deleted (open a safe garage explanation, not another vehicle silently substituted). Resolution runs after hydration completes; before hydration completes the URL is retained and the pre-hydration skeleton renders (FR-15).
- **FR-5:** The active vehicle URL survives ordinary refresh **and** LiveView reconnect (network blip, sleep/wake, Fly.io deploy). Reconnect re-runs the hydration path (INV-24.1, ADR-0004 §"Mount and hydration sequence"); the client DOM is retained under `phx-disconnected` styling and re-hydration replaces assigns underneath a stable view (ADR-0004 §"First-paint and empty-state rules"; INV-7).
- **FR-6:** If active vehicles exceed a design-tested threshold (propose 5), the persistent switcher retains the most recent/favorite entries and the full set is reachable via a dedicated `/garage` index route with search. Essential switching MUST NOT depend on a horizontal-scroll gesture as the only affordance.
- **FR-7:** Every create/edit/service/reading screen displays the active vehicle identity near the title and repeats it in destructive confirmations. Identity is rendered locally from hydrated assigns and never requires an additional round trip.
- **FR-8:** Switching with a dirty form prompts Save/Discard/Stay and MUST NOT commit values to the new vehicle. Save commits through the mutation round trip (INV-24.2, ADR-0004 §"Mutation round trip") to the vehicle ID captured at form open, waits for the hook's ack, and only then navigates.
- **FR-9:** Add vehicle reuses the M05 catalog selection and provenance rules by calling the rescoped catalog-query layer (`DOS-M09-005`, INV-26): impersonal selectors only; no VIN or personal parameter appears in a query. Save follows the mutation round trip and the new vehicle is not shown as saved until the hook acknowledges the IndexedDB write.
- **FR-10:** At the persisted active limit, the UI explains archive/delete options and blocks creation with guidance; the oldest profile is never overwritten. The limit itself is authoritative in `DOS-M08-001`; the UI reads it, does not decide it.
- **FR-11:** Reorder is available through buttons/menu; drag is optional. Every reorder action is announced to screen readers and committed atomically through the mutation round trip; a partial commit is impossible because the hook applies the change inside a single IndexedDB readwrite transaction across the affected stores plus `meta` (ADR-0004 §"Mutation round trip").
- **FR-12:** Multi-tab consistency (INV-24.7). The garage subscribes to `BroadcastChannel("dos_local_store")` per ADR-0004 §"Multi-tab consistency". A switch/reorder/archive/delete in tab A causes tab B to re-hydrate from IndexedDB and re-render; a stale-tab write that fails the compare-and-set on `meta.seq` triggers a visible "reloaded from this browser's newer data" notice. §9's two-tab mutation test row is the acceptance measurement.
- **FR-13:** Vehicle visual tokens (color/icon) are supplemental only and MUST NOT be the sole identity. Duplicate nicknames are disambiguated by appending year/make/model everywhere identity is rendered — including the accessible name in FR-2 and the URL page title.
- **FR-14:** Archived vehicles live in a separate, labeled `/garage/archived` route and cannot receive due/overdue state. Delete flows surface the export offer and typed/second-step confirmation defined by `DOS-M08-001`. Deleting or archiving the active vehicle lands the user on the deterministic successor URL (or the empty-garage state at `/garage`) via `push_navigate`.
- **FR-15 (new):** **Pre-hydration first paint.** The garage MUST NOT flash "no vehicles yet", welcome-first-vehicle onboarding, or any other empty-garage claim while `assigns.local_state == :hydrating` (INV-24.3, ADR-0004 §"First-paint and empty-state rules"). The static render and the pre-hydration live render show the skeleton defined by `DOS-M09-003`; interactive controls do not accept input until hydration resolves. Copy on any garage surface MUST NOT use the prohibited terminology from INV-21 ("saved to your account", "synced", "backed up", "restore").
- **FR-16 (new):** **Cleared-storage, storage-unavailable, and session-only states.** The garage renders the distinct states from `DOS-M09-003`: a `dos_boot_state == "has_data"` hint with a zero-record hydration is `:data_missing` (INV-24.4/24.8, INV-25) and MUST NOT show a fresh empty garage; an unopenable or probe-failing IndexedDB is session-only mode with the ADR-0004 persistent banner and prominent export offers before any data entry; a `QuotaExceededError` on write follows the failed-ack path from ADR-0004 §"Mutation round trip", the prior state is preserved, and export is offered (INV-24.5). Silent write failure is a release blocker per §9's honest-degradation-coverage row.
- **FR-17 (new — expressly retires pre-pivot FR-5 as written and addresses staleness finding 2):** **Disconnected behavior is a shell, not an offline garage.** When the LiveView socket is down the garage renders the `phx-disconnected` shell (ADR-0004 §"WebSocket and LiveView considerations"); IndexedDB records remain readable to whatever extent the client can render without a catalog round trip (INV-7), but the garage does not claim to work offline (INV-6 is amended and the bundled-catalog-at-first-launch promise is withdrawn). The old airplane-mode requirement from the pre-pivot spec is **retired** and its acceptance evidence is replaced by an ADR-0004-honest test that the disconnected shell renders, hydrated records remain readable per FR-15/FR-16, and the no-implied-backup / storage-loss copy is unambiguous.
- **FR-18:** The garage LiveView holds no personal data outside `socket.assigns` and holds nothing there beyond the current mount's hydration (INV-3, INV-23, INV-24.1, ADR-0004 §5 of Decision). It writes only through the mutation round trip; it never writes a personal row to any server-side store.
- **FR-19:** The garage does not render all vehicle histories or forecasts into assigns simultaneously. Only the active panel summary plus lightweight per-vehicle switcher metadata (identity, status, token) is held; per-vehicle history/forecast for non-active vehicles is not fetched or held. This bounds the assigns size against §9's storage and payload budgets.
- **FR-20:** Transport respects INV-22 and INV-27: the garage is served from `digitaloilsticker.com` over HTTPS with WSS for the LiveView socket; `check_origin` is an explicit allowlist; CSRF is enforced on the LiveView session; no third-party origin is contacted from any garage screen. Static Netlify subdomains are not linked from any interactive garage surface in a way that implies they hold personal data (INV-27).

## Technical implementation contract

### Components and ownership boundaries

- New/extended LiveViews and components: `GarageLive` (root at `/garage` and `/garage/:vehicle_id`), `ArchivedGarageLive` at `/garage/archived`, `AllVehiclesLive` (overflow index), `AddVehicleLive`, `VehicleSwitcherComponent` (persistent accessible switcher), `ActiveContextBannerComponent` (shared across create/edit/service/reading screens), `DirtyFormGuardComponent`.
- All state mutations delegate to the `DigitalOilSticker.Garage` context from `DOS-M08-001`; the LiveView holds no garage business rules. Contexts remain client-agnostic per ADR-0004 §6 and its static-check enforcement (`DOS-M09-008` scope).
- The URL is the sole source of truth for the active vehicle in a given tab; the LiveView does not hold a separate `assigns.active_vehicle_id` that can drift from the URL.
- Client integration flows through the `LocalStore` `phx-hook` defined by `DOS-M09-002`/`DOS-M09-003`; this card does not define hook events, it consumes the hydration and mutation-round-trip events those cards specify.

### Data and persistence

- Reads persisted `position`, active selection (as a hydrated hint; the URL is authoritative in-tab), archive state, and presentation tokens from the IndexedDB payload delivered by `local_store:hydrate`. Writes only through context commands (reorder commit, active-URL push, archive/restore, delete), each mediated by a `local_store:put` and gated on the corresponding `local_store:ack`.
- Switcher metadata is lightweight (identity, status, token) and vehicle-scoped; full history/forecast is not held for non-active vehicles.
- Deep-link route parameters carry stable client-generated UUIDs, never positional indexes or catalog identifiers with personal meaning. URLs carry no VIN, no odometer, no note text, no free-text field (INV-26).

### Interfaces and events

- Hook events consumed: `local_store:hydrate` (initial and reconnect), `local_store:put` (write), `local_store:ack` (write confirmed), `local_store:conflict` (compare-and-set failure), `local_store:multi_tab_update` (BroadcastChannel). All are defined by `DOS-M09-003`; this card wires them into garage state transitions.
- Deep-link contract: URL explicitly names its vehicle UUID; resolution outcomes are the four cases in FR-4. This is the deep-link contract other browser routes reuse when they need to jump to a vehicle (there is no notification-tap surface at MVP, INV-17).
- Dirty-form guard: a `handle_event("navigate", ...)` interceptor triggers the Save/Discard/Stay prompt; only an explicit Save commits, always to the vehicle ID captured at form open, always waiting for `local_store:ack` before navigating.

### Failure and recovery behavior

- A failed reorder/switch/archive command surfaces a recoverable error and leaves the previous rendered state; the compare-and-set on `meta.seq` means partial commits are impossible.
- Deep links to unknown/archived vehicles never crash and never silently substitute another vehicle; they open a safe garage explanation with clear next actions.
- Reconnect after a Fly.io deploy or a network blip re-runs hydration; assigns are rebuilt from the client payload and the URL re-resolves against the fresh hydration.
- A `QuotaExceededError` or a missing ack within the ADR-0004 2000 ms window renders the persistent "Not saved to this browser" affordance on the affected record and offers export (INV-24.5).

### Security and privacy

- HTTPS + WSS enforced; HSTS after certificate verification (INV-22). `check_origin` is an explicit allowlist for `digitaloilsticker.com` and `www.digitaloilsticker.com`; CSRF on the LiveView session and every mutating HTTP endpoint.
- CSP as specified in ADR-0004 §"Content Security Policy"; no external font, script, or connect origin is used by any garage surface.
- URLs, query strings, socket payloads, and log lines carry no personal data (INV-4, INV-26). Garage-related `handle_event` params are excluded from Logger by the allowlist established in `DOS-M09-008`.
- The session cookie contains no personal data and no stable user identifier (ADR-0004 §"CSRF and session").
- The garage never introduces a third-party origin (INV-4, INV-22).

### Accessibility and performance

- WCAG 2.2 AA where applicable (§9), verified against the `DOS-M09-009` browser and assistive-technology matrix.
- Reorder is announced to screen readers via `aria-live` regions defined by `DOS-M02-002`.
- Initial garage payload and DOM stay within the §9 first-visit and repeat-visit budgets at the maximum supported vehicle count.
- Hydration completion p95 ≤ 300 ms after socket join (§9); the garage skeleton never blocks past the hydration deadline established in ADR-0004 without transitioning to an explicit state.
- LiveView event round trip p95 ≤ 150 ms measured from the client (§9); switcher and switch events are held to this budget.

## UX and content states

- Pre-hydration: the ADR-0004 skeleton; interactive controls disabled; no empty-garage claim (INV-24.3).
- Empty garage (genuine first visit): onboarding explains adding the first vehicle and states records live in this browser only (INV-5, INV-25).
- Cleared or evicted (`:data_missing`): distinguishable from a first visit; states in plain language that browser storage appears to have been cleared, offers import, uses required terminology (INV-21, INV-25).
- Storage unavailable: session-only mode banner (from `DOS-M09-003`) is shown before data entry; the garage explains that nothing will be saved when the tab closes.
- Loaded, small garage: full persistent switcher.
- Loaded, overflow garage: recent/favorite entries in the switcher plus a link to `/garage` (All vehicles) with search.
- At-limit: explains archive/delete options; creation blocked with guidance; never overwrites the oldest profile.
- Active-context banner: near the title of every mutating screen and inside destructive confirmations.
- Dirty-form switch: Save/Discard/Stay prompt; Save waits for ack.
- Archived: separate labeled route with restore affordance.
- Deep-link failure: safe garage explanation for unknown/archived targets.
- Duplicate nicknames: disambiguated with year/make/model everywhere identity is rendered.
- Disconnected shell: `phx-disconnected` styling; garage does not claim to work offline; reconnect re-runs hydration.
- Multi-tab reload: an unobtrusive "reloaded from this browser's newer data" notice when a stale tab's write loses the compare-and-set.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC-2: Deep-link URLs shared into a new tab or bookmarked open the correct vehicle for exists-active/exists-inactive, land on the safe garage explanation for unknown/deleted IDs, and open the archived-vehicle detail for archived IDs (FR-4).
- [ ] AC-3: Every mutating form and confirmation renders the target vehicle unambiguously; a dirty-form switch never cross-saves (FR-7, FR-8).
- [ ] AC-4: The switcher and all garage surfaces pass the WCAG 2.2 AA measurements from §9 on the `DOS-M09-009` browser/AT matrix: keyboard operability, focus indicator, 200% zoom, 320 CSS px reflow, target size, live-region announcements (FR-2).
- [ ] AC-5: Initial garage payload and DOM at the maximum supported vehicle count stay within §9's first-visit and repeat-visit payload budgets and the client storage footprint ceiling; hydration completion p95 ≤ 300 ms after socket join (FR-19).
- [ ] AC-6: Essential switching works without a swipe gesture, and reorder works through buttons/menu with screen-reader announcements (FR-11).
- [ ] AC-7: Pre-hydration first paint never shows an empty-garage claim, welcome-first-vehicle onboarding, or any storage-loss copy prohibited by INV-21; the cleared/evicted state is distinguishable from a genuine first visit; storage-unavailable mode warns before data entry (FR-15, FR-16).
- [ ] AC-8: Two open tabs mutate the same garage without silent stale overwrite: the losing writer sees the "reloaded from this browser's newer data" notice and the winning writer's change is reflected in both tabs (FR-12, §9 multi-tab row).
- [ ] AC-9: With the network dropped, the garage renders the `phx-disconnected` shell, IndexedDB records remain readable per the hydration protocol, the no-implied-backup / storage-loss messaging is unambiguous, and no copy claims the garage works offline. On reconnect the hydration path re-runs (FR-17, INV-7; replaces the retired pre-pivot airplane-mode acceptance).
- [ ] AC-10: An idle 24 h capture on any garage surface shows zero outbound requests beyond the application origin; no URL, query string, socket payload, or log line contains a VIN, odometer value, vehicle name, or note text (§9 privacy-on-the-wire row; INV-4, INV-26).

## Test plan

### Automated

- LiveView tests covering URL resolution for exists-active/exists-inactive/archived/unknown; refresh restores the same vehicle; back/forward move between vehicles correctly; deep-linked URL from a new socket resolves after hydration.
- Multi-vehicle E2E scripts across the `DOS-M09-009` browser/engine matrix: create to the tested maximum, switch/reorder/archive/restore/delete, refresh, reopen tab, assert active/ordered garage via URL.
- Hydration-race tests: interactive controls do not accept input before hydration resolves; a URL for a vehicle not in the hydrated payload resolves to the unknown-vehicle safe state, not to a substituted vehicle.
- Dirty-form race tests: rapid switch during edit; assert no cross-vehicle commit under any interleaving of Save, Discard, Stay, and navigation.
- Reconnect tests: kill the socket mid-session; assert `phx-disconnected` styling, hydration re-runs on reconnect, active URL and hydrated garage are preserved.
- Multi-tab tests using two `Wallaby`/Playwright sessions against one origin: assert BroadcastChannel-driven re-hydration and the compare-and-set stale-write notice (FR-12).
- Storage-state tests exercising the states from `DOS-M09-003`: pre-hydration render never contains prohibited copy from INV-21; `:data_missing` is distinguishable from empty; `QuotaExceededError` follows the failed-ack path.
- Assigns/DOM/payload size assertions at maximum vehicle count against §9 budgets.
- Copy-lint gate that fails the build if any garage-surface string contains INV-21-prohibited terminology ("saved to your account", "synced", "backed up", "restore" in the durability sense).
- Log-scanning test asserting no personal field appears in captured server logs across the scripted session.

### Manual/browser matrix

- The `DOS-M09-009` browser matrix (§9 provisional: current and prior stable Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari on desktop; iOS Safari 16+ and Android Chrome on mobile), with keyboard-only desktop and touch mobile passes, VoiceOver/TalkBack/NVDA/JAWS on their supported browsers, and a 200% zoom / 320 CSS px reflow pass.
- Deep-link handling: shared URL opened in a new browser profile and in private browsing; assert exists-active, unknown-ID, and archived resolutions; assert the storage-unavailable state in private windows that block IndexedDB.
- Multi-tab manual pass on two tabs of the same origin, exercising the compare-and-set stale-write notice.
- **Disconnected-shell test (replaces the retired pre-pivot airplane-mode test per staleness finding 2):** disconnect the network mid-session and assert the garage renders the `phx-disconnected` shell, hydrated records remain readable per the ADR-0004 hydration protocol, and the no-implied-backup / storage-loss messaging is unambiguous. Airplane mode is not a green path.
- Payload/hydration trace captured on the slowest supported device class in the matrix at maximum vehicle count.

## Rollout and rollback

- Ships as a normal Fly.io deploy on the browser MVP release train (`DOS-M09-006`). The URL contract is additive: pre-existing bookmarks that used earlier route shapes redirect to the canonical `/garage/<vehicle_id>` form.
- Rollback is redeploying the previous Fly image (`fly releases`). Because the server persists no personal rows, no data migration is involved in either direction (ADR-0004 §"Consequences").
- If the client IndexedDB schema needs to bump for garage state, the version bump follows the `DOS-M09-001` forward-only migration contract and is not a rollback point.
- If a stale deployment lands on a browser holding a newer client payload, the server enters read-only mode and offers export per ADR-0004 §"Schema versioning and migration"; no downgrade is attempted.

## Risks, decisions, and open questions

- **Risk (R-1 inherited from ADR-0004):** Browser eviction of infrequently-used site storage — a person who checks their oil quarterly is exactly the profile most at risk. Mitigated in this card by rendering `DOS-M09-003`'s `:data_missing` state honestly on the garage rather than as an empty first visit; not solvable in this card, escalated to ADR-0004 R1 and the PWA spike `DOS-M09-010`.
- **Risk:** Reconnect after a Fly.io deploy re-runs hydration; if hydration exceeds §9's p95, the garage flashes a skeleton on every reconnect. Mitigated by holding the ADR-0004 deadline and by the multi-tab reload notice for stale-write cases.
- **Risk:** URL grammar drift — a later card changes the shape of `/garage/<vehicle_id>`. Mitigated by pinning the grammar in this card's deep-link contract documentation and by the redirect discipline in Rollout.
- **Decision (design-tested):** the visible switcher threshold before falling back to `/garage` (5 proposed).
- **Decision:** whether the archived route is `/garage/archived` or `/garage/archive` — recorded here and pinned in the deep-link contract doc.
- **Open question:** whether a bookmark to a URL whose vehicle was archived on another device (via export/import) should open exists-inactive after import, or continue to open the archived detail. Deferred to the design-review record in Documentation.
- **Open question:** whether the switcher's overflow "All vehicles" link should live in the shell or only appear when the threshold is exceeded; resolved in design review within this card's scope.

## Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)

### Outcome (pre-pivot)

Users can add, identify, reorder, switch, archive, restore, replace, and delete vehicles; the active vehicle is always obvious and each vehicle's dashboard/history is reachable with minimal friction.

### Why this matters and what it unblocks (pre-pivot)

Multi-vehicle state is dangerous if users cannot tell which vehicle a mileage or service entry affects. The navigation contract must precede feature reuse: every screen reused from the single-vehicle MVP will render inside this garage shell, so its identity/switching guarantees must exist before `DOS-M08-003` and `DOS-M08-004` can complete their user-facing surfaces.

### Context and source of truth (pre-pivot)

- Master prompt §5 "Explicit post-MVP": "Multiple vehicles enabled in the UI, vehicle tabs/list switching, and per-vehicle reminders."
- Master prompt §7 "Target implementation architecture": expected LiveViews/components (`AppShell`, `StickerLive`, `VehiclePickerLive`, `VehicleSummary`, `DueStatus`, `CascadeSelect`, consistent empty/error/recovery components); one Mob root WebView and Phoenix route stack for product navigation.
- Master prompt §4.1: Mob LiveView runs Phoenix in a native WebView on device loopback — the tablist is an ARIA/web construct inside the WebView, not native tab bars.
- Catalog entry "DOS-M08-002 — Build the accessible tabbed garage and vehicle-management UX" in the master prompt.
- `DOS-M08-001` for the persisted garage contract this UI renders (ordering, active selection, archive/delete semantics, maximum count, disambiguation data).
- M07 accessibility/performance gates for the budgets this UI must stay within.

### Included (pre-pivot)

- Garage landing screen.
- Horizontal/scrollable vehicle tabs with visible overflow affordance.
- Add-vehicle flow reusing the exact offline catalog selection and provenance rules.
- Active context banner on every create/edit/service/reading screen.
- Manage/reorder/archive/delete flows (reorder via buttons/menu as well as drag).
- Empty-garage and at-limit states.
- Duplicate nickname disambiguation (append year/make/model).

- Persistence of the active vehicle and ordering across ordinary relaunch.

### Excluded (pre-pivot)

- Cloud garage sharing.
- Swipe-only navigation.
- Vehicle comparison.
- Bulk service editing.
- Desktop browser tabs.

### Preconditions (pre-pivot)

- `DOS-M08-001` is closed: persisted ordering, active selection, archive/delete state, maximum-count enforcement, and vehicle-scoped context APIs exist.
- `DOS-M05-002` and `DOS-M05-005` are closed: the M05 LiveView surfaces and flows this garage shell wraps and reuses exist in final v1 form.
- The M07 accessibility and performance gates are closed, so their budgets and test procedures are available to measure this UI against.

### Functional requirements (pre-pivot)

- FR-1: Use an ARIA-compliant tablist in the LiveView WebView for a small active garage: each tab has selected state, an accessible label ("Nickname, year make model"), roving focus/arrow-key behavior where appropriate, and a corresponding tabpanel.
- FR-2: On compact phones, tabs scroll horizontally with a visible overflow affordance and an "All vehicles" control; essential switching never depends on an undiscoverable swipe.
- FR-3: If active vehicles exceed a design-tested threshold (propose 5), retain the most recent/favorite tabs plus an All Vehicles sheet/list with search.
- FR-4: Do not render all histories or forecasts into the DOM/LiveView assigns; load only the active panel summary and lightweight tab metadata.
- FR-5: Keep the active vehicle stable across ordinary relaunch.
- FR-6: Notification/deep-link routes explicitly select their vehicle if it still exists, then open current dashboard state. A missing/archived target opens a safe garage explanation, not another vehicle silently.
- FR-7: Every create/edit/service/reading screen displays the active vehicle identity near the title and repeats it in destructive confirmations.
- FR-8: Switching with a dirty form prompts Save/Discard/Stay and must not commit values to the new vehicle.
- FR-9: Add vehicle reuses the exact offline catalog selection and provenance rules from the M05 flow (`VehiclePickerLive`/`CascadeSelect` contract).
- FR-10: At the active limit, explain archive/delete options; never overwrite the oldest profile.
- FR-11: Reorder is available through buttons/menu as well as drag, announced to screen readers, and committed atomically.
- FR-12: Vehicle visual tokens (color/icon) are supplemental only; color/icon cannot be the sole identity. Duplicate nicknames append year/make/model.
- FR-13: Archived vehicles live in a separate, labeled management list and cannot receive routine reminders.
- FR-14: Delete flows surface the export offer and typed/second-step confirmation defined by `DOS-M08-001`, and deleting or archiving the active vehicle lands the user on the deterministic successor or empty-garage state.

### Technical implementation contract (pre-pivot)

#### Components and ownership boundaries

- New/extended LiveViews and components: garage landing, vehicle tablist/tabpanel components inside `AppShell`, All Vehicles sheet/list with search, manage/reorder screen, archived-vehicles list, active-context banner component reused across create/edit/service/reading screens.
- All state mutations delegate to the `DigitalOilSticker.Garage` context APIs from `DOS-M08-001`; the UI holds no garage business rules and no global process variable for the active vehicle.
- Navigation stays on the single Mob root WebView/Phoenix route stack; no native tab bar duplication (master prompt §4.1).

#### Data and persistence

- Reads persisted `position`, active selection, archive state, and presentation tokens from `DOS-M08-001`; writes only through context commands (reorder commit, active switch, archive/restore, delete).
- Tab metadata queries are lightweight (identity, status, token) and vehicle-scoped; full history/forecast queries occur only for the active panel.
- Deep-link route parameters carry stable vehicle UUIDs, never positional indexes.

#### Interfaces and events

- LiveViews subscribe to vehicle-scoped topics/events from `DOS-M08-001` (garage lifecycle, `vehicle_saved`, `forecast_changed`) to refresh tab metadata and the active panel.
- Deep-link contract: route explicitly names its vehicle ID; resolution outcomes are exists-active, exists-inactive (switch then render), archived, and deleted (both to the safe garage explanation). This is the same safe path `DOS-M08-004` notification taps use.
- Dirty-form guard: a navigation-away event triggers the Save/Discard/Stay prompt; only an explicit Save commits, and always to the originating vehicle ID captured at form open.

#### Failure and recovery behavior

- A failed reorder/switch/archive command leaves the previous rendered state and shows a recoverable error; reorder commits atomically or not at all.
- Deep links to missing/archived vehicles never crash or silently substitute a vehicle; they open the safe garage explanation.
- Relaunch after process death restores the persisted active vehicle and ordering; a stale rendered vehicle that was deleted on another screen resolves to the deterministic successor.

#### Security and privacy

- No new data collection; nicknames and vehicle identity render locally. Deep-link URLs remain loopback application routes (master prompt §7 production shell security) and carry stable IDs only, no VIN or personal data.
- Destructive confirmations display identity locally; the export offer never uploads.

#### Accessibility and performance

- Tabs/list must pass VoiceOver/TalkBack, keyboard/focus, large text, noncolor, compact width, and target-size tests.
- Reorder actions are announced to screen readers; drag always has a button/menu equivalent.
- Initial garage/dashboard payload and DOM stay within the M07 performance budget at the maximum supported vehicle count; assigns hold only active-panel summary plus lightweight tab metadata.

### UX and content states (pre-pivot)

- Empty garage: explains adding the first vehicle; no phantom vehicle.
- Small garage (at or below the proposed threshold of 5): full tablist.
- Overflow garage: most recent/favorite tabs plus "All vehicles" sheet/list with search.
- At-limit: explains archive/delete options; creation is blocked with guidance, never by overwriting the oldest profile.
- Active-context banner: vehicle identity near the title of every mutating screen and inside destructive confirmations.
- Dirty-form switch: Save/Discard/Stay prompt.
- Archived management list: separate and labeled; restore available.
- Deep-link failure: safe garage explanation for missing/archived targets.
- Duplicate nicknames: disambiguated with year/make/model everywhere identity is rendered.
