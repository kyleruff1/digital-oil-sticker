**RESCOPED FOR BROWSER MVP — 2026-08-01.** [ADR-0004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-0004-browser-first-client-and-hosting.md) established the browser-first MVP. This card is retained with the same identity; its Outcome and FRs below are the rescoped spec. The pre-pivot spec is preserved at the bottom under "Superseded pre-pivot spec (historical record)". Disposition per [PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md](../../docs/governance/PIVOT-2026-08-01-ROADMAP-RECLASSIFICATION.md#issue-by-issue-classification): _Per-vehicle isolation must be enforced in the client store and in the hydration payload shape, since there is no server-side row scoping to rely on._

## Outcome

Every vehicle in the browser garage independently owns its maintenance basis, service history, odometer readings, usage samples, forecast inputs, derived due status, and next action. Isolation is enforced in three places at once because there is no SQL boundary left to fall back on: (a) in the shape of the IndexedDB stores defined by [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md), where every child record carries a `vehicle_id` foreign key that the read-time integrity pass rejects if it references a vehicle absent from the payload; (b) in the shape of the hydration envelope defined by [`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md), where the server-side changeset validates cross-vehicle referential integrity before any record enters `socket.assigns`; and (c) in the per-socket assigns lifetime described by ADR-0004 §"The client-storage + LiveView hydration protocol" and INV-24.1, where personal data lives only for the connection and one vehicle's assigns are never derivable from another's. Cross-tab correctness is governed by the `meta.seq` compare-and-set protocol from ADR-0004 §"Multi-tab consistency" and [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) FR-16, not by a server-side lock. The pure forecast functions from [`DOS-M06-003`](DOS-M06-003.md) are unchanged and are invoked over the hydrated per-vehicle aggregate held in assigns, never over a queried table.

## Why this matters and what it unblocks

Reusing singleton assumptions is the highest multi-vehicle correctness risk and could cause a user to service the wrong car on the wrong schedule. Under the browser-first architecture that risk is materially larger, not smaller: with `UserRepo` gone (ADR-0004 §Decision 4), the server has no unique index, foreign key, or `CHECK` constraint that could catch a cross-vehicle leak, and `pushEvent` from a hook is untrusted client input by definition ([`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md), ADR-0004 §"Validation"). The isolation invariants therefore have to be authored, not inherited, and they have to be authored on both sides of the socket.

Closing this issue is the correctness core of the post-MVP multi-vehicle release. It unblocks the multi-vehicle release itself and the re-scoped garage surfaces that render vehicle-scoped state ([`DOS-M08-002`](DOS-M08-002.md)). Per-vehicle OS notifications are not a downstream consumer under this pivot: [`DOS-M08-004`](DOS-M08-004.md) is deferred to the native re-entry because OS pending-notification limits do not exist in a browser (INV-17, ADR-0004 §"Consequences" — Lost — honestly), so this card no longer blocks it and no notification-scheduling FRs are inherited from it.

## Included

- Per-vehicle aggregate in `socket.assigns`: interval selection/source, service baseline, odometer segment/readings, declared usage, and derived forecast/due status, keyed by `vehicle_id` and never derivable from another vehicle's assigns.
- Referential validation in the hydration changeset that rejects any event, reading, usage sample, or reminder whose `vehicle_id` does not name a vehicle also present in the same envelope, and quarantines the offending records per [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) FR-12 instead of silently dropping them.
- Per-vehicle write-back through the [`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md) mutation round trip, with each `local_store:put` naming the `vehicle_id`(s) the upserts and deletes are scoped to and no cross-vehicle upsert permitted in one envelope.
- Multi-tab correctness via the `meta.seq` compare-and-set from ADR-0004 §"Multi-tab consistency" and [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) FR-16, plus the `BroadcastChannel("dos_local_store")` per-tab re-hydration path.
- Per-socket LiveView subscription rules that never carry another vehicle's aggregate over the wire.
- The forecast recompute for a mutated vehicle, invoked over the hydrated assigns for that vehicle only, with a `revision` value that lets a late reply from a superseded revision be dropped.
- Garage overview derivation: one per-vehicle summary row computed from hydrated assigns (or from a cached derived record in `assigns`), never from a query over a global table.
- Single-vehicle and all-garage export selection at the UI layer, produced by the client-side export from ADR-0004 §"Export and import"; import duplicate/replace handling that never overwrites another vehicle silently.
- Stale-state handling ("Updating"/revision-safe loading states during recompute, tab switches, and reconnect re-hydration) that never displays vehicle B's data under vehicle A's identity.

- Fleet analytics.
- Shared mileage patterns.

- Any server-side per-vehicle table, cache, ETS entry, or process registry — all disallowed by INV-23, INV-3, and ADR-0004 §Decision 4.

- The hydration envelope wire protocol, the changeset per `schema_version`, and the acknowledgment/timeout state machine — owned by [`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md), not restated here.
- OS-scheduled per-vehicle notifications and fairness within OS pending-notification limits — deferred to the native re-entry via [`DOS-M08-004`](DOS-M08-004.md) (INV-17).

## Functional requirements

Each of the sweep-report findings numbered in the rescope brief is addressed by a specific FR change. Findings 1 and 2 (source-of-truth cited `UserRepo`; FRs 1–6 assumed server-side row scoping) are absorbed by the rewritten Context and by the FRs below. Finding 3 (pure forecast functions must be invoked over hydrated assigns rather than aggregated from `UserRepo`) is FR-6. Finding 4 (the foreground-recompute manual check assumed a backgrounded native app) is retired from the Test plan and its browser-native replacements are captured under FR-8 and Test plan §Manual.

- FR-1: The vehicle-scoped aggregate lives in `socket.assigns` for the LiveView connection lifetime only, keyed by `vehicle_id`, and is populated from the hydration envelope's `data.vehicles`, `data.events`, `data.readings`, `data.reminders`, and `data.prefs` after [`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md)'s server-side changeset accepts them. It is never written to disk, database, cache, ETS table, external store, or log, and is discarded on socket termination (INV-3, INV-23, INV-24.1). _Replaces the pre-pivot FR-1's `UserRepo`-aggregated read-path._
- FR-2: The [`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md) hydration changeset enforces per-vehicle referential integrity server-side: every event, reading, usage sample, and reminder whose `vehicle_id` does not name a vehicle present in the same envelope is quarantined via the [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) FR-11/FR-12 mechanism, not silently dropped and not silently attached to another vehicle. The quarantine count is surfaced through the [`DOS-M09-003`](DOS-M09-003.md) storage-status surface. _Replaces the pre-pivot FR-2's reliance on SQL foreign keys._
- FR-3: Every mutation is scoped to a single logical vehicle. The `pushEvent` payload names the target `vehicle_id`, the server updates that vehicle's aggregate in assigns only, and the `local_store:put` envelope pushed to the hook contains only that vehicle's upserts/deletes plus any `meta` update. A mutation event that names a `vehicle_id` absent from assigns is rejected with a typed error and does not modify any assigns. _Replaces the pre-pivot FR-3's supervised keyed worker / serialized transaction; there is no server-side worker to key, because there is no persisted state to serialize._
- FR-4: Cross-tab isolation is governed by `meta.seq`: every `local_store:put` transaction from the hook performs a compare-and-set on `meta.seq` inside the same IndexedDB `readwrite` transaction (ADR-0004 §"Mutation round trip" and §"Multi-tab consistency"; [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) FR-16). On mismatch the transaction aborts, the hook emits `local_store:conflict`, and the server discards its optimistic assign, triggers re-hydration, and renders the visible "reloaded from this browser's newer data" notice from [`DOS-M09-003`](DOS-M09-003.md). The losing tab's re-hydration re-computes the affected vehicle's derived state from the reconciled envelope; another vehicle's assigns are untouched. _Replaces the pre-pivot FR-3's/FR-4's server-side serialization guarantees._
- FR-5: LiveView subscriptions are scoped to the socket and the currently rendered `vehicle_id`. No Phoenix.PubSub topic broadcasts vehicle-scoped assigns to any other socket, no global broadcast is used for garage summaries, and no topic name includes a value that could correlate a person across visits (INV-3, INV-4, INV-24.1). _Replaces the pre-pivot FR-4's vehicle-scoped topics without the cross-user-broadcast concern that only mattered when server storage existed._
- FR-6: The pure forecast functions from [`DOS-M06-003`](DOS-M06-003.md) are invoked over the hydrated per-vehicle aggregate held in assigns, not aggregated from a `UserRepo` query. Signatures and math are unchanged. A recompute call receives one vehicle's aggregate; it never reads another vehicle's assigns and it never queries a personal-data store because none exists on the server (INV-3, INV-23; sweep finding 3). _Replaces the pre-pivot FR-1's/FR-5's aggregate-from-tables sourcing._
- FR-7: The garage overview summary for each active vehicle is derived from its hydrated assigns (nickname/identity, observed odometer as-of, estimated due date/cause, confidence, status). It is cached in assigns keyed by `vehicle_id` and `revision`, not persisted; a summary at revision R for vehicle A is never rendered under vehicle B and never carries data from a superseded revision.
- FR-8: Foreground-recompute triggers are the browser-native equivalents from [`DOS-M06-003`](DOS-M06-003.md) as invoked in a LiveView: on hydration completion, on `pageshow` (bfcache restore) via a `phx-hook` that pushes a `foreground_recompute` event, on visibility change from `hidden` to `visible`, and on LiveView reconnect after `phx-disconnected`. Each trigger recomputes only the vehicles present in the current assigns. The pre-pivot "background the app, mutate time/timezone, foreground" ritual has no browser analogue and is retired (sweep finding 4); the time-zone-change trigger survives because `prefs.time_zone` mutation goes through the standard mutation round trip.
- FR-9: Adding an oil change while vehicle A is active recomputes and re-renders A only. B's assigns, B's cached summary at its own revision, and B's rendered DOM are unchanged.
- FR-10: Switching per-vehicle URLs during recomputation shows A's prior result labeled "Updating" (or a revision-safe loading state) and never displays B's result under A. Late replies from a superseded revision are dropped, never rendered.
- FR-11: Export offered at the UI layer supports single-vehicle and all-garage selection. Single-vehicle export includes the referenced catalog snapshot fields ([`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) FR-4) sufficient to import as a new entry against a compatible or newer catalog version. The export is produced entirely client-side (ADR-0004 §"Export and import").
- FR-12: Import duplicate-`vehicle_id` handling routes to the [`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md) replace-versus-merge selector; a duplicate never overwrites another vehicle silently, and merge with cross-vehicle references is only accepted when the imported children resolve to vehicles present after the merge.
- FR-13: A hydration payload whose logical `schema_version` is newer than the deployed server's places the session in read-only mode (ADR-0004 §"Schema versioning and migration"); this issue does not add or override that policy, but the isolation surfaces MUST render each vehicle's read-only aggregate honestly and MUST NOT flash an empty state for a vehicle whose records were held aside.
- FR-14: Reconnect re-hydration is idempotent per vehicle and preserves per-vehicle identity across the reconnect: after a `phx-disconnected` → reconnect cycle, the active vehicle's aggregate at revision R is replaced by the freshly hydrated aggregate at revision R' with no intermediate frame in which A's identity was rendered next to B's data (ADR-0004 §"WebSocket and LiveView considerations").
- FR-15: All log lines produced by this issue's code paths conform to ADR-0004 §"Logging" and INV-4: no VIN, no odometer value, no service date, no note text, no custom vehicle name, no `vehicle_id`, and no hydration/mutation payload fragment appears in logs, error reports, or crash traces. The scripted-session test from ADR-0004 §"Enforcement" MUST include a per-vehicle isolation scenario and MUST fail the release on any occurrence.
- FR-16: The unit preference is app-global under this pivot. `prefs.units` in [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) is singleton; there is no per-vehicle unit override in the browser MVP. The pre-pivot FR-7 unit-preference-scope decision is retired: a per-vehicle unit toggle would require a schema-version bump in [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) and is out of scope here.

## Technical implementation contract

### Components and ownership boundaries

- `DigitalOilSticker.Garage` and `DigitalOilSticker.Maintenance` own the per-vehicle aggregate assembly from hydrated assigns; they do not open `Repo` calls for personal data because no `Repo` for personal data exists (ADR-0004 §Decision 4).
- `DigitalOilSticker.Forecasting` stays a set of deterministic pure functions with unchanged signatures; each invocation receives one vehicle's aggregate from assigns.
- The LiveView (per-vehicle route from [`DOS-M08-002`](DOS-M08-002.md)) mounts the aggregate for its `vehicle_id` from `socket.assigns` and never accesses another vehicle's assigns; the `AppShell` LiveView renders the garage overview summaries derived from the same assigns.
- The `LocalStore` hook ([`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md)) owns the hydration/mutation wire; this issue owns the assigns shape it fills and the isolation invariants that shape enforces.
- No perpetual background GenServer for per-vehicle recompute — recompute runs synchronously on the LiveView process after a mutation or a foreground trigger (INV-24.1; ADR-0004 §Decision 6).

### Data and persistence

- No personal-data database on the server. `CatalogRepo` remains read-only (ADR-0004 §Decision 2, §"Enforcement"); the release-gating test that asserts the Ecto repo list is `[CatalogRepo]` covers this issue too.
- Personal data lives in the IndexedDB stores from [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md); every child store carries `vehicle_id`, and the read-time integrity pass enforces referential linkage. This issue adds no new store and no new field, but it MAY require [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) to add an index on `vehicle_id` for any store that lacks one, tracked as an open question below.
- Derived per-vehicle summaries are held in `socket.assigns` keyed by `vehicle_id` and `revision`. They are never persisted, never broadcast, and never survive socket termination.
- `meta.seq` is monotonic across all writes from all tabs and is the sole cross-tab consistency signal for this issue.

### Interfaces and events

- The hydration envelope from ADR-0004 §"Payload envelope" is unchanged; this issue adds no new top-level keys. Server-side changeset per `schema_version` ([`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md)) MUST include the cross-vehicle referential check described in FR-2.
- `local_store:put` payloads name the `vehicle_id`(s) they affect; the acknowledgment path is unchanged from [`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md).
- Internal domain events (`service_event_recorded`, `odometer_recorded`, `forecast_changed`) are LiveView-process-local and carry `vehicle_id` plus `revision`; they are not published to Phoenix.PubSub with cross-socket topics under this pivot, because there is no other socket that should see them.
- The garage overview read API on `AppShell` reads the per-vehicle summary from its own assigns; a reconciliation between summary and full dashboard is a same-process comparison at the same revision.

### Failure and recovery behavior

- A recompute failure for vehicle A on this LiveView is caught, logged without personal data (FR-15), and rendered as a per-vehicle error affordance on A's surface only; B's assigns and B's rendered DOM are unaffected.
- Stale async replies from a superseded revision are dropped, never rendered (FR-10).
- A `meta.seq` compare-and-set conflict aborts the write, drops the server's optimistic assign, re-hydrates, and renders the [`DOS-M09-003`](DOS-M09-003.md) reload notice (FR-4).
- A hydration referential-integrity failure quarantines the offending records ([`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) FR-12) and renders the quarantine surface from [`DOS-M09-003`](DOS-M09-003.md); the valid subset hydrates.
- Import failures roll back before any store is written ([`DOS-M09-002`](DOS-M09-002.md) import flow); a partially imported vehicle never enters assigns.

### Security and privacy

- INV-22 (hosted transport) binds every hydration and mutation over WSS; `check_origin` and CSRF/socket-token verification apply (ADR-0004 §"Security and privacy boundary").
- INV-3, INV-4, INV-23 forbid any per-vehicle server-side row, cache, log entry, or error-report payload; enforcement is the ADR-0004 §"Enforcement" test suite, which this issue's isolation scenarios extend.
- The garage overview MUST NOT include any field that could correlate one person across visits.
- Third-party error reporting remains prohibited (ADR-0004 §"No third-party anything") because a crash reporter that serialized assigns would exfiltrate exactly this issue's per-vehicle data.

### Accessibility and performance

- Per-vehicle URL transitions announce the newly rendered vehicle via a polite live region ([`DOS-M09-003`](DOS-M09-003.md) FR-21).
- "Updating"/revision-safe loading states are announced consistently with the [`DOS-M08-002`](DOS-M08-002.md) accessibility bar and never trap focus.
- Per-mount recompute for the currently rendered vehicle MUST complete within the (to-be-re-ratified) §9 hydration-plus-first-meaningful-paint budget; garage-overview derivation for the documented maximum active vehicle count from [`DOS-M08-001`](DOS-M08-001.md) MUST be linear in the number of vehicles and MUST NOT walk the full event history in a render path.

## UX and content states

- Per-vehicle dashboard: identical to the [`DOS-M06-004`](DOS-M06-004.md) semantics, always labeled with the owning vehicle identity from the [`DOS-M08-002`](DOS-M08-002.md) contract and always rendered from that vehicle's assigns.
- Garage overview: one summary row per active vehicle (nickname/identity, odometer as-of, estimated due date/cause, confidence, status), derived from assigns.
- "Updating"/revision-safe loading state during recompute, per-vehicle URL transition, and reconnect re-hydration; the prior result for A remains visible and labeled rather than swapped for B's data.
- Multi-tab conflict notice from [`DOS-M09-003`](DOS-M09-003.md) is reused here when a per-vehicle assign is discarded because of a `meta.seq` mismatch.
- Quarantine notice from [`DOS-M09-003`](DOS-M09-003.md) is rendered when a hydration payload includes cross-vehicle orphan records; the notice names the count and offers export.
- Export flow: all-garage or single-vehicle selection; import flow: explicit duplicate/replace choices (never silent overwrite).
- Established terminology from INV-21 is unchanged: "estimated due date" with confidence label, source attribution, unknown/unsupported states.

## Test plan

### Automated

- Isolation property/concurrency tests: parallel randomized mutations across at least 50 fixture vehicles hydrated into one LiveView compared against per-vehicle isolated computation (AC-1).
- Cross-vehicle orphan quarantine tests: a fuzzed corrupt-payload fixture set drives the hydration changeset and asserts each orphan is quarantined per [`DOS-M09-001`](DOS-M09-001.md) FR-12, with the quarantine surface rendered (AC-3).
- `meta.seq` compare-and-set race tests: two-tab simulations interleave writes and re-hydration and assert conflict detection, re-hydration, and no cross-vehicle data leak (AC-4).
- Revision-race tests: interleave recompute completion with per-vehicle URL transitions and reconnect re-hydration; assert stale replies are dropped and A's identity is never rendered under B's data (AC-6).
- Subscription/topic tests: assert no Phoenix.PubSub topic broadcasts vehicle-scoped assigns to any other socket (FR-5).
- Export/import fixtures: single-vehicle, all-garage, duplicate-`vehicle_id`, and cross-vehicle-reference cases (AC-5).
- Summary-versus-detail reconciliation test comparing overview summary and full dashboard from the same assigns at identical revisions (FR-7).
- Log-redaction scripted-session test extended with per-vehicle scenarios (AC-7).
- Repo-shape and namespace-shape release-gating tests (AC-8; ADR-0004 §"Enforcement").
- Garage-overview linear-complexity benchmark at the [`DOS-M08-001`](DOS-M08-001.md) maximum active count, asserting derivation does not walk full event history in a render path.

### Manual/browser matrix

Manual checks run on the [`DOS-M09-009`](DOS-M09-009.md) supported browser matrix (desktop Chrome/Edge/Firefox/Safari plus iOS Safari and Android Chrome).

- Per-vehicle URL rapid-switch capture during recompute, recorded as evidence showing no cross-vehicle rendering.
- Multi-tab race: open the app in two tabs on the same origin, mutate the same vehicle in each within one `meta.seq` window, and observe the conflict notice and re-hydration; then repeat with mutations on different vehicles and observe no conflict.
- LiveView reconnect race: force `phx-disconnected` (network toggle) during a recompute and observe the per-vehicle re-hydration path (FR-14).
- Manual export/import round trip in a real browser, including a duplicate-`vehicle_id` import and cross-vehicle-reference resolution.
- Time-zone-change trigger: change `prefs.time_zone` and observe per-vehicle recompute over the standard mutation round trip.

**Retired manual check (sweep finding 4).** The pre-pivot "background the app, mutate time/timezone on the device, foreground the app, and verify only affected vehicles recompute" ritual is retired: there is no backgrounded native app in this platform, and time-zone changes now route through `prefs.time_zone` and the standard mutation round trip (FR-8, FR-16). The browser-native foreground triggers under FR-8 (hydration, `pageshow`, visibility change, reconnect) replace it.

## Rollout and rollback

### Outcome (pre-pivot)

Every vehicle independently owns its maintenance basis, readings, service history, forecast version/confidence, due status, and next action; one vehicle's edit cannot change another's derived state.

### Why this matters and what it unblocks (pre-pivot)

Reusing singleton assumptions is the highest multi-vehicle correctness risk and could cause a user to service the wrong car on the wrong schedule. This issue is the correctness core of the multi-vehicle release: `DOS-M08-004` (per-vehicle notifications) and the multi-vehicle release itself cannot ship until derived state is provably vehicle-scoped and revision-safe.

### Functional requirements (pre-pivot)

- FR-1: Treat interval selection/source, service baseline, odometer segment/readings, declared usage, forecast snapshot, and due status as a vehicle-scoped aggregate. Pure forecast functions remain unchanged and receive one aggregate only.
- FR-2: Recompute/invalidation events carry vehicle ID and input revision.
- FR-3: A supervised keyed worker or serialized transaction prevents same-vehicle stale writes while allowing independent vehicles to compute concurrently.
- FR-4: PubSub/topic names and LiveView subscriptions must not broadcast private aggregate data globally; subscriptions are vehicle-scoped.
- FR-5: The garage overview may query one denormalized/derived summary per active vehicle: nickname/identity, observed odometer as-of, estimated due date/cause, confidence, status, and reminder state.
- FR-6: Update summaries transactionally or rebuildably; never calculate every full history in one render.
- FR-7: Unit preference is explicitly app-global or vehicle-scoped by product decision and migration. Whichever is chosen, canonical stored miles do not change.
- FR-8: Date/timezone for scheduling remains device-global while due targets are vehicle-specific.
- FR-9: Adding an oil change while vehicle A is active invalidates/schedules A only.
- FR-10: Switching tabs during recomputation shows A's prior result as "Updating" or a revision-safe loading state and never displays B's result under A.
- FR-11: Export can include all or one selected vehicle. Single-vehicle export includes referenced catalog/provenance metadata sufficient to import as a new entry.
- FR-12: Import duplicate-ID handling never overwrites another vehicle silently; the user receives explicit duplicate/replace choices.
- FR-13: Recompute triggers remain those of master prompt §9 (vehicle/plan/service/reading/unit/time-zone/catalog changes and app foreground), applied per affected vehicle only.

### Technical implementation contract (pre-pivot)

#### Components and ownership boundaries

- `DigitalOilSticker.Maintenance` owns the vehicle-scoped aggregate assembly; `DigitalOilSticker.Forecasting` stays a set of deterministic pure functions with unchanged signatures receiving one aggregate.
- A supervised keyed recompute worker (or serialized per-vehicle transaction) owned by the domain layer — not a perpetual background GenServer for deadlines (master prompt §7) — serializes same-vehicle recomputes and permits cross-vehicle concurrency.
- The garage overview summary is a domain-owned read model; LiveViews (`StickerLive`, history, garage overview from `DOS-M08-002`) render it without recomputing.
- Export/import ownership stays with the context that owned it in v1, extended for vehicle selection.

#### Data and persistence

- No changes to forecast math tables: `forecast_snapshots` keeps algorithm version, input hash, computation time, confidence code, and reason code per vehicle/cycle.
- Overview summary rows (or derived queries) are per active vehicle, updated transactionally with their inputs or rebuildable from source data; they are never the source of truth.
- Input revision is persisted or derivable so stale recompute results are detectable and discarded.
- If the unit-preference decision selects vehicle-scoped units, the change ships as an additive migration; canonical stored mileage (integer base units) is untouched either way.
- Single-vehicle export bundles referenced catalog/provenance metadata (configuration key, catalog version, source snapshots) sufficient for standalone import.

#### Interfaces and events

- Events `service_event_recorded`, `odometer_recorded`, `forecast_changed`, `reminders_reconciled` carry vehicle ID plus input revision; topics are vehicle-scoped.
- Aggregate read API: one call returns the complete vehicle aggregate at a revision; UI requests carry the expected revision so late replies are identifiable.
- Summary read API: garage overview reads the per-vehicle summary; a reconciliation query can compare summary versus full dashboard at the same revision.
- Import API returns explicit duplicate/replace decisions to the UI rather than resolving silently.

#### Failure and recovery behavior

- A crashed recompute worker for vehicle A does not affect other vehicles; supervision restarts it and the next event or foreground recompute converges.
- Stale async replies (from a superseded revision) are dropped, never rendered.
- A summary that cannot be updated transactionally is marked stale and rebuilt; it must never persist a value from a different revision than its inputs.
- Import failures roll back completely; a partially imported vehicle never appears in the garage.

#### Security and privacy

- Vehicle-scoped topics prevent one LiveView from receiving another vehicle's private aggregate data; no global broadcast of history, odometer, or notes.
- Export files remain local and user-initiated; they carry vehicle history and provenance, so the UI presents them as user data to handle carefully. Log redaction rules (VIN, mileage, notes, vehicle identifiers) apply to all new worker/event logging.

#### Accessibility and performance

- Garage summaries must meet query/performance budgets from M07 at 50 active vehicles; one summary query per vehicle, never full-history computation in a render path.
- "Updating"/loading states are announced accessibly (consistent with the `DOS-M08-002` accessibility bar) and never trap focus.

### UX and content states (pre-pivot)

- Per-vehicle dashboard: identical to v1 dashboard semantics, always labeled with the owning vehicle identity from the `DOS-M08-002` contract.
- Garage overview: per-vehicle summary rows (nickname/identity, odometer as-of, estimated due date/cause, confidence, status, reminder state).
- "Updating"/revision-safe loading state during recomputation and tab switches; the prior result remains visible and labeled rather than swapped for another vehicle's data.
- Export flow: all-garage or single-vehicle selection; import flow: explicit duplicate/replace choices.
- Established terminology is unchanged: "Estimated due date" with confidence label, source attribution, unknown/unsupported states (master prompt §6).
